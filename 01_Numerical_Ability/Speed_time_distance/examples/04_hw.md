### Given:

* First part = **30 km**
* Speed for first part = **6 km/h**
* Remaining distance = **40 km**
* Time taken for remaining 40 km = **5 hours**

### Step 1: Find time for first 30 km

Formula:

[
Time = \frac{Distance}{Speed}
]

[
= \frac{30}{6} = 5\text{ hours}
]

So:

**First 30 km → 5 hours**

**Remaining 40 km → 5 hours**

---

### Step 2: Find total distance

[
30+40=70\text{ km}
]

### Step 3: Find total time

[
5+5=10\text{ hours}
]

### Step 4: Average speed

Remember:

[
\boxed{\text{Average Speed}=\frac{\text{Total Distance}}{\text{Total Time}}}
]

So:

[
\frac{70}{10}=\boxed{7\text{ km/h}}
]

✅ **Answer: 7 km/h**

**Important:** Don't take the average of the two speeds. Average speed is always **total distance ÷ total time**.
