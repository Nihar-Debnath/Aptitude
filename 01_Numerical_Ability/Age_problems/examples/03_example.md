# Normal Method

Absolutely. This one is a little more interesting because **both statements are about the same two people at different points in time**.

Let's translate it carefully.

---

# Given

> **3 years ago**, father's age was **7 times** son's age.

And:

> **At present**, father's age is **5 times** son's age.

We need:

> **Present age of father + present age of son**

---

## Step 1: Use the present-age relationship

Let the **son's present age** be:

\[
x
\]

The question says the father's present age is **5 times** the son's age.

Therefore:

\[
\text{Father's present age}=5x
\]

So right now:

\[
\boxed{\text{Son}=x,\quad \text{Father}=5x}
\]

---

# Step 2: Go 3 years back

If the son is currently:

\[
x
\]

then 3 years ago:

\[
x-3
\]

Father is currently:

\[
5x
\]

so 3 years ago:

\[
5x-3
\]

The question says that **3 years ago, father was 7 times son's age**.

Therefore:

\[
5x-3=7(x-3)
\]

---

# Step 3: Solve the equation

Expand the right side:

\[
5x-3=7x-21
\]

Move terms:

\[
21-3=7x-5x
\]

\[
18=2x
\]

Therefore:

\[
x=9
\]

So the son's present age is:

\[
\boxed{9}
\]

Father's present age:

\[
5x=5(9)=\boxed{45}
\]

---

# Step 4: Find the sum

\[
45+9
\]

\[
\boxed{54}
\]

# 🎯 Answer: **54 years**

---

## Let's verify it — VERY important

Present:

* Son = **9**
* Father = **45**

Father is:

\[
45=5\times9
\]

Correct. ✅

Three years ago:

* Son = (9-3=6)
* Father = (45-3=42)

Father was:

\[
42=7\times6
\]

Correct. ✅

Therefore:

\[
\boxed{54}
\]

---

# 🧠 Notice the trick in this question

You don't actually need **two variables**.

Because the question directly tells us:

> Present father = 5 × present son

So immediately:

\[
\boxed{S=x,\quad F=5x}
\]

Then move both ages back 3 years:

\[
S_{\text{past}}=x-3
\]

\[
F_{\text{past}}=5x-3
\]

And use the other condition:

\[
\boxed{5x-3=7(x-3)}
\]

That's the fastest approach.

# Faster Method 


The formula on the board is:

\[
\boxed{\text{Present age of younger}
=

\frac{\text{Years}\times(\text{Past times}-1)}
{\text{Past times}-\text{Present times}}}
\]

Let's understand **why this works**, instead of blindly memorizing it.

---

# Your question

> 3 years ago, father was **7 times** son's age.
> Present father is **5 times** son's age.

We want the son's present age.

Identify:

* Years = **3**
* Past times = **7**
* Present times = **5**

Plug directly:

\[
\text{Son's age}
=

\frac{3(7-1)}{7-5}
\]

\[
=\frac{3\times6}{2}
\]

\[
=\boxed9
\]

Then father:

\[
9\times5=45
\]

So sum:

\[
45+9=\boxed{54}
\]
