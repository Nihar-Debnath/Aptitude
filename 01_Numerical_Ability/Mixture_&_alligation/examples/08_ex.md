Sure bro. This one is **not really an alligation question**. It's a simple **percentage + mixture** question.

### Step 1: Find the amount of alcohol

We have:

* Total mixture = **15 litres**
* Alcohol = **20%**

So alcohol is:

\[
20% \text{ of } 15
\]

\[
=\frac{20}{100}\times15
\]

\[
=\boxed{3\text{ litres}}
\]

So originally:

* **Alcohol = 3 L**
* **Water = 12 L**

---

### Step 2: Add 3 litres of water

We're adding **water**, not alcohol.

So the amount of alcohol **doesn't change**.

Still:

\[
\boxed{3L\text{ alcohol}}
\]

But total mixture becomes:

\[
15+3=18L
\]

---

### Step 3: Find the new percentage

\[
\text{Alcohol %}
=

\frac{\text{alcohol}}{\text{total mixture}}\times100
\]

\[
=\frac{3}{18}\times100
\]

\[
=16.666\ldots%
\]

Therefore:

\[
\boxed{16.67%}
\]

### ✅ Answer: **B) 16.67%**

---

### 🧠 The trick

Whenever **water is added**, the amount of alcohol stays the same, but the **total mixture increases**.

Here:

```text
Before:
15 L mixture
3 L alcohol
20%

        ↓ add 3 L water

After:
18 L mixture
3 L alcohol
16.67%
```

So don't apply alligation here. **Find the actual amount of alcohol first, then divide by the new total.**
