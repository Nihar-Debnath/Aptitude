[

]

programmer-minutes.

They produce 15 lines.

[
\frac{225}{15}=15

[
60\text{ programmers} \times 60\text{ minutes}

[
=3600\text{ programmer-minutes}

[
\frac{3600}{15}=240

\boxed{240\text{ lines}}

### More time

### More work
