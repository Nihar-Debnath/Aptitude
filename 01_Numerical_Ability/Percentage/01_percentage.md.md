In **Numerical Ability (Quantitative Aptitude)**, **Percentage** is one of the most important topics. It deals with expressing a number as a fraction of 100 and is the foundation for many other aptitude topics like Profit & Loss, Simple Interest, Compound Interest, Discount, Data Interpretation, and Ratio.

### Basic Formula

A percentage means "per hundred."

$$[ \text{Percentage} = \frac{\text{Part}}{\text{Whole}} \times 100  ]$$

### Example 1

You scored **45 marks out of 60**.

Percentage = (45 ÷ 60) × 100 = **75%**

### Example 2

What is **20% of 150**?

20% = 20/100

= (20/100) × 150

= **30**

### Example 3

A shirt's price increases from ₹500 to ₹600.

Percentage increase = ((600 − 500) ÷ 500) × 100

= (100 ÷ 500) × 100

= **20%**

## Common Types of Percentage Questions

1. **Find the percentage**
    
    - 18 out of 24 is what percent?
        
2. **Find the value**
    
    - What is 35% of 800?
        
3. **Find the original number**
    
    - 40% of a number is 120. Find the number.
        
4. **Percentage increase/decrease**
    
    - Salary increased from ₹25,000 to ₹28,000. Find the percentage increase.
        
5. **Successive percentage changes**
    
    - Price increased by 20% and then decreased by 10%. Find the overall change.
