# What does Percentage mean?

The word **Percent** literally means:

> **Per Hundred**

* "Per" = for every
* "Cent" = hundred

So,

* **1%** means **1 out of 100**
* **25%** means **25 out of 100**
* **80%** means **80 out of 100**

That's why every percentage can be written as a fraction with denominator **100**.

---

# 1. Percentage → Fraction

### Rule

**Step 1:** Remove the % sign.

**Step 2:** Put the number over 100.

**Step 3:** Simplify the fraction.

---

### Example 1

Convert **25%** into a fraction.

Remove %

25/100

Now simplify.

Divide both by 25.

25 ÷ 25 = 1

100 ÷ 25 = 4

Answer:

**25% = 1/4**

---

### Example 2

Convert **50%**

50/100

Divide by 50.

= **1/2**

---

### Example 3

Convert **75%**

75/100

Divide by 25.

= **3/4**

---

### Example 4

Convert **20%**

20/100

Divide by 20.

= **1/5**

---

### Example 5

Convert **12.5%**

12.5/100

Multiply numerator and denominator by 10 to remove the decimal.

125/1000

Divide by 125.

= **1/8**

---

### Example 6

Convert **40%**

40/100

Divide by 20.

= **2/5**

# 2. Fraction → Percentage

This is simply the reverse process.

### Rule

Multiply the fraction by **100** and add the **%** sign.

[
\text{Percentage}=\frac{\text{Fraction}}{1}\times100
]

---

### Example 1

Convert **1/2**

(1/2) × 100

= **50%**

---

### Example 2

Convert **3/4**

(3/4) × 100

= **75%**

---

### Example 3

Convert **2/5**

(2/5) × 100

= 40%

---

### Example 4

Convert **1/8**

(1/8) × 100

= 12.5%

---

### Example 5

Convert **7/10**

(7/10) × 100

= 70%

---

### Example 6

Convert **5/20**

First simplify.

5/20 = 1/4

Now,

1/4 × 100

= **25%**
