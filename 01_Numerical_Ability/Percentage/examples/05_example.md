😂 This one is actually **just a percentage problem wearing unnecessary English clothing**.

Let's translate it into normal human language.

### What are they saying?

You currently have:

> **237 points out of 300**

Then there's a final test worth:

> **100 points**

Your **total possible points** after the final test will be:

\[
300+100=400
\]

You need **at least 80% overall**.

So the question is:

> **How many points do I need on the final 100-point test so that my total becomes at least 80% of 400?**

---

## Step 1: Find 80% of total points

Total = 400

\[
80% \text{ of }400
\]

\[
=\frac{80}{100}\times400
\]

\[
=\boxed{320}
\]

So you need **at least 320 total points**.

---

## Step 2: You already have 237

You need:

\[
320-237
\]

\[
=\boxed{83}
\]

Therefore:

# 🎯 Answer = **83 marks**

Check:

\[
237+83=320
\]

and:

\[
\frac{320}{400}\times100=80%
\]

So you need **at least 83/100 on the final test**.

---

### 🧠 The shortcut you should recognize

Whenever you see:

> "Already scored X out of A, final exam is B, need at least P% overall. Find minimum final score."

Do:

\[
\boxed{\text{Required final score}
=(A+B)\times\frac{P}{100}-X}
\]

Here:

\[
=(300+100)\times0.8-237
\]

\[
=320-237
\]

\[
=\boxed{83}
\]

That's it. **This is basically a weighted/overall percentage question**, not some weird new concept. 😭
