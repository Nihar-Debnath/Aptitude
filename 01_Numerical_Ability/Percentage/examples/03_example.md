> 220% of a number X is 44. What is 44% of X.




Absolutely! In fact, **the unitary method is one of the easiest ways** to solve this.

Let's do it.

---

## Question

> **220% of X = 44**
>
> Find **44% of X**.

### Step 1: Find 1%

If **220% = 44**, then

```text
1% = 44 / 220
   = 0.2
```

---

### Step 2: Find 44%

```text
44% = 44 × 0.2
    = 8.8
```

✅ **Answer = 8.8**

---

## Why did I use the ratio method then?

Because it's **faster** in aptitude exams.

### Unitary Method

```text
220% = 44

1% = 44/220

44% = 44 × (44/220)
     = 8.8
```

### Ratio Method

```text
220% → 44

44% is 1/5 of 220%

So,

44 ÷ 5 = 8.8
```

No need to calculate **1%**.

---

## Which method should you use?

| Method                    | Good for                  | Speed |
| ------------------------- | ------------------------- | ----- |
| **Unitary Method**        | Understanding the concept | ⭐⭐⭐   |
| **Ratio/Shortcut Method** | Aptitude exams            | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ |

If you're just learning percentages, I recommend using the **unitary method first**. Once you're comfortable, start spotting shortcuts like:

* 20% is half of 40%
* 15% is half of 30%
* 44% is one-fifth of 220%
* 12% is one-third of 36%

These shortcuts save a lot of time in competitive exams.
