# What is Arithmetic?

Arithmetic is the branch of mathematics that deals with **numbers and everyday calculations**.

It answers questions like:

* If a shirt costs ₹1000 and has a 20% discount, what is the final price?
* If a train travels at 60 km/h for 5 hours, how far does it go?
* If two people together finish a job in 6 days, how long will one person take?
* If ₹5000 is invested at 10% interest, how much interest is earned?

These are all arithmetic problems.

Unlike Number System (which focuses on properties of numbers), Arithmetic focuses on **real-life applications** of numbers.

# Difference Between Number System and Arithmetic

| Number System   | Arithmetic                               |
| --------------- | ---------------------------------------- |
| Studies numbers | Uses numbers to solve real-life problems |
| Prime numbers   | Profit & Loss                            |
| Divisibility    | Time & Work                              |
| HCF & LCM       | Speed, Distance & Time                   |
| Remainders      | Interest                                 |
| Unit digits     | Percentages                              |

Example:

### Number System

Is **567** divisible by 9?

You check the digit sum.

---

### Arithmetic

A laptop costs ₹50,000. A 10% discount is offered. What is the selling price?

You calculate the discount.

# Complete Arithmetic Syllabus

There are about **15–20 topics**, and almost every placement exam uses some of them.

## 1. Percentages ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

The most important topic.

Learn:

* Percentage to fraction
* Fraction to percentage
* Increase %
* Decrease %
* Successive percentage
* Percentage change

Example

A salary increases from ₹20,000 to ₹25,000.

Increase

```text
5000
```

Percentage increase

```text
5000/20000 ×100

=25%
```

# Prerequisite Chain (Very Important)

Arithmetic topics are connected. Learn them in this order:

```text
Fractions
      ↓
Decimals
      ↓
Percentages
      ↓
Ratio & Proportion
      ↓
Average
      ↓
Profit & Loss
      ↓
Simple & Compound Interest
      ↓
Time & Work
      ↓
Speed, Time & Distance
      ↓
Boats & Streams
      ↓
Pipes & Cisterns
```

Notice something?

Many topics depend on **Percentages** and **Ratios**. If you master those two, you'll find many later chapters much easier.

# Recommended Order for Placement Preparation

Here's the order I'd suggest:

1. Fractions & Decimals (if not already comfortable)
2. Percentages
3. Ratio & Proportion
4. Average
5. Profit & Loss
6. Simple Interest
7. Compound Interest
8. Time & Work
9. Pipes & Cisterns
10. Time, Speed & Distance
11. Boats & Streams
12. Ages
13. Mixture & Alligation
14. Problems on Numbers
15. Calendar & Clock (last)

This sequence builds each topic on top of the previous one, making the learning process much smoother. Since you've already finished Number System, **Percentages** is the best next topic to start with because it supports many of the chapters that follow.
