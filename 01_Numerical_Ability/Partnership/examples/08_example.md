Yep! This is another **Compound Partnership** question because **C joins after 6 months**, so the partners have different investment times.

Let's solve it carefully.

## Given

A and B start together.

Their investments are in the ratio:

\[
A:B=3:5
\]

C joins **after 6 months** with an amount **equal to B's investment**.

Business duration = **1 year = 12 months**

We need:

\[
A:B:C
\]

---

# Step 1: Assume actual investments

A and B are in the ratio:

\[
3:5
\]

So we can simply take:

* A = **₹3**
* B = **₹5**

C's investment is equal to B's.

Therefore:

* C = **₹5**

So:

\[
A:B:C=3:5:5
\]

**But we cannot use this directly as the profit ratio**, because C joins later.

---

# Step 2: Find the time each person invests

The business runs for **12 months**.

### A

A starts from the beginning:

\[
\boxed{12\text{ months}}
\]

### B

B also starts from the beginning:

\[
\boxed{12\text{ months}}
\]

### C

C joins after 6 months:

\[
12-6=6
\]

So C invests for:

\[
\boxed{6\text{ months}}
\]

---

# Step 3: Investment × Time

Now use:

\[
\boxed{\text{Profit Ratio}=\text{Investment}\times\text{Time}}
\]

### A

\[
3\times12=36
\]

### B

\[
5\times12=60
\]

### C

\[
5\times6=30
\]

Therefore:

\[
A:B:C=36:60:30
\]

---

# Step 4: Simplify

All three are divisible by 6:

\[
36\div6=6
\]

\[
60\div6=10
\]

\[
30\div6=5
\]

Therefore:

\[
\boxed{6:10:5}
\]

# ✅ Final Answer

\[
\boxed{A:B:C=6:10:5}
\]

So the correct option is **C. 6 : 10 : 5** ✅

---

## 🧠 Notice something important

C's investment is **the same as B's**:

B → ₹5
C → ₹5

But B gets more profit because:

* B's money stayed for **12 months**
* C's money stayed for only **6 months**

So:

\[
B=5\times12=60
\]

\[
C=5\times6=30
\]

B's contribution is **twice C's**.

That's why their profit shares aren't equal.

### Your pattern now:

Whenever someone **joins later**:

**1. Find investment**

**2. Find actual time**

**3. Investment × Time**

**4. Make ratio**

For this one:

\[
3\times12:5\times12:5\times6
\]

\[
36:60:30
\]

\[
\boxed{6:10:5}
\]
