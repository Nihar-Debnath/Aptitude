Yes! This question introduces **one new concept**: A is a **working member**, so he gets **5% of the total profit first**. Then the remaining profit is divided normally.

Let's do it step by step.

---

# Given

* A invests ₹6,500 for 6 months
* B invests ₹8,400 for 5 months
* C invests ₹10,000 for 3 months
* Total profit = ₹7,400
* A gets **5% of profit** for being the working member
* Find **B's share**

---

# Step 1: Give A his 5% first

Total profit:

\[
₹7,400
\]

A gets 5%:

\[
5% \text{ of } 7400
\]

\[
=\frac{5}{100}\times7400
\]

\[
=\boxed{₹370}
\]

So A gets **₹370 separately**.

---

# Step 2: Find the profit left for everyone

Original profit:

\[
₹7,400
\]

A's working-member share:

\[
₹370
\]

Remaining profit:

\[
7400-370
\]

\[
=\boxed{₹7,030}
\]

Now **₹7,030** will be divided among A, B and C according to their investment × time.

---

# Step 3: Find Investment × Time

Remember our rule:

\[
\boxed{\text{Profit Ratio}=\text{Investment}\times\text{Time}}
\]

### A

\[
6500\times6=39000
\]

### B

\[
8400\times5=42000
\]

### C

\[
10000\times3=30000
\]

Therefore:

\[
A:B:C=39000:42000:30000
\]

Remove three zeros:

\[
39:42:30
\]

Divide by 3:

\[
\boxed{13:14:10}
\]

---

# Step 4: Find B's share

The ratio is:

\[
13:14:10
\]

Total parts:

\[
13+14+10=37
\]

So B has **14 parts out of 37**.

Remaining profit:

\[
₹7,030
\]

Therefore:

\[
B=\frac{14}{37}\times7030
\]

Now:

\[
7030\div37=190
\]

So:

\[
190\times14
\]

\[
=\boxed{₹2,660}
\]

# 🎯 Final Answer

\[
\boxed{\text{B's share = ₹2,660}}
\]

So the correct option is **B. ₹2660** ✅

---

# 🧠 The NEW concept to remember

When a question says something like:

> "A is a working partner and receives 5% of the profit"

Do **NOT** include that 5% directly in the investment ratio.

Instead:

### ① Calculate working partner's percentage first

\[
5%\text{ of total profit}
\]

### ② Subtract it

\[
\text{Remaining Profit}
=

\text{Total Profit}-\text{Working Partner's Share}
\]

### ③ Divide the remaining profit using:

\[
\boxed{\text{Investment}\times\text{Time}}
\]

For this question:

![alt text](./images/image-3.png)

\[
7400-370=7030
\]

Then:

\[
6500\times6:8400\times5:10000\times3
\]

\[
=13:14:10
\]

Then B:

\[
\frac{14}{37}\times7030
=\boxed{2660}
\]

**That's the entire question.**
