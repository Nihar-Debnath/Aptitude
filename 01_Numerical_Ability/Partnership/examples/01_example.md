Yes! This is a **Compound Partnership** question because **both the investment and the time are different**.

Let's solve it very slowly.

## Given

* **Balu:** ₹35,000 for **8 months**
* **Somu:** ₹42,000 for **10 months**
* Total profit = **₹31,570**
* Find **Balu's share**

---

# Step 1: Identify the type

Look at the time:

* Balu → 8 months
* Somu → 10 months

Time is **different**.

Therefore, this is **Compound Partnership**.

So we use:

\[
\boxed{\text{Profit Ratio} = \text{Investment} \times \text{Time}}
\]

---

# Step 2: Find Balu's contribution

Balu:

\[
35000\times8
\]

\[
=280000
\]

So Balu's contribution is **2,80,000**.

---

# Step 3: Find Somu's contribution

Somu:

\[
42000\times10
\]

\[
=420000
\]

So Somu's contribution is **4,20,000**.

---

# Step 4: Make the ratio

Therefore:

\[
Balu:Somu=280000:420000
\]

Cancel the zeros:

\[
280:420
\]

Divide both by 140:

\[
\boxed{2:3}
\]

So the **profit ratio is 2 : 3**.

---

# Step 5: Divide the total profit

Total profit:

\[
₹31,570
\]

Profit ratio:

\[
2:3
\]

Total parts:

\[
2+3=5
\]

So one part is:

\[
31570\div5=6314
\]

Balu gets **2 parts**:

\[
6314\times2
\]

\[
=\boxed{₹12,628}
\]

# ✅ Final Answer

\[
\boxed{\text{Balu's share = ₹12,628}}
\]

---

## 🧠 The method you should use for EVERY question like this

Whenever you see:

> A invests ₹X for Y months
> B invests ₹Z for W months

Do this:

### ① Investment × Time

**A:**

\[
X\times Y
\]

**B:**

\[
Z\times W
\]

### ② Make the ratio

\[
A:B=(X\times Y):(Z\times W)
\]

### ③ Divide the total profit according to that ratio

---

### For this question:

| Partner | Investment |      Time | Investment × Time |
| ------- | ---------: | --------: | ----------------: |
| Balu    |    ₹35,000 |  8 months |         ₹2,80,000 |
| Somu    |    ₹42,000 | 10 months |         ₹4,20,000 |

Therefore:

\[
\boxed{Balu:Somu=2:3}
\]

And because Balu has **2 out of the total 5 parts**:

\[
\boxed{Balu=\frac{2}{5}\times31,570=₹12,628}
\]

**The most important thing:** Don't directly compare ₹35,000 and ₹42,000. Since their times are different, first do **Investment × Time**.
