> In how many ways a team of 11 must be selected a team 5 men and 11 women such that the team must comprise of not more than 3 men.


Yes! This is a **Permutation & Combination (P&C)** question, specifically a **selection/combination with a restriction**.

The key phrase is:

> **"not more than 3 men"**

That means **men ≤ 3**.

---

## 1. First understand the question

You have:

* **5 men**
* **11 women**
* Need to select a **team of 11**
* Team must have **not more than 3 men**

So possible numbers of men are:

* 0 men
* 1 man
* 2 men
* 3 men

We **cannot** select 4 or 5 men.

---

## 2. For each case, select men + women

Remember:

**Combination = choosing/selecting**

Formula:

\[
^nC_r = \frac{n!}{r!(n-r)!}
\]

### Case 1: 0 men

If we select 0 men, we need all 11 women:

\[
^5C_0 \times ^{11}C_{11}
\]

---

### Case 2: 1 man

Select 1 from 5 men.

Then select remaining 10 from 11 women:

\[
^5C_1 \times ^{11}C_{10}
\]

---

### Case 3: 2 men

Select 2 from 5 men.

Then select remaining 9 women from 11:

\[
^5C_2 \times ^{11}C_9
\]

---

### Case 4: 3 men

Select 3 from 5 men.

Then select remaining 8 women from 11:

\[
^5C_3 \times ^{11}C_8
\]

---

## 3. Add all the cases

Therefore:

\[
^5C_0 ^{11}C_{11}
+
^5C_1 ^{11}C_{10}
+
^5C_2 ^{11}C_9
+
^5C_3 ^{11}C_8
\]

Calculate:

\[
1(1)+5(11)+10(55)+10(165)
\]

\[
=1+55+550+1650
\]

\[
\boxed{2256}
\]

### ✅ Answer: **2256 ways**

---

# 🧠 The pattern you should remember

Whenever you see:

> **"Select a team of X people from men and women such that..."**

Think:

### **Separate the cases according to the number of men/women.**

For example:

**"11-member team, not more than 3 men"**

means:

```text
0 men + 11 women
1 man + 10 women
2 men + 9 women
3 men + 8 women
```

Then:

```text
C(5,0) × C(11,11)
+
C(5,1) × C(11,10)
+
C(5,2) × C(11,9)
+
C(5,3) × C(11,8)
```

---

## ⭐ One important shortcut

If the question says:

### **"Not more than 3 men"**

Immediately translate it to:

> **Men ≤ 3**

If it says:

### **"At least 3 men"**

Translate:

> **Men ≥ 3**

If it says:

### **"Exactly 3 men"**

Translate:

> **Men = 3**

If it says:

### **"At most 3 men"**

Same as:

> **Men ≤ 3**

If it says:

### **"At least 3 women"**

Translate:

> **Women ≥ 3**

This translation is often the **most important part of these P&C questions**. Once you know the allowed cases, the calculation is usually straightforward.
