# Question

> The mean of a set of data is **5**.
>
> What will be the mean if **10 is subtracted from each data value?**

Options:

* A. 5
* B. 10
* C. -10
* D. -5

---

# Method 1: Understand the Concept (Best Method)

Suppose the data is:

```text
4, 5, 6
```

Mean =

```text
(4+5+6)/3

=15/3

=5
```

Now subtract **10** from every number.

```text
-6, -5, -4
```

New Mean =

```text
(-6-5-4)/3

=-15/3

=-5
```

✅ Answer = **-5**

---

# Method 2: Shortcut Rule ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

Remember this rule forever:

| Operation on every value | Effect on Mean          |
| ------------------------ | ----------------------- |
| Add k                    | Mean increases by k     |
| Subtract k               | Mean decreases by k     |
| Multiply by k            | Mean is multiplied by k |
| Divide by k              | Mean is divided by k    |

Here,

```text
Original Mean = 5

Subtract 10 from every value
```

So,

```text
New Mean

=5−10

=-5
```

✅ **Answer = D (-5)**

---

# Why does this happen?

Suppose there are **n** numbers.

Original Mean:

```text
Mean = Total / n
```

If you subtract **10** from **every number**,

then the total decreases by:

```text
10 × n
```

New Mean:

```text
=(Total−10n)/n

=Total/n−10

=Mean−10
```

That's why the mean also decreases by **10**.

# Ultimate Cheat Sheet

Memorize these four rules:

```text
Every value + k
        ↓
Mean + k

Every value − k
        ↓
Mean − k

Every value × k
        ↓
Mean × k

Every value ÷ k
        ↓
Mean ÷ k
```

These transformation questions appear very frequently in placement aptitude exams, and using these rules lets you solve them in a few seconds without doing any calculations.
