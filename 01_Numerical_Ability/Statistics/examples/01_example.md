# Q2

Jimmy's dog has 6 puppies. He weighed the puppies right after they were born.

Their weights were:

657, 789, 456, 554, 635, 446

Find the **mean (average)** weight.

Options:

a) 589.5  
b) 707.4  
c) 353.5  
d) 632.9

---

# Fast Option Elimination Trick

We know,

Mean = Total / n

Here,

n = 6

So,

Mean × 6 = Total

Since all the given weights are **integers**,

**Total must also be an integer.**

Therefore,

Mean × 6 **must be an integer.**

---

## Step 1: Eliminate impossible option

The smallest weight is **446 g**.

The mean **cannot be less than the smallest value** when there are values larger than it.

So,

❌ 353.5 is eliminated.

Remaining options:

- 589.5
- 707.4
- 632.9

---

## Step 2: Check only the decimal part

Since

Mean × 6 = Integer,

only multiply the **decimal part** by 6.

### Option A

0.5 × 6 = **3.0** ✅ (Integer)

Possible

---

### Option B

0.4 × 6 = **2.4** ❌ (Not an integer)

Reject

---

### Option D

0.9 × 6 = **5.4** ❌ (Not an integer)

Reject

---

Only one option remains.

✅ **Answer = 589.5 grams**

---

# Why does this trick work?

Since,

Mean × Number of Observations = Total

and the **Total is always an integer** (because all observations are integers),

the decimal part of

**Mean × Number of Observations**

must become **.0** (a whole number).

If it doesn't become a whole number, that option is impossible.

This trick is very useful in aptitude exams whenever:

- All given numbers are integers.
- The number of observations is known.
- Options contain decimal values.
