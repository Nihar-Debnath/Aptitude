Yes! This question is **perfect for the tricks you've just learned**. 🔥 Let's do it step by step.

### Question

> ₹1,25,000 at **12% p.a.**, compounded **half-yearly**, for **1½ years**. Find CI.

Given:

\[
P=₹1,25,000
\]

\[
R=12%
\]

\[
T=1.5\text{ years}
\]

Compounding = **half-yearly**

---

# Step 1: Convert the rate

Half-yearly means **2 times per year**.

So divide the annual rate by 2:

\[
12\div2
\]
\[
=6
\]

Therefore, **each 6-month period has 6% interest**.

---

# Step 2: Convert the time

1½ years means:

\[
1.5\times2=3
\]

So there are:

\[
\boxed{3\text{ half-year periods}}
\]

Therefore our question has effectively become:

> **₹1,25,000 at 6% for 3 periods.**

---

# Method 1 — Normal CI formula

\[
A=P\left(1+\frac{R}{100}\right)^n
\]

Here:

\[
A=125000(1.06)^3
\]

\[
A=125000\times1.191016
\]

\[
A=148877
\]

Therefore:

\[
CI=A-P
\]

\[
CI=148877-125000
\]

\[
\boxed{CI=₹23,877}
\]

---

# Method 2 — Your **3 : 3 : 1 trick** 🔥

We have **3 periods**, so:

\[
\boxed{3:3:1}
\]

Our rate is **6%**.

Now calculate 6% at each level.

### Level 1 — 6% of ₹1,25,000

\[
10%=12500
\]

Since:

\[
6%=10%-4%
\]

But easier:

\[
125000\times6%=7500
\]

So:

\[
\boxed{7500}
\]

Now multiply by the first coefficient **3**:

\[
7500\times3=22500
\]

---

### Level 2

Now find 6% of ₹7,500:

\[
7500\times6%=450
\]

Multiply by coefficient **3**:

\[
450\times3=1350
\]

---

### Level 3

Find 6% of ₹450:

\[
450\times6%=27
\]

Multiply by coefficient **1**:

\[
27\times1=27
\]

---

### Add them:

\[
CI=22500+1350+27
\]

\[
\boxed{CI=₹23,877}
\]

---
