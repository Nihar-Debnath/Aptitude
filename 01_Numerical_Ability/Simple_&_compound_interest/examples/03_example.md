Ah, **this is an important point**. 👍

When a question gives time in **days**, you need to know whether to use **365 days or 366 days** for 1 year.

### But in THIS question:

> ₹2500 at 5% per annum for **219 days**

There is **no year/date mentioned**, so you should **not assume a leap year**.

For standard aptitude questions, unless the question explicitly says **leap year** or gives dates indicating a leap year, use:

\[
\boxed{1\text{ year}=365\text{ days}}
\]

---

## Now solve it

Given:

\[
P=2500,\quad R=5%,\quad T=219\text{ days}
\]

Convert days to years:

\[
T=\frac{219}{365}
\]

Now use your shortcut:

\[
SI=(RT)%\text{ of }P
\]

So:

\[
RT=5\times\frac{219}{365}
\]

Notice:

\[
\frac{219}{365}=\frac35
\]

Therefore:

\[
RT=5\times\frac35=3%
\]

So the overall interest is **3% of ₹2500**.

\[
SI=3%\text{ of }2500
\]

\[
=\frac3{100}\times2500
\]

\[
\boxed{SI=₹75}
\]

### Final answer:

\[
\boxed{₹75}
\]

---

### Remember this rule

| Time given         | Use                        |
| ------------------ | -------------------------- |
| Years              | (T) directly               |
| Months             | (\frac{\text{months}}{12}) |
| Days               | (\frac{\text{days}}{365})  |
| Explicit leap year | (\frac{\text{days}}{366})  |

**So don't randomly assume leap year.** If the question doesn't specify it, **365 days is the standard assumption**.

YES 😂 — **now I see exactly what trick you're talking about.** This is even faster because you're thinking in terms of **percentage per chunks of days**, rather than converting 219 days into years.

The teacher is doing this:

### Given

* Rate = **5% per year**
* 1 year = **365 days**
* Therefore, split 365 days into **5 equal parts**:

[
365\div5=73\text{ days}
]

So:

[
\boxed{73\text{ days}=1%}
]

Because **365 days → 5%**, therefore:

* 73 days → 1%
* 146 days → 2%
* 219 days → 3%
* 292 days → 4%
* 365 days → 5%

And our question has:

[
219\text{ days}
]

Since:

[
219=73\times3
]

we immediately know:

[
\boxed{219\text{ days}=3%}
]

---

### Now calculate SI

Principal = ₹2500

So:

[
SI=3%\text{ of }2500
]

[
=\frac{3}{100}\times2500
]

[
\boxed{SI=₹75}
]

### 🔥 The shortcut to remember

If the annual rate is **5%**:

[
365\div5=73
]

So **every 73 days = 1% interest**.

Therefore:

[
219\div73=3%
]

Then:

[
3%\text{ of }2500=₹75
]

**This is the fastest method when the numbers divide nicely.**

And notice something important: **we didn't need to write (219/365) at all.** That's exactly the trick shown in your image.
