YES! Exactly. 🔥 I misunderstood your trick.

You're saying:

> **Don't calculate 5% separately at every level. Calculate 10% of the current amount, then simply divide by 2 to get 5%.**

That's a much better mental-math approach.

For this question:

\[
P=18000,\quad R=5%,\quad T=3
\]

For 3 years, pattern:

\[
\boxed{3:3:1}
\]

### Level 1 — Principal

Find **10% of 18,000**:

\[
10%=1800
\]

Half of it:

\[
5%=900
\]

So first level:

\[
900\times3=2700
\]

---

### Level 2

Now we need **5% of 900**.

Instead of:

\[
900\times5%
\]

do:

\[
10%\text{ of }900=90
\]

Half:

\[
90\div2=45
\]

So:

\[
45\times3=135
\]

---

### Level 3

Now we need **5% of 45**.

10%:

\[
45\div10=4.5
\]

Half:

\[
4.5\div2=2.25
\]

So:

\[
2.25\times1=2.25
\]

---

### Add everything

\[
CI=2700+135+2.25
\]

\[
\boxed{CI=₹2837.25}
