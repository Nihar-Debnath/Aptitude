Yes! Let's solve it using the **normal PRT method** — no confusing shortcuts. 👍

### Question

Principal = ₹3200
Rate = 5% per annum
Time = **4 April to 16 June**

We use:

\[
SI=\frac{P\times R\times T}{100}
\]

But because the time is given in **days**, first find the number of days.

### Step 1: Find number of days

From **4 April → 30 April**

\[
30-4=26\text{ days}
\]

May:

\[
31\text{ days}
\]

June:

\[
16\text{ days}
\]

So,

\[
T=26+31+16=\boxed{73\text{ days}}
\]

### Step 2: Convert days into years

Since 1 year = 365 days:

\[
T=\frac{73}{365}\text{ years}
\]

### Step 3: Apply PRT formula

\[
SI=\frac{3200\times5\times\frac{73}{365}}{100}
\]

Now simplify:

\[
\frac{3200\times5\times73}{36500}
\]

Since:

\[
\frac{73}{365}=\frac15
\]

Therefore:

\[
SI=\frac{3200\times5}{100}\times\frac15
\]

\[
=160\times\frac15
\]

\[
=\boxed{₹32}
\]

## ✅ Answer: ₹32

**The important thing:**
For these date-based SI questions, just remember:

**P → R → T**

\[
\boxed{SI=\frac{P\times R\times T}{100}}
\]

and when T is in days:

\[
\boxed{T=\frac{\text{number of days}}{365}}
\]
