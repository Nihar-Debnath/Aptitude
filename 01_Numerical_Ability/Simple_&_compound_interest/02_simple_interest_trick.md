### Example

Suppose:

* Principal = ₹20,000
* Rate = 8% per year
* Time = 3 years

Instead of immediately doing:

\[
SI=\frac{20000\times8\times3}{100}
\]

First calculate:

\[
RT=8\times3=24
\]

So the **overall interest = 24% of P**.

Now:

\[
SI=24%\text{ of }20000
\]

\[
=\frac{24}{100}\times20000
\]

\[
\boxed{₹4,800}
\]

That's the trick.
