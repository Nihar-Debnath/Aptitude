**Numeric Ability** (also called **Numerical Ability** or **Quantitative Aptitude**) is the section in placement exams and competitive exams that tests your ability to solve math problems quickly and accurately.

For companies like **TCS, Wipro, Cognizant, Capgemini, Accenture**, and many others, this section is very common.

## Example Questions

**Percentage**

> A shirt costs ₹800. A 15% discount is offered. What is the selling price?

**Time & Work**

> A can complete a job in 10 days and B in 15 days. How many days will they take together?

**Average**

> The average of five numbers is 24. What is their sum?

**Profit & Loss**

> A shopkeeper buys an item for ₹500 and sells it for ₹650. Find the profit percentage.

**Data Interpretation**

> A table shows the sales of five products. Which product had the highest sales?

## How to Prepare

1. Learn the formulas and concepts.
2. Practice basic questions topic by topic.
3. Solve timed aptitude questions.
4. Take mock tests regularly.
5. Improve calculation speed (tables, squares, cubes, percentages).

Since you're preparing for entry-level IT placements, I'd recommend mastering **Arithmetic first**, because it makes up the largest portion of most placement exams.

**Suggested order:**

1. Percentages
2. Ratio & Proportion
3. Average
4. Profit & Loss
5. Simple & Compound Interest
6. Time & Work
7. Time, Speed & Distance
8. Mixture & Alligation
9. Number System
10. Data Interpretation
11. Probability
12. Permutation & Combination

This order builds your fundamentals and matches what commonly appears in placement tests.
