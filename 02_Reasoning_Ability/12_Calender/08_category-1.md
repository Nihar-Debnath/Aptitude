Here:

**1 March 2011 → Monday**

Find:

**31 March 2011 → ?**

### Your method:

**1. Find the difference between dates**

\[
31-1=30\text{ days}
\]

**2. Find odd days**

\[
30\div7=4\text{ remainder }2
\]

So:

\[
\boxed{2\text{ odd days}}
\]

**3. Since we're going from past → future, ADD the odd days to the known weekday.**

Known:

\[
1\ March = Monday
\]

Move 2 days forward:

\[
Monday\rightarrow Tuesday\rightarrow Wednesday
\]

Therefore:

\[
\boxed{31\ March\ 2011=\text{Wednesday}}
\]

## 🧠 Category 1 rule

When:

> **Month and Year are SAME, Date is DIFFERENT**

Just:

\[
\boxed{\text{Date difference}\rightarrow\text{odd days}\rightarrow\text{move from known day}}
\]

### And YES:

**Past → Future = ADD**

Example:

\[
1 March\rightarrow31 March
\]

Monday + 2 = Wednesday.

---

### One tiny correction

Don't think **"always + it"**.

Think:

> **If target date is AFTER the given date → move forward.**

If the question were reversed:

**31 March 2011 = Wednesday**
Find **1 March 2011**

Then target is **BEFORE**, so you'd move **backward 2 days**:

Wednesday → Tuesday → Monday.

So:

\[
\boxed{\text{Future = forward, Past = backward}}
\]

That's the entire **Category 1** method.

# Reverse (Future to Past)

![alt text](./images/image-6.png)

Exactly. If the question gives you the **future date first** and asks for a **past date**, you simply go **backward**.

### Example

Suppose:

**31 March 2011 → Wednesday**

Find:

**1 March 2011 → ?**

### Step 1: Difference

\[
31-1=30
\]

### Step 2: Odd days

\[
30\div7=4\text{ remainder }2
\]

So:

\[
2\text{ odd days}
\]

### Step 3: Go backward

We know:

**31 March = Wednesday**

Go **2 days backward**:

\[
Wednesday\rightarrow Tuesday\rightarrow Monday
\]

Therefore:

\[
\boxed{1 March 2011=\text{Monday}}
\]

### 🔥 Rule to remember

| Direction         | What to do                 |
| ----------------- | -------------------------- |
| **Past → Future** | ➕ Add / move forward       |
| **Future → Past** | ➖ Subtract / move backward |

So your Category 1 formula is:

\[
\boxed{\text{Date difference → odd days → move in the correct direction}}
\]

**Don't blindly add. First check which date is earlier and which is later.**
