Yes! We can solve this **exactly using your Category-4 method**, and **we don't need the year at all** because both birthdays are in the **same year**.

Given:

**18 June = Monday**

Find:

**12 October = ?**

### Step 1 — Make the DATE same

We have:

**18 June → 12 June**

Go **backward 6 days**:

Monday − 6 days = **Tuesday**

So:

**12 June = Tuesday**

---

### Step 2 — Make the MONTH same

Now:

**12 June → 12 October**

Count month-to-month:

* June → July = **30 days**
* July → August = **31 days**
* August → September = **31 days**
* September → October = **30 days**

Total:

**30 + 31 + 31 + 30 = 122 days**

122 ÷ 7 → remainder **3**

So move **3 days forward**:

**Tuesday → Wednesday → Thursday → Friday**

## ✅ Answer: **Friday — Option B**

### Your Category-4 method

**18 June (Monday)**
↓ **Make date same**
**12 June (Tuesday)**
↓ **Make month same**
**12 October (Friday)**

**No year calculation needed.** 🔥

Yes! You **can absolutely do 18 October first**. In fact, that is a nice way to see the same logic.

Given:

**18 June = Monday**

### Step 1 — Make the MONTH same first

Go from:

**18 June → 18 October**

Count:

* June → July = 30
* July → August = 31
* August → September = 31
* September → October = 30

Total = **122 days**

122 ÷ 7 → remainder **3**

So:

**Monday + 3 = Thursday**

Therefore:

**18 October = Thursday**

### Step 2 — Make the DATE same

Now we need:

**18 October → 12 October**

Go **6 days backward**:

**Thursday − 6 = Friday**

Therefore:

## ✅ 12 October = Friday

So you can do Category 4 in either order:

**Method 1:**

> Date same → Month same

**18 June → 12 June → 12 October**

OR

**Method 2:**

> Month same → Date same

**18 June → 18 October → 12 October**

Both give:

# **Friday ✅**

The key idea is: **you can make the different parts same in any order; just keep moving the weekday correctly.**
