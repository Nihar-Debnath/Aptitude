Question:

> **On what dates of April 2001 did Wednesday fall?**

Options:

A. 1, 8, 15, 22, 29
B. 2, 9, 16, 23, 30
C. 3, 10, 17, 24
D. 4, 11, 18, 25

---

## Step 1: Find the day of **1 April 2001**

Once we know the day of 1 April, the rest is easy because every **7 days is the same weekday**.

So first find **1 April 2001**.

### Completed years

We take:

\[
2001-1=2000
\]

Now use our odd-day table:

\[
\boxed{2000\rightarrow0\text{ odd days}}
\]

Why 0?

Because 2000 is a complete 400-year cycle:

\[
2000=5\times400
\]

and:

\[
400\text{ years}=0\text{ odd days}
\]

So:

\[
2000\rightarrow0
\]

---

## Step 2: Months before April

We need January, February and March.

2001 is **not a leap year**.

So:

### January

\[
31\rightarrow3
\]

### February

\[
28\rightarrow0
\]

### March

\[
31\rightarrow3
\]

### April

\[
1\rightarrow1
\]

Total:

\[
3+1+3=7
\]

\[
\text{Which is }  0 \text{ odd days}
\]


So the months contribute:

\[
\boxed{0\text{ odd days}}
\]


---

## Step 3: Find the odd days for 1 April

use: 

| Odd days | Day          |
| -------: | ------------ |
|        0 | Sunday       |
|        1 | Monday       |
|        2 | Tuesday      |
|        3 | Wednesday    |
|        4 | Thursday     |
|        5 | Friday       |
|      6   |   Saturday   |


Using your **0–6 weekday mapping**, (0) is treated as **Sunday**:

\[
\boxed{0=\text{Sunday}}
\]

Therefore:

\[
\boxed{1\text{ April 2001 = Sunday}}
\]

Wednesday is **3 days after Sunday**:

\[
0\rightarrow Sunday
\]
\[
1\rightarrow Monday
\]
\[
2\rightarrow Tuesday
\]
\[
3\rightarrow Wednesday
\]

Then add 7 each time:

\[
\boxed{4,\ 11,\ 18,\ 25}
\]

### ✅ Answer: **D — 4th, 11th, 18th, 25th**
