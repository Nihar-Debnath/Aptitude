Yes — **exactly!** Since the **date and month are the same**, this is **Category 2**.

Given:

**11 March 2003 = Tuesday**

Find:

**11 March 2004 = ?**

### Step 1: Year difference

2004 − 2003 = **1 year**

Normally, 1 year = **1 odd day**.

### Step 2: Check leap year

**2004 is a leap year**, and because we are going:

**11 March 2003 → 11 March 2004**

we **cross February 29, 2004**.

So we get **1 extra odd day**.

Therefore:

**1 + 1 = 2 odd days**

### Step 3: Move forward

Tuesday + 2 days:

**Tuesday → Wednesday → Thursday**

## ✅ Answer: **Thursday (Option C)**

### Your Category 2 rule

When **DATE + MONTH are same**:

**Future → Past:** subtract odd days
**Past → Future:** add odd days

And for this question:

**11 Mar 2003 → 11 Mar 2004**

= **1 year + 1 leap-day extra = 2 odd days**

→ **Tuesday + 2 = Thursday**.
