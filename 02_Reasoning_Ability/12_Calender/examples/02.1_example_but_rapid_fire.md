Let's use the same question:

> **22 October 1964**

## ⚡ RAPID-FIRE METHOD

### Step 1 — Year − 1

\[
1964-1=1963
\]

Break:

\[
1963=1600+300+63
\]

Table:

\[
0+1
\]

For 63 years:

\[
63\div4=15
\]

Leap = 15, Ordinary = 48

\[
15(2)+48(1)=78
\]

\[
78\bmod7=1
\]

So year odd days:

\[
0+1+1=\boxed{2}
\]

---

### Step 2 — Months before October

1964 is **leap**.

Just use the month odd-day shortcut:

\[
Jan+Feb+Mar+Apr+May+Jun+Jul+Aug+Sep
\]

\[
3+1+3+2+3+2+3+3+2
\]

Instead of adding:

\[
\boxed{22\bmod7=1}
\]

---

### Step 3 — Date

\[
22\bmod7=\boxed1
\]

---

### Step 4 — Total

\[
2+1+1=4
\]

---

### Step 5 — Convert

Use:

```text
0 = Sunday
1 = Monday
2 = Tuesday
3 = Wednesday
4 = Thursday
5 = Friday
6 = Saturday
```

Therefore:

\[
\boxed{\text{Thursday}}
\]

# 🚀 Your RAPID-FIRE TEMPLATE

For **DD/MM/YYYY**:

### ① Year

\[
\boxed{YYYY-1}
\]

Break into century + remaining years.

Use:

\[
\boxed{100=5,\ 200=3,\ 300=1,\ 400=0}
\]

For remaining years:

\[
\boxed{\text{Leap}\times2+\text{Ordinary}\times1}
\]

Then `% 7`.

---

### ② Months

Add odd days of **months BEFORE the target month**.

Remember:

\[
31\to3,\quad30\to2,\quad28\to0,\quad29\to1
\]

Then `%7`.

---

### ③ Date

Just:

\[
\boxed{\text{date}%7}
\]

---

### ④ Add

\[
\boxed{\text{Year OD + Month OD + Date OD}}
\]

Then `%7`.

---

### ⑤ Convert

\[
\boxed{0S,\ 1M,\ 2T,\ 3W,\ 4T,\ 5F,\ 6S}
\]

---

## ⚠️ ONE thing that will save you time

**Don't calculate the full odd days and then reduce every single time.**

Keep reducing by 7 as you go.

For example:

\[
78\rightarrow1
\]

not:

\[
78\rightarrow70\rightarrow8\rightarrow1
\]

Just mentally:

\[
78\bmod7=1
\]

## 🔥 Ultra-short version for the exam

Write this mentally:

> **Y−1 → century OD + remaining-year OD → previous-month OD → date → %7 → day**

For:

**22 Oct 1964**

```text
1963 → 0+1+1 = 2
Jan-Sep → 22 → 1
22 → 1

2+1+1 = 4
4 = Thursday
```

**Answer: Thursday.**
