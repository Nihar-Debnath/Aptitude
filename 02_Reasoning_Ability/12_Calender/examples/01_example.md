Question:

> **Which day of the week was 18 March 1977?**

We need to find the weekday.

---

# Step 1: Break the year

We take the **years completed before 1977**, so:

\[
1977-1=1976
\]

Now break 1976 using the odd-day table:

\[
1976=1600+300+76
\]

From the table:

\[
1600\rightarrow0
\]

\[
300\rightarrow1
\]

So now we need the odd days of **76 years**.

---

# Step 2: Find odd days of 76 years

First find leap years:

\[
76\div4=19
\]

So there are **19 leap years**.

Therefore ordinary years:

\[
76-19=57
\]

Now:

### Ordinary years

\[
57\times1=57
\]

### Leap years

\[
19\times2=38
\]

Total:

\[
57+38=95
\]

Now divide by 7:

\[
95=91+4
\]

So:

\[
\boxed{76\text{ years}=4\text{ odd days}}
\]

---

# Step 3: Add the year odd days

From 1600:

\[
0
\]

From 300:

\[
1
\]

From 76:

\[
4
\]

Therefore:

\[
0+1+4=\boxed{5}
\]

So the completed years before 1977 give **5 odd days**.

---

# Step 4: Now deal with 1977

1977 is **not a leap year**.

We need to reach **18 March**.

Months completed before March:

### January

31 days:

\[
31\rightarrow3\text{ odd days}
\]

### February

28 days:

\[
28\rightarrow0
\]

So:

\[
3+0=3
\]

---

# Step 5: Add March 18

Here's the important part.

We want **18 March**, so we count **18 days**:

\[
18\div7
\]

Remainder:

\[
\boxed{4}
\]

So March contributes **4 odd days**.

---

# Step 6: Add everything

Years:

\[
5
\]

Months:

\[
3
\]

March:

\[
4
\]

Total:

\[
5+3+4=12
\]

Reduce by 7:

\[
12-7=5
\]

So we have:

\[
\boxed{5\text{ odd days}}
\]

---

# Step 7: Convert 5 odd days into a weekday

For the standard calendar method, **1 January 1 AD is Monday**.

So:

| Odd days | Day        |
| -------: | ---------- |
|        0 | Sunday     |
|        1 | Monday     |
|        2 | Tuesday    |
|        3 | Wednesday  |
|        4 | Thursday   |
|    **5** | **Friday** |
|        6 | Saturday   |

Therefore:

# ✅ **18 March 1977 was Friday.**

---

## 🔥 The whole calculation

You can write this in your exam as:

\[
1976=1600+300+76
\]

\[
0+1+\left(57\times1+19\times2\right)
\]

\[
=0+1+95
\]

\[
=96
\]

\[
96\div7\rightarrow5
\]

Then:

\[
Jan(3)+Feb(0)+March(18\rightarrow4)
\]

\[
3+0+4=7\rightarrow0
\]

Total:

\[
5+0=\boxed{5}
\]

→ **Friday**.

### ⚠️ One thing to notice

I earlier described the month calculation as "days before the date" loosely. For this particular **1 Jan 1 = Monday** method, using **18 March as 18 days** gives the correct weekday. If you're using another calendar shortcut/reference-day method, the counting convention can look slightly different.

The key workflow is:

**Completed years → odd days → completed months → date → total odd days → weekday.**
