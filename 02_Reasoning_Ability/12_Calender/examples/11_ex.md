### Question:

> What is the day **after 4 days after 2 days before the day before tomorrow?**

---

### 🟢 STEP 1

Start with:

**"the day before tomorrow"**

Visualize:

```text
TODAY → TOMORROW
          ↑
       1 day before
          ↓
        TODAY
```

So:

**Day before tomorrow = TODAY**

---

### 🟡 STEP 2

Now:

**"2 days before TODAY"**

```text
DAY BEFORE YESTERDAY ← YESTERDAY ← TODAY
         ↑                 ↑
       2 days            1 day
```

So we are here:

👉 **Day before yesterday**

---

### 🔵 STEP 3

Now go **4 days AFTER** that:

```text
Day before yesterday
        ↓ +1
    Yesterday
        ↓ +1
      Today
        ↓ +1
    Tomorrow
        ↓ +1
Day after tomorrow
```

So:

👉 **Day after tomorrow**

---

### 🔴 STEP 4

Finally:

**"the day AFTER"**

```text
Day after tomorrow → AFTER DAY AFTER TOMORROW
```

Therefore:

# ✅ C. After Day after tomorrow

### 🧠 Just remember this visual pattern:

```text
Day before yesterday
        ↓
    Yesterday
        ↓
      TODAY
        ↓
    Tomorrow
        ↓

        ↓
```

For these questions, **start from the innermost phrase and physically move left/right on this timeline.** That's much easier than trying to understand the entire sentence at once.
