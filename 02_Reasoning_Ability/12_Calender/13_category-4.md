Since **date, month, and year are all different**, we make them the same **one by one**.

Given:

**10 March 2013 → Monday**
Find: **27 August 2017 → ?**

### Step 1 — Make the DATE same

Change:

**10 March 2013 → 10 March 2017**

From 2013 to 2017:

* 2013 → 2014 = 1 odd day
* 2014 → 2015 = 1
* 2015 → 2016 = 1
* 2016 → 2017 = **2** because 2016 is leap year

Total = **5 odd days**

Monday + 5 = **Saturday**

So:

**10 March 2017 → Saturday**

---

### Step 2 — Make the MONTH same

Now:

**10 March 2017 → 27 March 2017**

Difference = 27 − 10 = **17 days**

17 ÷ 7 gives remainder **3**

Saturday + 3 = **Tuesday**

So:

**27 March 2017 → Tuesday**

---

### Step 3 — Make the YEAR same

Now:

**27 March 2017 → 27 August 2017**

Count the days:

* March → April = 31
* April → May = 30
* May → June = 31
* June → July = 30
* July → August = 31

Total = **153 days**

153 ÷ 7 → remainder **6**

Tuesday + 6 = **Monday**

Wait — there's an important correction here: this calculation is for **27 March → 27 August**, and the month lengths between those same dates are:

31 + 30 + 31 + 30 + 31 = **153**, yes.

So the final answer is:

## ✅ **Monday**

Your **method is absolutely right**:

**Date same → Month same → Year same → Final day**.
