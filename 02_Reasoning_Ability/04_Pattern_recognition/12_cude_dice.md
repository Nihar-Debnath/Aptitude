# What is Cube & Dice Reasoning?

This topic is about **3-dimensional visualization**.

You'll be given a **cube** or **dice**, and you have to answer questions like:

* Which faces are opposite?
* Which faces are adjacent?
* Which number comes on top?
* How many cubes are painted?
* How many cubes have 1, 2, or 3 painted faces?

---

# First Understand a Cube

A cube has:

* **6 Faces**
* **12 Edges**
* **8 Vertices (Corners)**

Example

```text
        Top
         □
      /-----/
Left □     □ Right
     |     |
     |     |
     □-----□
      Front
```

There is also:

* Bottom
* Back

which are hidden.

---

# Important Rule

Every face has

* **4 adjacent faces**
* **1 opposite face**

Example

If

```text
Top = 1
```

then

```text
Bottom
```

is its opposite.

The top face can never touch the bottom face.

---

# Most Important Concept

## Adjacent Faces

Adjacent means

> Two faces sharing an edge.

Example

Top touches

```text
Front

Back

Left

Right
```

Total = **4 adjacent faces**

---

## Opposite Faces

Opposite faces never touch.

Example

```text
Top ↔ Bottom

Front ↔ Back

Left ↔ Right
```

---

# Rule to Remember

Every face has

```text
4 Adjacent

1 Opposite
```

This single rule solves half the questions.

---

# Dice Basics

A normal die has numbers

```text
1

2

3

4

5

6
```

Opposite faces are

```text
1 ↔ 6

2 ↔ 5

3 ↔ 4
```

*(This is true only for a standard dice. In reasoning questions, the opposite faces are usually **not given** and must be determined from the views.)*

# Types of Cube & Dice Questions

There are mainly **5 types**.

---

# Type 1: Opposite Face

Example

View 1

```text
Top = 1

Front = 2

Right = 3
```

View 2

```text
Top = 1

Front = 3

Right = 5
```

Question

Which face is opposite to 1?

We'll learn the trick shortly.

---

# Type 2: Adjacent Faces

Question

Which face is adjacent to

```text
4
```

Remember

Every face has **4 adjacent faces**.

---

# Type 3: Missing Face

Given three visible faces

Find the hidden one.

---

# Type 4: Cube Rotation

The cube rotates.

Example

Initially

```text
Top = 1

Front = 2

Right = 3
```

Rotate right.

What becomes the new top?

---

# Type 5: Painted Cube

This is one of the most important topics.

A large cube is painted.

Then cut into many smaller cubes.

Questions ask

How many cubes have

* 3 painted faces?
* 2 painted faces?
* 1 painted face?
* No painted face?

---

# Opposite Face Trick

This is the most important trick.

Suppose

View 1

```text
Top = 1

Front = 2

Right = 3
```

View 2

```text
Top = 1

Front = 3

Right = 5
```

Notice

Top is same

```text
1
```

Only the other two changed.

The changing faces

```text
2

3

5
```

rotate around the top.

Here,

```text
2 and 5
```

become opposite.

This is called the **Common Face Method**.

---

# Common Face Method

Whenever two views have

one common face,

the remaining changing faces help determine opposites.

Example

View 1

```text
1

2

3
```

View 2

```text
1

3

5
```

Common

```text
1
```

Changing

```text
2

3

5
```

The face replacing **2** around the common face (**5**) is opposite to **2**.

This is the quickest method used in competitive exams.

---

# Cube Rotation

Suppose

```text
Top = A

Front = B

Right = C
```

Rotate right.

Result

```text
Top = Left

Right = Top

Bottom = Right
```

Instead of memorizing all rotations, imagine holding a real cube and turning it.

---

# Painted Cube Problems

Very common.

Example

A cube is painted on all six faces.

Then divided into

```text
3 × 3 × 3
```

small cubes.

Question

How many cubes have

3 painted faces?

---

# Three Painted Faces

These are

the **corner cubes**.

Every cube has

```text
8 corners
```

Answer

```text
8
```

Always.

---

# Two Painted Faces

These are the **edge cubes**.

Formula

```text
12 × (n−2)
```

Example

For

```text
3 × 3 × 3
```

```text
12 × (3−2)

=12
```

---

# One Painted Face

These are the **center cubes on each face**.

Formula

```text
6 × (n−2)²
```

Example

```text
6 × 1²

=6
```

---

# No Painted Face

These are the **inner cubes**.

Formula

```text
(n−2)³
```

Example

```text
(3−2)³

=1
```

---

# Important Formulas

Suppose the cube is divided into

```text
n × n × n
```

Then

| Type             | Formula |
| ---------------- | ------- |
| Total cubes      | n³      |
| 3 painted faces  | 8       |
| 2 painted faces  | 12(n−2) |
| 1 painted face   | 6(n−2)² |
| No painted faces | (n−2)³  |

---

# Example

A cube is cut into

```text
4 × 4 × 4
```

Find

Total cubes

```text
4³

=64
```

---

Three painted

```text
8
```

---

Two painted

```text
12 × 2

=24
```

---

One painted

```text
6 × 2²

=24
```

---

No paint

```text
2³

=8
```

# Common Tricks Used in Exams

## Trick 1

Opposite faces never appear together.

If

```text
1

2

3
```

are visible together,

then

```text
1 and 2

1 and 3

2 and 3
```

are **adjacent**, never opposite.

---

## Trick 2

Three visible faces always meet at one corner.

If three faces are shown together, they all share a single vertex.

---

## Trick 3

Painted Cube

Students often forget

Corner cubes

always =

```text
8
```

regardless of cube size.

---

## Trick 4

Hidden Faces

Only three faces are visible.

The remaining three must be imagined.

# Quick Solving Checklist

Whenever you see a Cube or Dice question:

1. ✅ Identify the three visible faces.
2. ✅ Find any common face across different views.
3. ✅ Remember: opposite faces never appear together.
4. ✅ Every face has **4 adjacent** and **1 opposite** face.
5. ✅ Use the Common Face Method for opposite-face questions.
6. ✅ For painted cubes, use formulas instead of drawing.

# Cube vs Dice

Many students think these are different topics.

| Cube                                            | Dice                                                                    |
| ----------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| A general six-faced solid                       | A numbered cube                                                         |
| Faces may have shapes, colors, or symbols       | Faces usually have numbers or dots                                      |
| Questions involve rotation, adjacency, painting | Questions mainly involve opposite faces, rotations, and visible numbers |

---

## Most Important Exam Tricks

There are **two ideas** that solve the majority of placement questions:

### 1. **Common Face Method** (for opposite-face questions)

If two views share one common face, compare the remaining faces to identify opposite faces.

### 2. **Painted Cube Formulas**

For a cube divided into (n \times n \times n):

* **Total cubes:** (n^3)
* **3 painted faces:** (8)
* **2 painted faces:** (12(n-2))
* **1 painted face:** (6(n-2)^2)
* **0 painted faces:** ((n-2)^3)

Mastering these two concepts will allow you to solve most Cube & Dice questions asked in placement exams quickly and accurately.
