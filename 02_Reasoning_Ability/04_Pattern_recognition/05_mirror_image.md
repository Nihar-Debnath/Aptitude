# What is a Mirror Image?

A **mirror image** is the image formed when an object is placed in front of a mirror.

The mirror reflects the object, causing the **left and right sides to interchange**, while the **top and bottom remain the same** (for a vertical mirror).

Think of standing in front of a bathroom mirror.

If you raise your **right hand**, your mirror image appears to raise its **left hand**.

---

# Types of Mirror Images

There are mainly **two types** asked in reasoning exams.

## 1. Vertical Mirror (Most Common)

The mirror is placed **on the left or right side** of the object.

```text
Mirror | ABC
```

The image becomes

```text
CBA | Mirror
```

Notice:

* Left becomes right.
* Right becomes left.
* Top and bottom do **not** change.

---

## 2. Horizontal Mirror (Water Image)

The mirror is placed **below** the object.

```text
ABC
────── Mirror
```

The image is upside down.

Here:

* Top becomes bottom.
* Bottom becomes top.
* Left and right stay the same.

These are called **Water Images** and are usually treated as a separate topic, but remember the difference.

---

Everything is reversed horizontally.

Some numbers also have vertical symmetry.

Approximately symmetrical:

```text

```

Most other digits change their appearance in a mirror.


# Example 1

Original

```text
CAT
```

Mirror image

Think in two steps.

### Step 1

Reverse the order.

```text
T A C
```

### Step 2

Each letter itself is mirrored.

So the final mirror image is **not simply "TAC"**. In the exam, the options will show mirrored letters, and you must choose the correct one.

**Important:** Never assume that reversing the order alone is enough.

# Common Tricks Used by Examiners

### Trick 1

Students only reverse the order.

Wrong.

You must also remember that **each character is reflected**.

---

### Trick 2

Ignoring symmetry.

Example

```text
AHA
```

Reverse

```text
AHA
```

Since **A** and **H** are vertically symmetrical, the mirror image looks very similar.

Many students think nothing changed, but this only works because of the symmetry of the letters.

---

### Trick 3

Directional Objects

Example

```text
→ ↑ ←
```

Mirror image

```text
→ ↑ ←
```

Let's analyze it:

* Reverse the order: `← ↑ →`
* Flip left-right directions:

  * `←` becomes `→`
  * `↑` remains `↑`
  * `→` becomes `←`

Final result:

```text
→ ↑ ←
```

This is an interesting case where the final sequence looks the same after both operations!

---

# How to Solve Mirror Image Questions Quickly

Whenever you see a mirror image question:

1. **Imagine the mirror** (usually on the right unless shown otherwise).
2. **Reverse the order** of all characters or objects.
3. **Flip each character horizontally** if it has a left-right direction.
4. **Check for symmetrical letters or numbers**, as they may remain unchanged.
5. **Compare with the options** rather than trying to draw the entire image yourself.

## Shortcut to Remember

**Mirror Image (Vertical Mirror):**

```text
Left  ⇄  Right
Top   ✔ Same
Bottom ✔ Same
```

**Water Image (Horizontal Mirror):**

```text
Top    ⇄ Bottom
Left   ✔ Same
Right  ✔ Same
```

Master these two rules, and you'll be able to solve most mirror image questions in placement exams within a few seconds.
