Great! The next major topic in Reasoning Ability is **Classification**, also called **Odd One Out**.

This topic is asked very frequently in placement exams because it tests your ability to recognize relationships and patterns.

---

# What is Classification?

Classification means:

> **Among a group of items, find the one that does NOT belong to the group.**

You have to identify the common property shared by most items and then find the item that breaks that pattern.

Example:

```text
Dog
Cat
Cow
Car
```

Think:

* Dog → Animal ✅
* Cat → Animal ✅
* Cow → Animal ✅
* Car → Vehicle ❌

Answer:

```text
Car
```

---

# How to Solve Classification Questions

Always follow this order.

### Step 1: Look for the common relationship.

Ask yourself:

* Are they animals?
* Fruits?
* Countries?
* Programming languages?
* Prime numbers?
* Squares?

---

### Step 2: Find the different one.

The one that doesn't follow the rule is the answer.

# Exam Strategy

Whenever you see a classification question, check in this order:

1. **Category** (animals, fruits, professions, etc.)
2. **Mathematical property** (prime, square, cube, even, odd)
3. **Alphabet pattern** (vowel/consonant, positions, sequence)
4. **Relationship** (city–country, profession–workplace, planet–star)
5. **Size, type, or structure** (number of letters, geometric shapes, word groups)

This systematic approach helps you avoid jumping to conclusions and increases your accuracy, especially when multiple options look similar.

**Placement exam difficulty:**

* **TCS NQT:** Mostly easy to moderate.
* **Cognizant GenC:** Easy to moderate, with a few tricky logical classifications.
* **Infosys, Capgemini, Wipro, Accenture:** Usually test category, number properties, alphabet patterns, and simple logical relationships. Complex puzzles are less common in this topic.
