Excellent! The next topic is **Water Images**, also called **Horizontal Mirror Images**.

This topic is very common in **TCS, Cognizant, Infosys, Wipro, Capgemini, Accenture**, SSC, Banking, and other aptitude exams.

Many students confuse **Mirror Image** and **Water Image**, but after this explanation, you'll never confuse them again.

---

# What is a Water Image?

Imagine an object placed above still water.

The water acts like a **horizontal mirror**.

Example

```text id="0n1g7v"
      TREE

~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
      Water
```

The reflection appears **below** the water.

Unlike a normal mirror,

* Left and Right remain the same.
* Top and Bottom are exchanged.

---

# Difference Between Mirror Image and Water Image

This is the most important concept.

| Mirror Image         | Water Image               |
| -------------------- | ------------------------- |
| Vertical mirror      | Horizontal mirror (water) |
| Left ↔ Right changes | Top ↔ Bottom changes      |
| Top stays same       | Left stays same           |

# Quick Solving Checklist

Whenever you see a Water Image question:

1. ✅ Imagine the figure reflected in water.
2. ✅ Flip **top and bottom**.
3. ✅ Keep **left and right unchanged**.
4. ✅ Do **not** reverse the order of letters or objects.
5. ✅ Check arrows and triangles carefully—they often reveal the correct answer.

---

# Mirror Image vs Water Image

This comparison is one of the most important for exams.

| Feature         | Mirror Image               | Water Image |
| --------------- | -------------------------- | ----------- |
| Mirror Position | Side                       | Bottom      |
| Left ↔ Right    | ✔ Changes                  | ❌ Same      |
| Top ↔ Bottom    | ❌ Same                     | ✔ Changes   |
| Word Order      | Reversed                   | Same        |
| Arrow →         | ←                          | →           |
| Arrow ↑         | ↑                          | ↓           |
| Triangle ▲      | Looks horizontally flipped | ▼           |

---

## Final Shortcut

Remember these two rules:

### 🪞 Mirror Image

```text
Left  ↔ Right
Top   ✔ Same
Bottom ✔ Same
```

### 🌊 Water Image

```text
Top    ↔ Bottom
Left   ✔ Same
Right  ✔ Same
```

If you can instantly identify **which axis is being flipped**, you'll solve almost every Mirror Image and Water Image question in just a few seconds.
