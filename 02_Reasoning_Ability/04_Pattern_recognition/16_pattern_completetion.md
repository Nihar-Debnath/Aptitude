This is one of the most common **Non-Verbal Reasoning** topics in placement exams like **TCS, Cognizant, Infosys, Wipro, Capgemini, and Accenture**.

---

# What is Pattern Completion?

In Pattern Completion, you are given an **incomplete figure or design**.

One part of the figure is missing.

Your task is:

> **Choose the missing piece that completes the figure perfectly.**

Imagine a jigsaw puzzle where one piece is missing.

Example

```text id="iv1fwz"
Original Pattern

+---------+
|  ○  ○   |
|  ○  ?   |
+---------+
```

You must select the piece that completes the design.

---

# Objective

You are given

```text id="n8gynf"
Incomplete Figure

↓

Find the Missing Piece

↓

Complete Figure
```

Unlike **Shape Construction**, here you are **not combining separate pieces**. You are simply finding the **one missing part**.

---

# Basic Rules

The missing piece must:

✔ Continue every line correctly.

✔ Continue every curve correctly.

✔ Continue every pattern correctly.

✔ Match the direction.

✔ Match the shading.

# Common Tricks Used in Exams

## Trick 1

Tiny Line Difference

One option looks correct but one line does not continue.

Always check the line endings.

---

## Trick 2

Curve Mismatch

The curve should join smoothly.

If it forms a sharp corner,

reject it.

---

## Trick 3

Wrong Rotation

Students choose the correct shape but the wrong orientation.

---

## Trick 4

Shading

Many options differ only in shading.

Look carefully.

---

## Trick 5

Direction

Arrow questions often differ only by direction.

# Step-by-Step Method

Whenever you solve Pattern Completion:

### Step 1

Look at the boundary where the piece is missing.

Ignore the rest of the figure.

---

### Step 2

Check which lines enter the missing area.

---

### Step 3

Imagine where those lines should leave the missing area.

---

### Step 4

Check curves.

Do they connect smoothly?

---

### Step 5

Compare

* Rotation
* Shading
* Size
* Position

---

### Step 6

Eliminate impossible options.

Whenever you see a Pattern Completion question:

2. ✅ Check curve continuation.

4. ✅ Check rotation.

# Pattern Completion vs Shape Construction vs Figure Matrix

Students often confuse these three topics.

| Topic                  | What You Do                                                              |
| ---------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Pattern Completion** | Find the **one missing piece** that completes an existing figure.        |
| **Shape Construction** | Combine several separate pieces to make a complete figure.               |
| **Figure Matrix**      | Find the missing figure by identifying patterns across rows and columns. |

### Final Strategy

The biggest secret to solving Pattern Completion questions is this:

> **Don't look at the entire picture first. Look only at the edges of the missing space.**

Ask yourself:

* Which lines enter the missing area?
* Where should they continue?
* Do the curves connect smoothly?
* Does the shading match?

Once the edges match perfectly, the correct option is usually obvious. This approach is much faster and more accurate than trying to compare every option against the whole figure.
