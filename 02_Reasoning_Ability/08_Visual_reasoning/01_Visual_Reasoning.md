# What is Visual Reasoning?

Visual Reasoning tests your ability to identify patterns, shapes, rotations, reflections, and relationships between figures.

Instead of words or numbers, you'll see **images**.

Example:

```text
⬜ → ⬛ → ⬜ → ?
```

or

```text
▲ ▼ ▲ ?
```

You have to find the next figure or the odd one out.

---

# Types of Visual Reasoning Questions

## 1. Figure Series ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ (Most Common)

Find the next figure in a sequence.

Example:

```text
○
↓

◐
↓

●
↓

?
```

Look for changes like:

* Rotation
* Shading
* Size
* Number of objects

---

## 2. Odd One Out

Four figures are similar, one is different.

Example:

```text
□   □   △   □
```

Answer:

△ is the odd one.

---

## 3. Mirror Image ⭐⭐⭐⭐

Find how an object looks in a mirror.

Example:

```text
ABC | Mirror
```

Becomes

```text
CBA
```

Remember:

* Left ↔ Right changes.
* Top and bottom stay the same.

---

## 4. Water Image

Reflection in water.

Example:

```text
A
```

Becomes upside down.

Top ↔ Bottom changes.

Left and right remain the same.

---

## 5. Rotation

Find the rotated figure.

Example:

```text
↑
```

90° clockwise →

```text
→
```

180° →

```text
↓
```

270° →

```text
←
```

---

## 6. Embedded Figure

Find a small shape hidden inside a larger figure.

Example:

Find

```text
△
```

inside a complicated drawing.

---

## 7. Paper Folding

A paper is folded and punched.

Question:

When opened,

Where will the holes appear?

---

## 8. Paper Cutting

A folded paper is cut.

Predict the final pattern.

---

## 9. Cube & Dice

Questions involve:

* Opposite faces
* Adjacent faces
* Visible faces after rotation

---

## 10. Figure Completion

One part is missing.

Choose the correct piece.

---

## 11. Pattern Completion

Complete the design.

Usually based on symmetry.

---

## 12. Counting Figures

Count:

* Squares
* Triangles
* Rectangles

inside a diagram.

# Universal Solving Strategy

Whenever you see a visual reasoning question, check these in order:

### 1. Rotation 🔄

Is the shape rotating?

---

### 2. Reflection 🪞

Is it a mirror or water image?

---

### 3. Number 🔢

Are objects increasing or decreasing?

---

### 4. Size 📏

Are shapes getting bigger or smaller?

---

### 5. Shading ⚫⚪

Is the black/white area changing?

---

### 6. Position 📍

Is the object moving?

---

### 7. Direction ➡️

Is the arrow changing direction?

---

### 8. Shape 🔺

Is the shape itself changing?

---

# Fast Elimination Trick

Before trying to find the answer:

* Eliminate options with the **wrong rotation**.
* Eliminate options with the **wrong number of shapes**.
* Eliminate options with the **wrong shading**.
* Compare only the remaining options.

This is much faster than analyzing every choice in detail.

# Study Order

I recommend learning Visual Reasoning in this sequence:

1. Figure Series
2. Odd One Out
3. Mirror Image
4. Water Image
5. Rotation
6. Figure Completion
7. Embedded Figures
8. Counting Figures
9. Cube & Dice
10. Paper Folding & Paper Cutting

This order goes from the easiest and most frequently asked topics to the more advanced ones.

We'll follow the same approach we used for Blood Relations:

* Learn one type.
* Understand the pattern.
* Learn shortcuts.
* Practice TCS-level questions.
* Move to the next type only after mastering the current one.
