## 1️⃣ Person is facing the CENTRE

Imagine A is sitting here and looking toward the middle:

```text
        A
        ↓
       CENTER
```

For a person **facing the centre**:
- Here we are taking the head of the arrow for checking

### 👉 Right = Clockwise

### 👈 Left = Anti-clockwise
