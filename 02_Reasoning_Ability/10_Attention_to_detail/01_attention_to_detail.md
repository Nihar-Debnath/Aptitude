## What is Attention to Detail?

It tests whether you can **spot tiny differences between things that look almost identical**.

For example:

```text
Original:  AB7C9X2P
Option:    AB7C9X2P  ✅

Original:  AB7C9X2P
Option:    AB7C9K2P  ❌
```

Only **one character** changed: `X → K`.

That's attention to detail.

---

## What types can TCS give?

You'll commonly see:

### 1. Compare two strings

```text
ABC7X92PQ
ABC7X92PQ
```

Same → ✅

Or:

```text
ABC7X92PQ
ABC7X29PQ
```

Different → ❌

---

### 2. Find the different character

Example:

```text
A7B9C2D8
A7B9C2D6
       ↑
```

Answer = **8 vs 6**

---

### 3. Numbers

```text
58392174
58392147
```

Very similar, but the last four digits have changed order.

---

### 4. Symbols / shapes

You may get something like:

```text
△ ○ □ ★ △
△ ○ □ ☆ △
```

Only the star is different.

---

# 🔥 The most important trick

**DON'T try to read the entire thing repeatedly.**

Compare **position by position**.

Suppose:

```text
Original:  8 A 4 K 9 P 2 M
Option:    8 A 4 K 9 R 2 M
```

Go:

```text
1 → 8 = same
2 → A = same
3 → 4 = same
4 → K = same
5 → 9 = same
6 → P ≠ R  ❌
```

**STOP.**

You've found the difference.

---

## ⚠️ Characters that are designed to trick you

Pay special attention to:

* `O` vs `0`
* `I` vs `1`
* `S` vs `5`
* `B` vs `8`
* `Z` vs `2`
* `G` vs `6`
* `C` vs `G`

And numbers where **two digits have swapped places**:

```text
12345678
12346578
```

Here `5` and `6` have swapped.

## 🧠 Your exam strategy

When you see an Attention to Detail question:

**Step 1:** Don't panic because everything looks similar.

**Step 2:** Compare from **left → right**.

**Step 3:** Compare **one position at a time**.

**Step 4:** The moment you find a mismatch, **stop and eliminate that option**.
