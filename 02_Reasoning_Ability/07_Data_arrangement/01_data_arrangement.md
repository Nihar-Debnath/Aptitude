**Data Arrangement** (also called **Arrangement & Ordering**, **Seating Arrangement**, or **Puzzle Arrangement**) is one of the most important topics in **Logical Reasoning**.

It tests your ability to **arrange people or objects according to given conditions** and answer questions based on that arrangement.

---

# What is Data Arrangement?

You are given some clues, such as:

* Who sits where
* Who is taller
* Who likes which subject
* Who lives on which floor
* Who owns which car

Your job is to **arrange the data correctly**.

---

# Simple Example

There are **5 friends**:

* A
* B
* C
* D
* E

They are sitting in a row.

Conditions:

* A sits left of B.
* C sits at the extreme right.
* D sits between A and B.

Find the arrangement.

Let's solve step by step.

### Step 1

C is at the extreme right.

```text
_  _  _  _  C
```

---

### Step 2

D is between A and B.

Possible block:

```text
A D B
```

---

### Step 3

Place the block.

```text
A  D  B  E  C
```

Now all conditions are satisfied.

# Types of Data Arrangement Questions

## 1. Linear Arrangement ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ (Most Common)

People sit in a straight line.

Example:

```text
A  B  C  D  E
```

Questions involve:

* Left
* Right
* Immediate neighbour
* Between
* Extreme ends

---

## 2. Circular Arrangement

People sit around a table.

```text
        A

    B       C

    E       D
```

Questions:

* Who sits opposite whom?
* Who is second to the left?
* Clockwise / Anti-clockwise

---

## 3. Floor Arrangement

People live on different floors.

```text
7th  A
6th  D
5th  B
4th  E
3rd  C
```

Questions:

* Who lives above whom?
* Highest floor
* Second from bottom

---

## 4. Box / Object Arrangement

Boxes are arranged by colour, weight, or size.

Example:

* Red box is left of Blue.
* Green is heavier than Yellow.

---

## 5. Ranking Arrangement

People are arranged by rank.

Example:

```text
1st
2nd
3rd
4th
5th
```

Questions:

* Who comes before whom?
* Third from the top
* Rank from bottom

---

## 6. Scheduling Arrangement

Arrange events by:

* Day
* Month
* Time
* Order

Example:

Monday → English

Tuesday → Maths

Wednesday → Science

---

## 7. Mixed Puzzle Arrangement

The hardest type.

Example:

There are

* 5 people
* 5 cities
* 5 colours
* 5 professions

You must match all of them.

---

# Common Words Used

| Word            | Meaning                 |
| --------------- | ----------------------- |
| Immediate left  | Exactly one place left  |
| Immediate right | Exactly one place right |
| Second left     | Two places left         |
| Between         | Middle of two people    |
| Extreme left    | First seat              |
| Extreme right   | Last seat               |
| Adjacent        | Next to                 |
| Opposite        | Facing each other       |

# Example (TCS Level)

Five friends sit in a row.

* A is left of B.
* C is right of D.
* D is left of E.
* B is at the extreme right.

### Step 1

B is fixed.

```text
_  _  _  _  B
```

---

### Step 2

A is left of B.

```text
_  _  _  A  B
```

---

### Step 3

D is left of E.

Possible block:

```text
D E
```

---

### Step 4

C is right of D.

Only one arrangement works:

```text
D  C  E  A  B
```

---

# Tricks to Solve Data Arrangement

### 1. Draw a table first.

Don't solve mentally.

Example:

```text
_  _  _  _  _
```

---

### 2. Fix definite positions first.

Examples:

* Extreme left
* Extreme right
* Middle seat
* Top floor
* Bottom floor

These clues give fixed positions.

---

### 3. Make blocks.

If the clue says:

> A sits immediately left of B

Treat them as one block:

```text
[A B]
```

---

### 4. Eliminate impossible positions.

If a clue says:

> C cannot sit at either end

Then don't place C at the ends.

---

### 5. Use Pencil Logic

Keep filling one clue at a time.

Never try to solve all clues together.

# Is Data Arrangement Important for TCS Ignite?

**Yes, but the level varies by exam.**

For **TCS Ignite / TCS Smart**, you are most likely to encounter:

* ✅ Linear Arrangement (most common)
* ✅ Simple Seating Arrangement
* ✅ Ranking Arrangement
* ✅ Small Floor Arrangement (4–6 people)

The more difficult puzzles with **8–10 people, multiple attributes, or circular arrangements** are much more common in banking exams (IBPS, SBI, RRB) than in TCS.

---

# Recommended Learning Order

Since you're preparing for **TCS Ignite**, I recommend learning Data Arrangement in this sequence:

1. ✅ Linear Arrangement (left, right, between)
2. ✅ Ranking Arrangement
3. ✅ Circular Arrangement
4. ✅ Floor Arrangement
5. ✅ Mixed Puzzle Arrangement

This order builds your skills gradually, and each topic becomes easier because it uses the same logical thinking process as the previous one.
