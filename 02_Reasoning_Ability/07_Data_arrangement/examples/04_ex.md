Yes — this is a **reading-order / sequencing question**. The easiest way is to convert every sentence into a simple position rule.

We have 6 people:

**A, B, C, D, E, F**

Since only one person can read at a time, we need to find the order.

---

## Step 1: "Two individuals have read the novel before E"

This means exactly **2 people are before E**.

So E must be in **position 3**.

```text
1   2   3   4   5   6
_   _   E   _   _   _
```

---

## Step 2: "Before reading F, B and D have finished reading"

This means:

**B and D must come before F.**

So:

```text
B < F
D < F
```

---

## Step 3: "There are three people between A and F"

This is the most useful clue.

If there are **3 people between A and F**, their positions must have a gap of 4.

Possible pairs:

```text
A _ _ _ F
F _ _ _ A
```

Since E is already at position 3, let's test.

### Possibility 1:

```text
A _ _ _ F _
```

That means:

```text
A = 1
F = 5
```

But F must come **after B and D**.

There are only positions 2, 3, 4 before F.

E already occupies 3.

So B and D must occupy 2 and 4:

```text
A  B  E  D  F  _
```

The remaining person is C, so:

```text
A B E D F C
```

But now look at:

> **C gives the novel to the last person.**

C would be the **last person**, so C cannot give the book to the last person.

❌ Therefore this arrangement doesn't work.

---

### Possibility 2:

```text
F _ _ _ A _
```

So:

```text
F = 1
A = 5
```

But B and D must be **before F**.

That's impossible because F is already first.

❌ So this doesn't work either.

---

Wait — there's an important interpretation here.

The clue **"C gives the novel to the last person"** means C must be the person **immediately before the last person**.

So C must be at position **5**, and the last person is position 6.

But from the A/F clue, we need A and F to be separated by 4 positions.

Since position 5 is C, A cannot be position 5.

Therefore the only workable pair is:

```text
A = 2
F = 6
```

Because:

```text
Position:  1   2   3   4   5   6
           _   A   E   _   C   F
```

Now B and D must be before F.

Available positions before F are:

**1, 2, 3, 4**

A is already 2 and E is 3.

Therefore B and D must occupy:

**1 and 4**

So we get:

```text
1   2   3   4   5   6
B   A   E   D   C   F
```

Now check the final clue:

> **A does not get novel from D.**

D is at position 4 and A is at position 2.

So A did **not** get the novel directly from D. ✅

---

# 🎯 Final order

**B → A → E → D → C → F**

Or:

### **BAEDCF**

---

## The important trick for these questions

Don't try to solve all clues simultaneously.

Use the clues in this order:

### ① Exact position clues

> "Two people before E"

Immediately:

**E = 3rd**

### ② Distance clues

> "Three people between A and F"

Means:

**A and F have 4 positions difference.**

### ③ Before/after clues

> "B and D finished before F"

Means:

**B < F, D < F**

### ④ Transfer clues

> "C gives the novel to the last person"

Means:

**C = second-last, and someone else = last.**

Then everything falls into place.

**Final: B A E D C F.**
