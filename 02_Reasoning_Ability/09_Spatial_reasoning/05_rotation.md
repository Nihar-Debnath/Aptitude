## 1. What is Rotation?

**Rotation = turning a shape around a fixed point.**

Think about a fan:

```text
       /
      /
----- ● -----
      \
       \
```

The **● is the center**.

The fan blades don't move away from the center or become bigger — they **turn around the center**.

That's rotation.

---

## 2. The 3 things you need to look for

Whenever you see a rotation question, ask:

### **① Around what point?**

Usually:

\[
(0,0)
\]

which is called the **origin**.

### **② How much?**

Usually:

* 90°
* 180°
* 270°
* 360°

### **③ Which direction?**

* **Clockwise** ↻
* **Anticlockwise** ↺

---

# 3. The most important rules

If rotation is **around the origin (0,0)**:

### 90° CLOCKWISE ↻

\[
\boxed{(x,y)\rightarrow(y,-x)}
\]

Example:

\[
(2,3)
\]

becomes:

\[
(3,-2)
\]

---

### 90° ANTICLOCKWISE ↺

\[
\boxed{(x,y)\rightarrow(-y,x)}
\]

Example:

\[
(2,3)
\]

becomes:

\[
(-3,2)
\]

---

### 180°

This one is super easy:

\[
\boxed{(x,y)\rightarrow(-x,-y)}
\]

Example:

\[
(2,3)\rightarrow(-2,-3)
\]

---

### 360°

It comes back to exactly where it started:

\[
\boxed{(x,y)\rightarrow(x,y)}
\]

---

## 🧠 But don't memorize everything yet

First understand this:

### Translation

**Move**

### Scaling/Dilation

**Make bigger/smaller**

### Rotation

**Turn**

| Transformation | Think             |
| -------------- | ----------------- |
| Translation    | 🚶 Move           |
| Dilation       | 🔍 Bigger/smaller |
| Rotation       | 🔄 Turn           |

So if TCS gives you a rotation graph, your first question should be:

> **"Which point is the shape rotating around, and how many degrees did it turn?"**
