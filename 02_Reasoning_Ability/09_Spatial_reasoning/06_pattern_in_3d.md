Absolutely bro. **Pattern in 3D** is basically the 3D version of the pattern/visual reasoning you've been doing.

The important thing is: **don't try to imagine the whole 3D object at once.** Break it into simple rules.

## What is "Pattern in 3D"?

You'll usually get something like:

* a **cube**
* a **dice-like object**
* a 3D block with symbols/numbers
* a sequence of 3D shapes

And you have to figure out **what comes next** or **which option matches**.

Think of it as:

> **2D pattern = look at a flat picture**
> **3D pattern = look at how the faces/objects change in space**

### The 3 things you should check first

**1. What is changing?**

For example:

* number of dots
* symbols
* shaded faces
* direction
* position of a particular face

**2. What is NOT changing?**

This is extremely useful for elimination.

If a symbol always stays opposite another symbol, an option violating that can immediately go.

**3. Is the object rotating?**

Most 3D questions involve the cube/object being:

* rotated left/right
* rotated up/down
* flipped
* rearranged according to a pattern

---

### 🔥 For TCS-type questions, use this mindset

Don't ask:

> "How do I visualize this complicated 3D thing?"

Ask:

> **"What happens to each face when I rotate the object?"**

For example, imagine a cube:

```text
       TOP
        ▲
        │
LEFT ◄ CUBE ► RIGHT
        │
       FRONT
```

If you rotate it to the **right**, the faces don't randomly change.

They move in a fixed cycle:

**Front → Right → Back → Left → Front**

while the **Top and Bottom** stay where they are.

That's the kind of pattern you need to track.

---

## Most important trick

### ⭐ Pick ONE unique face/object and track it.

Suppose a cube has:

* ⭐ star
* ● circle
* ▲ triangle
* ■ square
* etc.

Don't track all 6 faces.

Pick the **star**.

Ask:

> Where is ⭐ now?

Then:

> Where should ⭐ be after the next rotation?

This lets you eliminate options very quickly.

---

### One more important thing

There are actually **different types of 3D reasoning questions**:

1. **Cube rotation**
2. **Cube unfolding / nets**
3. **3D pattern sequence**
4. **Counting cubes**
5. **Hidden/visible faces**
6. **Paper folding / 3D folding**
7. **Mirror/rotation of 3D objects**
