**Spatial Reasoning** is a part of **Non-Verbal/Analytical Reasoning** that tests your ability to **visualize objects in 2D and 3D space**.

In simple words:

> **Can you mentally imagine how an object looks after rotating, folding, reflecting, or assembling it?**

---

# Topics in Spatial Reasoning

### 1. Mirror Image 🪞

Find how an object looks in a mirror.

---

### 2. Water Image 🌊

Find the reflection in water.

---

### 3. Rotation 🔄

Rotate a figure by 90°, 180°, or 270°.

---

### 4. Paper Folding 📄

Fold a paper.

Predict the final shape.

---

### 5. Paper Cutting ✂️

Fold → Cut → Open

Predict the holes or cuts.

---

### 6. Cube & Dice 🎲

Questions like:

* Which face is opposite?
* Which faces are adjacent?
* What will be visible after rotation?

---

### 7. 3D Object Visualization

Imagine blocks or shapes from different angles.

---

### 8. Figure Completion

Choose the missing piece.

---

### 9. Embedded Figures

Find a small shape hidden inside a large figure.

---

### 10. Mental Assembly

Join different pieces to form one figure.

---

# Difference from Other Topics

### Image & Figure Counting

You **count**:

* Squares
* Rectangles
* Triangles
* Straight Lines

---

### Spatial Reasoning

You **visualize**:

* Rotation
* Folding
* Reflection
* Cubes
* Dice
* Hidden figures

---

# Example

Suppose you see:

```text
    ↑
```

Rotate it 90° clockwise.

Answer:

```text
    →
```

This is **Spatial Reasoning**.
