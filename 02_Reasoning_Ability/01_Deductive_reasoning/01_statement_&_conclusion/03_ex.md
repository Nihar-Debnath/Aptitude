Yes, I understand why you're thinking **"at least 50%"**, but here **"at least" is NOT possible**. Let's see why very simply.

We know:

> If **X participates**, **Y does not participate**.

So **X and Y can never participate in the same event.**

Now:

> **Y participated in 50% of the events.**

Imagine there are **100 events**.

* Y participated in **50 events**.
* In those 50 events, X **cannot** participate.
* Therefore X can participate only in the **remaining 50 events**.

So:

**X ≤ 50%**

That's why the answer is:

### ✅ X participated in **at most 50%** of the events.

---

### Why NOT "at least 50%"?

Because X could participate in:

* **0%** of events
* **10%**
* **20%**
* **40%**
* **50%**

All of these are possible.

For example:

```text
100 events

Y: ████████████████████ 50%
X:                      0%
```

This satisfies the condition.

Or:

```text
Y: ████████████████████ 50%
X: ████████████████████ 50%
```

This also satisfies the condition because they participate in different events.

But X **cannot exceed 50%**, because Y already occupies 50% of the events.

---

### 🔥 Remember this pattern

If:

> **X → NOT Y**

and Y participates in **50%**,

then:

> **X can participate in the remaining 50% at maximum.**

Therefore:

**X ≤ 50% → AT MOST 50%** ✅

**"At least 50%" would mean X ≥ 50%, which we cannot guarantee.**
