This is actually one of the **most important ideas** in Statement & Course of Action.

When teachers say:

> **"See the solution from a political/government eye."**

they don't mean politics or political parties.

They mean:

> **Think like a responsible authority** (government, principal, police, company management, hospital administration).

Not like an emotional person.

---

## Example 1

**Statement:**

> Theft has increased in the city.

### Emotional thinking ❌

> Hang all thieves.

Very emotional.

Not practical or legal.

---

### Government thinking ✅

> Increase police patrolling.
>
> Install CCTV cameras.
>
> Strengthen law enforcement.

These are **practical, legal, and realistic**.

---

## Example 2

**Statement:**

> Students are failing in mathematics.

### Parent thinking ❌

> Close the school.

Emotional.

---

### School management thinking ✅

> Arrange extra classes.
>
> Improve teaching methods.

Practical.

---

## Example 3

**Statement:**

> Water shortage has become severe.

### Emotional thinking ❌

> Everyone should leave the city.

Not practical.

---

### Government thinking ✅

> Promote water conservation.
>
> Repair leaking pipelines.
>
> Construct rainwater harvesting systems.

---

# Why do aptitude exams expect this?

Because the exam wants to know whether you can make **sound decisions**, not emotional ones.

A good Course of Action should be:

* ✅ Practical
* ✅ Legal
* ✅ Reasonable
* ✅ Directly related to the problem
* ✅ Feasible with available resources

---

# Think like this

Instead of asking:

> **"What would I do?"**

Ask:

> **"What should a responsible authority do?"**

That mindset gives the correct answer most of the time.

---

# Quick Comparison

| Problem          | Emotional Eye ❌    | Government/Practical Eye ✅                  |
| ---------------- | ------------------ | ------------------------------------------- |
| Crime increases  | Kill all criminals | Increase police patrols                     |
| Pollution        | Stop all factories | Enforce pollution-control rules             |
| Road accidents   | Ban all vehicles   | Improve traffic enforcement and road safety |
| Low exam results | Cancel exams       | Improve teaching and provide support        |

---

## Golden Rule for Course of Action

Whenever you see a question, imagine **you are the District Magistrate, School Principal, CEO, or Government Officer**.

Ask yourself:

> **"What action would a responsible authority take that is legal, practical, and actually helps solve the problem?"**

That's why teachers call it looking through a **"political eye"**—it's really about **policy-level, practical decision-making**, not politics.
