In **Deductive Reasoning**, **Mathematical Questions** are those where you use **numbers, quantities, percentages, ratios, ages, marks, etc.** along with logical deductions to reach the answer. They are **not calculation-heavy aptitude questions**. Instead, they test whether you can draw conclusions from mathematical facts.

## Types of Mathematical Deductive Reasoning Questions

### 1. Quantity Comparison

You compare two quantities based on the given statements.

**Example:**

* A is taller than B.
* B is taller than C.

What can you conclude?

✔ A is taller than C.

This is deduction using comparison.

---

### 2. Age-Based Questions

**Statements:**

* Ram is older than Shyam.
* Shyam is older than Mohan.

Conclusion?

✔ Ram is the oldest.

---

### 3. Marks / Scores

**Statements:**

* Rahul scored more than Amit.
* Amit scored more than Neha.

Conclusion?

✔ Rahul scored more than Neha.

---

### 4. Percentage / Ratio

**Statements:**

* 60% of students are boys.
* All boys wear uniforms.

Conclusion?

✔ Some students who wear uniforms are boys.

---

### 5. Set-Based Mathematics

These involve words like **all, some, none, only**.

**Example:**

* All even numbers are divisible by 2.
* 8 is an even number.

Conclusion?

✔ 8 is divisible by 2.

This follows the same pattern as:

* All amphibians live on land and water.
* Newt is an amphibian.
* Therefore, Newt lives on land and water.

---

### 6. Ranking

**Statements:**

* A ranks above B.
* B ranks above C.

Conclusion?

✔ A ranks above C.

---

### 7. Profit/Loss or Money Logic

**Statements:**

* Every product costs more than ₹500.
* Laptop X is one of these products.

Conclusion?

✔ Laptop X costs more than ₹500.

---

## Common Deduction Patterns

These are the patterns you should memorize:

| Given        | Conclusion            |
| ------------ | --------------------- |
| All A are B  | If X is A → X is B    |
| A > B, B > C | A > C                 |
| A = B, B = C | A = C                 |
| Some A are B | At least one A is B   |
| No A is B    | A and B never overlap |

---

## Example (Placement Exam Level)

**Statements:**

1. All engineers know C++.
2. Rohan is an engineer.

Conclusion?

✔ Rohan knows C++.

---

**Statements:**

1. Rahul's salary is greater than Amit's.
2. Amit's salary is greater than Neha's.

Conclusion?

✔ Rahul's salary is greater than Neha's.

---

## Difference Between Aptitude Math and Deductive Math

| Aptitude Mathematics     | Mathematical Deductive Reasoning        |
| ------------------------ | --------------------------------------- |
| Solve equations          | Draw conclusions from facts             |
| Heavy calculations       | Very little calculation                 |
| Uses formulas            | Uses logical implications               |
| Example: Find 25% of 480 | Example: If A > B and B > C, then A > C |
