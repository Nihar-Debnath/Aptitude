### Q2

**Statement:** Electric Vehicles produce less air pollutants.

**Assumption:** Electric Vehicles will be the only means of transportation in future.

❌ **False**

**Rule Applied:**

* **Rule 1:** Uses the extreme word **"only"**.
* **Rule 3:** Talks about the **future** ("will be").

### Q3

**Statement:** Seats allotted for only physically disabled people.

**Assumption:** Only females who are physically disabled can occupy such seats.

❌ **False**

**Rule Applied:**

* **Rule 2:** "Females" is **not mentioned** in the statement (extra information).
* **Rule 4:** The statement is for **all physically disabled people**, but the assumption restricts it to **only females**, which changes the meaning.
