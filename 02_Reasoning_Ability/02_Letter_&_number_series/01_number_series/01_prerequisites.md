## My recommendation for TCS, Cognizant, and similar placement exams

Don't try to memorize everything at once. Learn them in this order:

1. Alphabet positions (A=1 to Z=26)
2. Squares (1–30 first, then 31–50)
3. Cubes (1–20)
4. Prime numbers (up to 100 first, then 200)
5. Tables (1–20 first, then 21–30)
6. Fibonacci, powers, and factorials

Once you're comfortable with these, Letter & Number Series questions become much easier because you'll recognize patterns almost instantly.
