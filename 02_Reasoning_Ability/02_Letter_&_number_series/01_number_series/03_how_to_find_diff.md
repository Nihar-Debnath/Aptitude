# The Golden Rule

Never jump randomly.

Always check in this exact order:

```
1. Difference (★★★★★)
2. Second Difference (★★★★★)
3. Multiplication (★★★★★)
4. Division
5. Alternate Terms
6. Squares
7. Cubes
8. Prime Numbers
9. Combination Patterns
10. Special Series (Fibonacci, Factorial, Powers)
```

This order solves **about 90–95% of series questions** in placement exams.

---

## One final tip

In exams like **TCS, Cognizant, Accenture, and Capgemini**, **start with the differences first**. Around **60–70% of number series questions** are solved either by:

* a **constant difference** (e.g., +5, +10),
* a **changing difference** (e.g., +2, +4, +6, +8),
* or a **second-difference pattern**.

That's why experienced aptitude solvers almost always subtract consecutive terms before trying anything else. It quickly reveals the hidden structure in most series.
