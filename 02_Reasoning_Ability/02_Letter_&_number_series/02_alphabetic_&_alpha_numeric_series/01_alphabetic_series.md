# For TCS, Cognizant, Accenture, Wipro

The most frequently asked patterns are:

1. ✅ Consecutive (+1, +2, +3, +4)
2. ✅ Reverse alphabet
3. ✅ Alternate series (odd-even)
4. ✅ Increasing/decreasing jumps
5. ✅ Mirror letters
6. ✅ Alphabet positions (odd, even, prime)
7. ✅ Mixed patterns

---

## Prerequisites you should memorize before solving Alphabet Series

* Alphabet positions (A = 1 to Z = 26)
* Reverse alphabet (Z to A)
* Mirror pairs (A↔Z, B↔Y, C↔X, ...)
* Odd positions: 1, 3, 5, 7, ...
* Even positions: 2, 4, 6, 8, ...
* Prime positions up to 26: **2, 3, 5, 7, 11, 13, 17, 19, 23**
