### Question

> Kaleen Bhaiya is facing a pole at **1:48 PM**. The shadow of the pole fell towards his **right**. Which direction is he facing?

Options: West, South, East, North.

---

## Step 1: Look at the time ⏰

**1:48 PM = afternoon, after noon.**

For these reasoning questions, we use:

* Morning → Sun in **East**
* Afternoon/evening → Sun moves toward **West**

So here:

### ☀️ Sun = West

---

## Step 2: Find the shadow

Shadow is always **opposite to the Sun**.

Sun = West

Therefore:

### Shadow = East

So the question is effectively saying:

> **East is on Kaleen Bhaiya's right side.**

---

## Step 3: If East is on your right, which way are you facing?

Let's check:

### Facing North:

```text
             North
               ↑
               🧍
        West ←     → East
                    RIGHT
```

When facing **North**:

**East = Right** ✅

Therefore:

# ✅ Answer: D) North

---

## 🧠 Whole question in one chain

```text
1:48 PM
   ↓
Afternoon
   ↓
Sun = West
   ↓
Shadow = East
   ↓
Shadow is on his RIGHT
   ↓
East is on his RIGHT
   ↓
He is facing NORTH
```

### ⭐ Final Answer: **North**

---

### One important thing for your notes

For these **exam-style questions**, remember:

| Time                      | Sun   | Shadow |
| ------------------------- | ----- | ------ |
| 🌅 Morning / sunrise      | East  | West   |
| ☀️ After noon / afternoon | West* | East*  |
| 🌇 Evening / sunset       | West  | East   |

*This is the **simplified convention commonly used in reasoning questions**. In real astronomy, the Sun's exact direction changes continuously with time, location, and season.

So for your aptitude exam, when you see **1:48 PM**, treat it as the **post-noon/West-side Sun → East-side shadow** convention unless the question gives some more specific information.
