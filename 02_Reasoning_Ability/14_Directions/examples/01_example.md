## Question

> A boy is facing **West** and turns **45° clockwise**.
> Which direction is he facing now?

---

## Step 1: Find West

```text
             North
               ↑
               |
West  ←────────●────────→ East
               |
               ↓
             South
```

The boy is facing:

**West ←**

---

## Step 2: What does clockwise mean?

Remember:

> **Clockwise = turn to your RIGHT**

So the question is basically saying:

> The boy is facing West. What is **45° to his right**?

Now imagine the boy actually facing West:

```text
        Boy
         🧍  ← West
```

His **right side** points toward **North**.

So:

**West → 45° clockwise → North-West**

Why?

Because West and North are 90° apart:

```text
             North
               ↑
               |
               | 45°
               ↖
             NW
               |
West ←─────────●
```

Therefore:

### ✅ Answer = **North-West (NW)**

---

# Now understand the 45° part

This is the key idea from your screenshot.

The main directions are 90° apart:

```text
             N
             ↑
             |
             | 90°
             |
W ←──────────●──────────→ E
             |
             |
             ↓
             S
```

But the diagonal directions sit **exactly halfway** between them.

Therefore:

```text
                 N
                 ↑
            NW ↖ | ↗ NE
                 |
W ←──────────────●──────────────→ E
                 |
            SW ↙ | ↘ SE
                 ↓
                 S
```

So:

### 90° ÷ 2 = 45°

Therefore:

* N → NE = **45°**
* NE → E = **45°**
* E → SE = **45°**
* SE → S = **45°**
* S → SW = **45°**
* SW → W = **45°**
* W → NW = **45°**
* NW → N = **45°**

---

# 🔥 The easiest way to solve these

Don't calculate complicated angles.

Just use this direction circle:

```text
                    N
                    ↑
              NW ↖     ↗ NE
                  \     /
                   \   /
W ←─────────────────●─────────────────→ E
                   /   \
                  /     \
              SW ↙     ↘ SE
                    ↓
                    S
```

Then remember:

### Clockwise ↻

Move **one step to the right**:

**N → NE → E → SE → S → SW → W → NW → N**

### Anti-clockwise ↺

Move **one step to the left**:

**N → NW → W → SW → S → SE → E → NE → N**

# 🧠 Super important shortcut

For **45° questions**, memorize this:

| Starting | 45° Clockwise | 45° Anti-clockwise |
| -------- | ------------- | ------------------ |
| N        | NE            | NW                 |
| NE       | E             | N                  |
| E        | SE            | NE                 |
| SE       | S             | E                  |
| S        | SW            | SE                 |
| SW       | W             | S                  |
| W        | NW            | SW                 |
| NW       | N             | W                  |

You don't really need to memorize the whole table if you understand the circle.

### Just remember:

> **Clockwise → move one step clockwise**
>
> **Anti-clockwise → move one step anti-clockwise**

And **one step = 45°**.

---

## One more thing: don't confuse "45° clockwise" with "45° to East"

For example:

**Facing West + 45° clockwise**

❌ Don't think: "Clockwise means East."

Instead:

1. Start at **West**
2. Go clockwise
3. Move **45°**
4. You land at **NW**

That's the entire trick.

If you understand this, you're ready for the next level: **questions where they give 90°, 135°, 180°, 225° etc.** Those become ridiculously easy once we use the same circle.
