### Question

> One morning after sunrise, Anup Ji was standing facing a pole. The shadow of the pole fell exactly to his right. To which direction was he facing?

Options:

A. East
B. South
C. West
D. Data is inadequate

---

## Step 1: Look at the time 🌅

It says:

> **morning after sunrise**

Therefore:

**Sun = East**

---

## Step 2: Find the shadow ☀️

Shadow is always **opposite the Sun**.

Sun = East

Therefore:

**Shadow = West**

So we know:

> **The pole's shadow is towards West.**

---

## Step 3: The shadow is on Anup's RIGHT

The question says:

> "The shadow fell exactly to his right."

So:

**Right side of Anup = West**

Now ask:

### "If West is on my right, which direction am I facing?"

Look at our left/right table:

| Facing    | Left     | Right    |
| --------- | -------- | -------- |
| North     | West     | East     |
| **South** | **East** | **West** |
| East      | North    | South    |
| West      | South    | North    |

If **West = right**, the person must be facing:

# ✅ South

### Answer: **B. South**

---

## 🧠 The whole solution in 3 lines

```text
Morning
   ↓
Sun = East
   ↓
Shadow = West
   ↓
Shadow is on his RIGHT
   ↓
West is his RIGHT
   ↓
He is facing SOUTH
```

### 🔥 Important pattern to remember

If the **shadow is West**:

* Facing **North** → shadow is **Left**
* Facing **South** → shadow is **Right**

And because this is **morning**, shadow is West.

So here:

**Shadow = West + Shadow is Right → Facing South.** ✅
