Yep! This one adds **one extra concept**: **two people facing each other**. Once you handle that, it's easy. 🔥

## Question

> James and Henry are standing **facing each other at 8 AM**.
> Henry's shadow falls exactly to **his right**.
> Which direction is **James facing**?

Options:

* East
* South
* **North**
* Data inadequate

---

## Step 1: Look at the time 🌅

**8 AM = morning**

For these reasoning questions:

**Morning → Sun = East**

Therefore:

**Sun = East**

So the shadow is opposite:

### Shadow = West

---

## Step 2: Henry's shadow is on his RIGHT

So:

> **West is on Henry's right.**

Now ask:

### Which direction must Henry be facing if West is on his right?

Look at this:

```text
             North
               ↑
               |
West ←──────── 🧍 ────────→ East
               |
               ↓
             South
```

If a person faces **South**:

```text
               North
                 ↑

West ←──────── 🧍
               ↓
             South
```

When facing South:

**West = Right** ✅

Therefore:

### Henry is facing South.

---

# Step 3: James and Henry are facing each other

This is the important part.

If Henry is facing **South**, James must be facing the **opposite direction**.

Opposite of South = **North**.

```text
          James
            🧍
            ↓
          South
            ↑
            🧍
          Henry
```

Actually, let's visualize their faces:

```text
          JAMES
           🧍
           ↓
        facing South
           
           ↑
        facing North
           🧍
          HENRY
```

They're looking directly at each other.

Therefore:

# ✅ Answer = **North**

### **Option C**

---

## 🧠 The complete chain

Don't get confused by all the information. Just do this:

```text
8 AM
 ↓
Morning
 ↓
Sun = East
 ↓
Shadow = West
 ↓
Henry's RIGHT = West
 ↓
Henry faces South
 ↓
James faces opposite Henry
 ↓
Opposite of South = North
```

### ⭐ Final Answer: **C) North**

---

### 🔥 New rule to add to your notes

Whenever the question says:

> **A and B are facing each other**

their directions are always **opposite**.

So:

* A = North → B = South
* A = South → B = North
* A = East → B = West
* A = West → B = East

This is the extra trick this question is testing.
