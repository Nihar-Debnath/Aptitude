This is a **trick question**. 😄 The key is that they haven't told us **what time it is**.

### Question

> If a man stands **in front of the Sun**, what is the first letter of the direction which is **left to him**?

Options:

* N
* S
* W
* Cannot be determined

---

## Step 1: "In front of the Sun"

This means the man is **facing the Sun**.

But where is the Sun?

That depends on the **time of day**.

### 🌅 Morning

Sun = **East**

So the man faces **East**.

If you're facing East:

```text
             North
               ↑
               |
               🧍 → East
               |
               ↓
             South
```

Your **left = North**.

So answer would be:

**N**

---

### 🌇 Evening

Sun = **West**

So the man faces **West**.

If you're facing West:

```text
             North
               ↑
               |
               🧍 ← West
               |
               ↓
             South
```

Your **left = South**.

So answer would be:

**S**

---

## Therefore...

The question doesn't tell us whether it's **morning or evening**.

So:

* Morning → left = **North**
* Evening → left = **South**

Both are possible.

# ✅ Answer: D) Cannot be determined

---

### 🧠 The important lesson

Whenever you see:

> **"A person is facing the Sun"**

Immediately ask:

> **"What time is it?"**

Because:

**Morning → Sun East → person faces East → left = North**

**Evening → Sun West → person faces West → left = South**

So **without time, you cannot determine the answer.**
