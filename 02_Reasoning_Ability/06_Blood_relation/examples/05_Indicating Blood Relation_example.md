Yes! ✅ This is exactly an **Indicating Blood Relation (Pointing Question).**

These questions look difficult because of the long sentence, but there's a **fixed method** that works every time.

---

# The Question

> Pointing to a photograph, Asha said,
>
> **"His mother's only daughter is my mother."**
>
> How is Asha related to the man in the photograph?

---

# Golden Rule ⭐

In pointing questions:

> **Start from the person being pointed at (His)** and move step by step.

Don't read the whole sentence at once.

---

# Step 1

The word **His** refers to the **man in the photograph**.

Let's call him **X**.

```text
      X (Man in Photograph)
```

---

# Step 2

> **His mother**

Move one step up.

```text
        Mother
          │
          X
```

---

# Step 3

> **His mother's only daughter**

The mother has **only one daughter**.

That daughter is...

```text
        Mother
          │
          │
     Only Daughter
```

The sentence says:

> **His mother's only daughter is my mother.**

So replace **Only Daughter** with **Asha's Mother**.

```text
          Grandmother
               │
        ┌──────┴──────┐
   X (♂)      Asha's Mother (♀)
```

Wait...

Since **Asha's mother** is the **only daughter**, **X must be her brother** (the son).

---

# Step 4

Now place Asha.

```text
          Grandmother
               │
        ┌──────┴──────┐
   X (♂)      Asha's Mother (♀)
                    │
                 Asha
```

---

# Final Question

How is **Asha** related to **X**?

X is Asha's **mother's brother**.

So X is Asha's **maternal uncle**.

Therefore,

👉 **Asha is X's niece.**

✅ **Answer: D) Niece**

---

# The Shortcut Method (Very Important)

Whenever you see a pointing question, don't panic.

Break it like this:

```
His
↓
His Mother
↓
His Mother's Only Daughter
↓
My Mother
↓
Me
```

You're simply walking through the family tree one relation at a time.

---

# Another Example

> "His father is my grandfather."

```
His
↓
Father
↓
My Grandfather
↓
My Father
↓
Me
```

Diagram:

```text
      Grandfather
      │
 ┌────┴────┐
His Father  My Father
                │
               Me
```

So **I and the man are cousins.**

---

# The 5-Step Formula for Every Indicating Question

Whenever you see words like:

* Pointing to...
* Looking at...
* Showing a photograph...
* Referring to...

Follow this order:

1. **Identify the reference person** (`His`, `Her`, `This man`, `That woman`).
2. **Draw only one relation at a time.**
3. **Replace phrases** like "my mother", "my father" with actual people in your diagram.
4. **Place yourself (speaker) at the end.**
5. **Answer from the speaker to the pointed person.**

---

This type of question becomes much easier after solving 8–10 examples because almost all of them follow the same pattern. We can practice them one by one until you can solve each in under 30 seconds.
