# Paternal = Father's Side 👨

Anything related to your **father's family** is called **paternal**.

```text
        Grandfather
        Grandmother
             │
    -----------------
    │               │
 Father         Father's Brother
    │               │
  You          Paternal Uncle
```

Examples:

* **Paternal grandfather** = Father's father
* **Paternal grandmother** = Father's mother
* **Paternal uncle** = Father's brother
* **Paternal aunt** = Father's sister

---

# Maternal = Mother's Side 👩

Anything related to your **mother's family** is called **maternal**.

```text
        Grandfather
        Grandmother
             │
    -----------------
    │               │
 Mother      Mother's Brother
    │
   You
```

Examples:

* **Maternal grandfather** = Mother's father
* **Maternal grandmother** = Mother's mother
* **Maternal uncle** = Mother's brother
* **Maternal aunt** = Mother's sister

---

# Easy Trick to Remember

* **P**aternal → **P**apa → Father's side 👨
* **M**aternal → **M**om → Mother's side 👩

### Example Questions

**Q1.** Who is your **maternal uncle**?

* ✅ Your **mother's brother**.

**Q2.** Who is your **paternal aunt**?

* ✅ Your **father's sister**.

**Q3.** Your father's father's wife is your...

* Father's father = Grandfather
* His wife = **Paternal grandmother**.
