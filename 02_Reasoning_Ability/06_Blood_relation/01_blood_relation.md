Blood Relation is one of the most common topics in **Reasoning Ability**. It tests your ability to identify relationships between family members based on given statements.

Think of it like solving a **family tree puzzle**.

---

# Types of Blood Relation Questions

## 1. Direct Blood Relation

The relationship is given directly.

**Example:**

> Ravi is the son of Mohan.

Question:

> How is Mohan related to Ravi?

Answer:

> Father.

---

## 2. Indirect Blood Relation

You need to connect multiple statements.

**Example:**

> A is the sister of B.
>
> B is the son of C.

Question:

> How is C related to A?

Let's draw it.

```text
      C
      |
      B ---- A
     Son   Sister
```

Since B is C's son,
A is also C's child.

Answer:

> Mother or Father (cannot be determined)

---

## 3. Coded Blood Relation

Relationships are represented by symbols.

Example:

> A + B → A is father of B
>
> A - B → A is sister of B

Question:

> P + Q - R

Means

```text
P
|
Q ---- R
```

P is father of Q

Q is sister of R

So P is also father of R.

---

## 4. Pointing or Introducing Questions

Someone points to another person.

Example:

> Pointing to a lady, Rahul says,
> "She is the daughter of my mother's only son."

Let's solve.

"My mother's only son"

↓

Rahul himself

↓

His daughter

So the lady is Rahul's daughter.

---

## 5. Family Tree Questions

You'll get a complete family and answer questions.

Example

```text
        Grandfather
        Grandmother
             |
      ----------------
      |              |
    Father        Uncle
      |
   ----------
   |        |
 You      Sister
```

Questions may ask:

* Who is your uncle?
* Who is your grandmother?
* How many females?
* How many generations?

# Tricks to Solve Blood Relation Questions

1. **Draw a family tree** instead of trying to solve it mentally.
2. **Go one statement at a time.** Don't jump ahead.
3. **Mark genders immediately** whenever they are given.
4. **Use symbols** while solving:

   * ♂ = Male
   * ♀ = Female
   * ─ = Sibling
   * │ = Parent → Child
5. If gender is **not mentioned**, don't assume it.

For exams like **TCS Ignite, TCS Smart, Cognizant GenC, Infosys, Wipro, Capgemini, Accenture, and banking exams (IBPS, SBI)**, you'll usually see:

* ✅ Finding the relationship between two people

* ✅ Family tree questions
* ✅ Mixed reasoning questions combining blood relations with directions or seating arrangements

## Example

Question:

> A is the brother of B.
> B is the daughter of C.
> C is the wife of D.
> E is the son of D.

Find the relation of **E to A**.

### Step 1: Draw the family tree

```text
        D (♂)
          │
        C (♀)
          │
     ┌────┴────┐
     B (♀)    A (♂)
       │
      (Sibling)

     E (♂) is son of D
```

Since B and A are siblings and E is also the son of D, E is another child of D.

```text
        D
        │
   ┌────┼────┐
  A     B     E
```

**Answer:** **E is A's brother.**
