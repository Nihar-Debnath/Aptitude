This one is a little different, but the key is **understanding the exact meaning of “hope” and “aspires.”** Let's go slowly.

## Given

**HOPE : ASPIRES :: ?**

### Step 1: Understand the words

### 🔵 Hope

**Hope** = to want something to happen or to believe something good may happen.

Example:

> "I **hope** I get the job."

### 🟣 Aspires

**Aspires** = strongly **desires/aims for something**, usually a goal.

Example:

> "He **aspires** to become a software engineer."

So both are related to **wanting/aiming for something**.

The important relationship is:

> **HOPE → ASPIRES**
> Both express a **desire/aim for something**.

Now find the option where the two words have a similar relationship.

---

## Check the options

### A. Love : Elevates ✅

**Love** = strong affection/care.

**Elevates** = raises something to a higher level; can also mean **uplifts**.

Love can **elevate/uplift** a person.

So:

> **Love → Elevates/uplifts**

This is a meaningful relationship, but let's compare the other options.

---

### B. Film : Flam ❌

**Film** and **flam** don't have a meaningful relationship.

❌

---

### C. Fib : Lie ✅/❌

This one is tempting!

**Fib** = a small lie.

**Lie** = a false statement.

So:

> Fib = a type of lie.

That's a **category/type relationship**, not the same relationship as Hope → Aspires.

❌

---

### D. Fake : Ordinary ❌

**Fake** = not genuine.

**Ordinary** = normal/common.

These aren't related in the required way.

❌

---

# 🎯 Answer: **A. Love : Elevates**

The intended relationship is:

> **Hope → Aspires**
> **Love → Elevates**

Both involve the **effect/quality associated with the first word**.

### ⚠️ One important point

This is a **poorly worded analogy** compared with the previous questions. "Hope" and "aspires" aren't a clean grammatical pair because **hope** is a noun/verb while **aspires** is a verb.

But among the given choices, **A is the intended answer**.

### Final answer: **A. Love : Elevates** ✅
