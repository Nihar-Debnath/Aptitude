Yes — this is a **Verbal Analogy** question. The key is to first identify the **exact relationship** in the given pair.

### Given:

**Children : Pediatrician**

Ask yourself:

> **Who is a Pediatrician?**

A **Pediatrician is a doctor who treats children.**

So the relationship is:

**Person/group → Specialist who treats them**

Now check each option.

### A. Adult : Orthopaedist ❌

An orthopaedist treats **bones, joints, muscles**, not specifically adults.

### B. Females : Gynaecologist ✅

A **gynaecologist is a doctor who specializes in the female reproductive system and women's health.**

So:

**Children → Pediatrician**
**Females → Gynaecologist**

Same relationship.

### C. Kidney : Nephrologist ❌

A nephrologist treats **kidney-related diseases**.

Here the first word is an **organ**, while "Children" is a **group of people**.

### D. Skin : Dermatologist ❌

A dermatologist treats **skin**, again an organ/body part rather than a group of people.

## ✅ Answer: **B. Females : Gynaecologist**

### 🧠 The trick to remember

Don't just look for:

> **Who treats what?**

Look at the **type of relationship**:

**Children** = group of people
**Pediatrician** = specialist who treats that group

Therefore look for:

**Group of people → specialist for that group**

→ **Females → Gynaecologist**.
