This one is a **“small units combine to make a larger thing”** analogy. This is actually a very useful type to recognize. 🔥

## Given:

**Cells : Tissues :: Atoms : ?**

First, understand the relationship.

### 🧬 Cells → Tissues

A **cell** is a small/basic unit.

Many cells combine together to form a **tissue**.

For example:

> Cells → Tissue → Organ → Organ system

So:

**Cells are the building blocks of tissues.**

---

Now look at:

### ⚛️ Atoms → ?

An **atom** is a very small/basic unit of matter.

When atoms combine, they form **molecules**.

For example:

> Hydrogen atom + Oxygen atom → Water molecule (H₂O)

So:

**Atoms are the building blocks of molecules.**

Therefore:

> **Cells : Tissues :: Atoms : Molecules**

# ✅ Answer: **B. Molecules**

---

### Let's eliminate the others

**A. Elements ❌**

An element is a type of substance defined by its atoms. The relationship isn't the same.

**B. Molecules ✅**

Atoms combine to form molecules.

**C. Resistance ❌**

Completely unrelated.

**D. Diseases ❌**

Unrelated.

---

### 🧠 The pattern to remember

This is:

> **SMALL UNIT → LARGER STRUCTURE MADE FROM IT**

Examples:

**Cells → Tissues**
**Atoms → Molecules**

So whenever you see this kind of analogy, ask:

> **“What is the second thing made from the first thing?”**

And here:

**What are molecules made from? → Atoms.**

### 🎯 Final answer: **B. MOLECULES** ✅
