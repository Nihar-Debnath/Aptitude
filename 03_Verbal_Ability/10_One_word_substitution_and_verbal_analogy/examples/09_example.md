This one is a **person → thing they study/collect/work with** analogy. Let's break down the unfamiliar words first.

## Given

**Numismatist : Coins :: ?**

### 1. What is a Numismatist?

A **numismatist** is a person who **collects or studies coins and currency**.

So:

> **Numismatist → Coins**
> Person → thing they collect/study

Now we need another pair with the **same relationship**.

---

## Check the options

### A. Jeweller : Jewels ❌

**Jeweller** = a person who makes, sells, or works with jewellery.

**Jewels** = precious stones/jewellery.

This is somewhat related, but the relationship isn't as exact as the given one.

---

### B. Cartographer : Maps ❌

**Cartographer** = a person who **makes/draws maps**.

> Cartographer → Maps

This is also person → thing they work with.

But **numismatist specifically studies/collects coins**, so let's keep checking.

---

### C. Philatelist : Stamps ✅

**Philatelist** = a person who **collects or studies postage stamps**.

So:

> **Numismatist → Coins**
> **Philatelist → Stamps**

🔥 **Exactly the same relationship!**

---

### D. Geneticist : Chromosomes ❌

**Geneticist** = a scientist who studies genetics/heredity.

They can study chromosomes, but chromosomes aren't specifically what defines a geneticist.

The relationship is less direct.

---

# ✅ Final Answer: **C. Philatelist : Stamps**

### 🧠 The important vocabulary

| Word             | Meaning                                |
| ---------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| **Numismatist**  | Person who collects/studies **coins**  |
| **Philatelist**  | Person who collects/studies **stamps** |
| **Cartographer** | Person who makes **maps**              |
| **Geneticist**   | Scientist who studies **genetics**     |
| **Jeweller**     | Person who makes/sells **jewellery**   |

### The pattern to remember:

**PERSON → WHAT THEY COLLECT/STUDY**

> **Numismatist → Coins**
> **Philatelist → Stamps** ✅

So the answer is **C**.
