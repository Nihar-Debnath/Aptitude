## Question

**Unemployed : Worker**

We need to understand **what connects these two words**.

### Step 1: Start with the second word

**Worker** means:

> A person who **works**.

Now what does **unemployed** mean?

> A person who **doesn't have a job / isn't working**.

So we can say:

**Worker → works**
**Unemployed → doesn't work**

That's the relationship.

---

## Step 2: Now look at the options

We need to find another pair where:

> **Something normally gets used/done → but it is NOT being used/done**

### Option A: **Fallow : Land**

First understand **land**.

Land is normally:

> **cultivated / used for farming**

But **fallow land** means:

> Land that is **not being cultivated**.

So:

**Land → cultivated**
**Fallow → not cultivated**

Look at the similarity:

**Worker → works**
**Unemployed → doesn't work**

**Land → cultivated**
**Fallow → not cultivated**

🔥 **Same relationship!**

Therefore:

### ✅ A. Fallow : Land

## Why NOT C?

**Wit : Jester**

What is **wit**?

> Cleverness or the ability to make clever/funny remarks.

What is a **jester**?

### 🧠 The easiest way to solve analogy

Whenever you see:

> **A : B**

Don't immediately look at the words individually.

Make a tiny sentence:

> **B is normally ______, but A means ______.**

For this:

> **Worker is normally working, but unemployed means not working.**

Then try the options:

> **Land is normally cultivated, but fallow means not cultivated.** ✅

That's why **A**.
