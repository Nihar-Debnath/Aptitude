Yep! This one is much easier once we identify the **relationship**. Let's do it slowly.

## Question

**Automobile : Petrol :: ?**

Options:

A. Fire : Fuel
B. Plane : Propeller
C. Diesel : Gas
D. Man : Food

---

### Step 1: What is the relationship?

An **automobile** needs **petrol** to run.

So:

> **Automobile → Petrol**
> A vehicle **uses petrol as fuel**.

Now we need an option with the same relationship:

> **_____ → _____**
> The first thing **uses the second thing as fuel/energy**.

---

### A. Fire : Fuel ❌

Fire uses/burns fuel, so this sounds somewhat related.

But notice the direction:

**Automobile → Petrol**
= an automobile **uses petrol as its fuel**.

**Fire → Fuel**
= fire burns fuel.

This is close, but not the intended relationship.

---

### B. Plane : Propeller ❌

A plane can have a propeller, but:

> Propeller is a **part/component** of some planes.

It isn't the fuel.

❌ Different relationship.

---

### C. Diesel : Gas ❌

Diesel and gas are both types of fuel.

There is no **vehicle → fuel** relationship.

❌

---

### D. Man : Food ✅

A **man/person needs food for energy**.

So:

> **Automobile → Petrol**
> Vehicle needs fuel to run.

and

> **Man → Food**
> Human needs food for energy.

🔥 Same relationship!

# ✅ Answer: **D. Man : Food**

### Remember this analogy type:

**THING → WHAT IT NEEDS TO RUN/LIVE**

* Automobile → Petrol 🚗
* Man → Food 👨

So whenever you see:

> **Automobile : Petrol**

think:

> **"What does the first thing need/use to function?"**

Then look for the same relationship.
