This one is a **“field of study → what it studies”** analogy. Once you know the two words, it's easy. Let's break it down. 👇

## Given

**Penology : Punishment :: Seismology : ?**

### Step 1: Understand **Penology**

**Penology** = the study of **punishment and prisons**, especially how criminals are punished and rehabilitated.

So:

> **Penology → Punishment**

It is the **study of punishment**.

---

### Step 2: Understand **Seismology**

**Seismology** = the scientific study of **earthquakes and seismic waves**.

So:

> **Seismology → Earthquakes**

Therefore:

**Penology : Punishment**
**Seismology : Earthquakes**

# ✅ Answer: **C. Earthquakes**

---

### Why the other options are wrong?

**A. Law ❌**
Penology is related to law, but it specifically studies **punishment**, not law itself.

**B. Liver ❌**
That's a body organ. Seismology has nothing to do with it.

**C. Earthquakes ✅**
Seismology = study of earthquakes.

**D. Medicine ❌**
Medicine has nothing to do with seismology.

---

## 🧠 Remember these two words

| Word           | Meaning                         |
| -------------- | ------------------------------- |
| **Penology**   | Study of **punishment/prisons** |
| **Seismology** | Study of **earthquakes**        |

### The pattern:

> **FIELD OF STUDY → SUBJECT IT STUDIES**

**Penology → Punishment**
**Seismology → Earthquakes** ✅

### 🎯 Final answer: **C. Earthquakes**
