This one is a **CREATION → CREATOR** analogy. Once you see that, it's very easy. 😄

## Given

**SYMPHONY : COMPOSER :: ?**

### Step 1: What is a Symphony?

A **symphony** is a large piece of **music** written/composed for an orchestra.

Who creates a symphony?

👉 A **composer**.

So:

> **Symphony → Composer**
> **Creation → Person who creates it**

Now we need another pair with exactly the same relationship.

---

## Check the options

### A. Leonardo da Vinci : Music ❌

Leonardo da Vinci = a **person**
Music = a **creation/type of art**

This is:

> **Creator → Creation**

But our question is:

> **Creation → Creator**

So the direction is reversed.

❌

---

### B. Fresco : Painter ✅

A **fresco** is a type of painting made by applying paint/plaster to a wall or ceiling.

Who creates a fresco?

👉 A **painter**.

So:

> **Fresco → Painter**
> **Creation → Creator**

Exactly the same as:

> **Symphony → Composer**
> **Creation → Creator**

✅ **Correct!**

---

### C. Colors : Palette ❌

A **palette** is a surface/container used by an artist to hold and mix colors.

This is more like:

> **Colors → tool/container used for them**

Not creation → creator.

❌

---

### D. Art : Appreciation ❌

**Appreciation** means understanding/enjoying art.

Not creator relationship.

❌

# 🎯 Final Answer: **B. Fresco : Painter** ✅

### 🧠 Remember the pattern

**THING CREATED → PERSON WHO CREATES IT**

* **Symphony → Composer** 🎵
* **Fresco → Painter** 🎨

So whenever you see an analogy, first ask:

> **"What exactly is the relationship, and which direction is it going?"**

Here:

**CREATION → CREATOR**

That's why **B** is correct.
