Yes! This one is a **“living thing/person → place where it lives/is kept”** analogy. Let's make it very simple. 👇

## Question

> **Rabbit : Burrow :: Lunatic : ?**

First, understand the two important words.

### 🐇 Rabbit → Burrow

A **burrow** is a hole or underground shelter where a rabbit lives.

So:

> **Rabbit → Burrow**
> = **animal → place where it lives**

Now we need the same relationship for **Lunatic**.

---

## What does "Lunatic" mean?

**Lunatic** is an old-fashioned word for a **person who is mentally ill**.

You may see this word in aptitude questions, but in normal modern English, **"person with mental illness"** is more appropriate.

Now ask:

> Where would a person with severe mental illness traditionally be kept/treated?

### **Asylum**

An **asylum** is an institution/shelter where people needing care, historically including people with severe mental illness, were housed.

So:

> **Lunatic → Asylum**

---

## Check the options

### A. Prison ❌

A prison is for people who are legally imprisoned.

**Lunatic ≠ prisoner**

### B. Cell ❌

A cell is a small room, especially in a prison.

Not the intended relationship.

### C. Barrack ❌

A barrack is a building where soldiers live.

**Barrack → soldiers**

### D. Asylum ✅

An asylum was traditionally a place where people with severe mental illness were housed/treated.

---

# 🎯 Answer: **D. Asylum** ✅

### 🧠 The relationship

> **Rabbit : Burrow**
> **Animal → place where it lives**

> **Lunatic : Asylum**
> **Person → place where they are traditionally housed/treated**

So the trick is:

**WHO → WHERE THEY LIVE / ARE HOUSED**

### Final answer: **D. ASYLUM** ✅
