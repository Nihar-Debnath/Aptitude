Absolutely. This question looks scary because of the vocabulary, but the **relationship is actually very simple**. Let's build it from zero. ❤️

## Question

**CORPOREAL : SPIRITUAL :: ?**

Options:

A. Mesa : Plateau
B. Moron : Savant
C. Foreigner : Immigrant
D. Pedagogue : Teacher

We need to find a pair that has the **same relationship** as:

> **CORPOREAL → SPIRITUAL**

---

# Step 1: Understand the two difficult words

### 🔵 Corporeal

**Corporeal = related to the physical body / something physical**

Example:

> Your hands, legs, eyes, etc. are **corporeal** things.

Think:

**Corporeal = BODY / PHYSICAL**

---

### 🟣 Spiritual

**Spiritual = related to the spirit, soul, or non-physical side of life**

Think:

**Spiritual = SOUL / NON-PHYSICAL**

So:

> **Corporeal = physical**
> **Spiritual = non-physical**

They are basically **opposites**.

### Therefore:

**CORPOREAL : SPIRITUAL**

means:

> **PHYSICAL : NON-PHYSICAL**

So we need an option where the two words are **opposites**.

---

# Step 2: Check every option

### A. Mesa : Plateau ❌

**Mesa** = a flat-topped elevated area of land.

**Plateau** = a raised area of land with a relatively flat top.

They are **similar things**, not opposites.

❌ Not correct.

---

### B. Moron : Savant ✅

This is the important one.

#### Moron

**Moron = a very foolish or unintelligent person**

Think:

> **Moron = stupid person**

#### Savant

**Savant = a highly knowledgeable or exceptionally skilled person**, especially in a particular field.

Think:

> **Savant = extremely intelligent/knowledgeable person**

So:

**Moron → unintelligent**

**Savant → highly intelligent/knowledgeable**

They represent **opposite ends**.

🔥 Exactly like:

**Corporeal → physical**

**Spiritual → non-physical**

Therefore:

### ✅ **B. Moron : Savant**

---

### C. Foreigner : Immigrant ❌

**Foreigner** = a person from another country.

**Immigrant** = someone who comes to another country to live there.

These aren't opposites.

They can actually describe the **same person**.

❌ Not correct.

---

### D. Pedagogue : Teacher ❌

**Pedagogue** = a teacher/educator.

**Teacher** = someone who teaches.

So these are basically **synonyms** (words with similar meanings).

❌ Not correct.

---

# 🎯 Final Answer

## **B. Moron : Savant** ✅

Because:

| First word    | Second word   | Relationship |
| ------------- | ------------- | ------------ |
| **Corporeal** | **Spiritual** | Opposites    |
| **Moron**     | **Savant**    | Opposites    |

### The core trick:

Don't try to understand the entire sentence.

First ask:

> **"What is the relationship between the two given words?"**

Here:

**CORPOREAL = physical**
**SPIRITUAL = non-physical**

➡️ **Opposite meanings**

Then find the option with **opposite meanings**.

**Moron ↔ Savant** ✅

---

### 🧠 Save this vocabulary

* **Corporeal** = physical / relating to body
* **Spiritual** = relating to spirit/soul
* **Moron** = very foolish/unintelligent person
* **Savant** = highly knowledgeable/skilled person
* **Mesa** = flat-topped elevated landform
* **Plateau** = elevated flat area of land
* **Foreigner** = person from another country
* **Immigrant** = person who moves to another country to live
* **Pedagogue** = teacher/educator

And the **analogy relationship** here is:

> **CORPOREAL : SPIRITUAL = OPPOSITES**
> **MORON : SAVANT = OPPOSITES**

So **B** is the answer. ✅
