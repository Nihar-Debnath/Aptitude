Ahhh, this one is a **different type of analogy**. The trick is actually very simple once you spot it. 😄

### Given:

**Parts : Strap :: Wolf : ?**

Look at the first pair:

**PARTS → STRAP**

They contain **exactly the same letters**:

* PARTS = P, A, R, T, S
* STRAP = S, T, R, A, P

They're simply **anagrams** — the letters are rearranged.

Now do the same for **WOLF**.

Options:

* A. Fox ❌ — different letters
* B. Animal ❌ — different letters
* C. Wood ❌ — different letters
* **D. Flow ✅** — F, L, O, W are exactly the same letters as WOLF

So:

> **PARTS → STRAP** = letters rearranged
> **WOLF → FLOW** = letters rearranged

### ✅ Answer: **D. FLOW**

🧠 **Remember this type:**

Whenever you see an analogy like this, check whether the two words have **the same letters in a different order**.

**PARTS ↔ STRAP**
**WOLF ↔ FLOW**

That's the entire trick.
