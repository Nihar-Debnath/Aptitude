The answer is **D. Rainfall** ✅

Let's understand the analogy carefully:

### Given:

**Disease : Medicine :: Famine : ?**

Think:

* **Disease → Medicine**
  Medicine is used to **treat/cure** a disease.

Now apply the same relationship:

* **Famine → Rainfall**
  Adequate rainfall helps **end/relieve famine** by producing water and crops.

So:

> **Disease is treated by Medicine**
> **Famine is relieved by Rainfall**

### Why NOT the others?

* ❌ **Drought** → Drought is actually a **cause of famine**.
* ❌ **River** → A river doesn't directly represent the normal remedy for famine.
* ❌ **Waterfall** → Not the relevant solution.
* ✅ **Rainfall** → Provides water needed for agriculture and helps prevent/end famine.

### 🧠 Quick analogy trick

Ask: **"What is done to the first word?"**

**Disease → Medicine** = *medicine treats disease*
**Famine → Rainfall** = *rainfall relieves famine*

🔥 **Final answer: D. Rainfall**
