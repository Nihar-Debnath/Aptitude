This one is actually **very straightforward once we understand “sedative.”** Let's go step by step. 👇

## Given

**SEDATIVE : SLEEP :: ?**

### Step 1: What does "Sedative" mean?

**Sedative** = a medicine/substance that **calms you or makes you sleepy**.

So the relationship is:

> **Sedative → produces/causes sleep**

We need an option where:

> **First word → produces/causes/is associated with the second word**

---

## Check the options

### A. Challenging : Knowledge ❌

**Challenging** = difficult.

It doesn't directly produce knowledge.

❌

---

### B. Mnemonic : Memory ✅

This is the one.

**Mnemonic** = a technique, word, phrase, or device that **helps you remember something**.

So:

> **Mnemonic → helps memory**

Just like:

> **Sedative → helps/causes sleep**

✅ Same relationship.

---

### C. Dramatic : Story ❌

**Dramatic** describes something exciting/emotional.

A story can be dramatic, but dramatic doesn't **produce** a story.

❌

---

### D. Vocal : Eloquence ❌

**Vocal** = related to the voice/speech.

**Eloquence** = the ability to express ideas beautifully and effectively.

They are related, but the relationship isn't the same.

❌

---

# ✅ Answer: **B. Mnemonic : Memory**

### 🧠 Remember these two words

**Sedative** → something that makes you **sleepy/calm**

**Mnemonic** → something that helps you **remember**

So:

> **SEDATIVE : SLEEP**
> **MNEMONIC : MEMORY** ✅

The analogy relationship is basically:

**THING/DEVICE → EFFECT OR PURPOSE**

And that's why **B** is correct.
