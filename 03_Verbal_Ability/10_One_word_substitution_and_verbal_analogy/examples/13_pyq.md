Let's solve it from the **relationship**, not by guessing. This one is a little tricky. 👇

## Given

**Angle : Radian :: ?**

First, understand these two words.

### 🔵 Angle

An **angle** is a measurement of how much two lines/rays are turned apart.

Angles can be measured in different units, such as:

* Degrees
* **Radians**

### 🟣 Radian

A **radian is a unit used to measure an angle.**

So the relationship is:

> **Angle → Radian**
> **Radian is a unit of measurement for an angle.**

Therefore, we need an option where:

> **First word → second word = second is a unit used to measure the first.**

---

## Now check the options

### A. Cattle : Herd ❌

**Cattle** = cows/bulls etc.

**Herd** = a group of cattle.

So:

> Cattle → Herd = **individual/group relationship**

Not measurement.

❌

---

### B. Odometer : Speed ❌

An **odometer** measures **distance**, not speed.

A **speedometer** measures speed.

So this is actually incorrect even before considering the analogy.

❌

---

### C. Dearth : Scarcity ❌

**Dearth** = scarcity / shortage.

**Scarcity** = shortage.

These two words have **similar meanings**.

> Dearth ≈ Scarcity

That's a **synonym relationship**, not measurement.

❌

---

### D. Area : Hectares ✅

This is the one!

**Area** can be measured in **hectares**.

For example:

> A farm has an area of **5 hectares**.

So:

> **Angle → Radian**
> **Area → Hectare**

In both:

**The second word is a unit used to measure the first.**

# ✅ Answer: **D. Area : Hectares**

---

## 🧠 Save this pattern

This is a **MEASUREMENT UNIT analogy**.

### Given:

**Angle : Radian**

Ask:

> "What is the second word doing?"

**Radian = unit for measuring angle**

Then:

**Area : Hectare**

> Hectare = unit for measuring area.

### 🔥 Final:

**ANGLE : RADIAN :: AREA : HECTARES** ✅

**Answer = D**.
