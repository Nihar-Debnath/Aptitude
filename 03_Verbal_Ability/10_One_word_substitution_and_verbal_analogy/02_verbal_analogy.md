## 🔹 What is Verbal Analogy?

**Analogy means a relationship between two words.**

In verbal ability, you are given:

> **A : B :: C : ?**

Read it as:

> **A is related to B in the same way that C is related to ?**

Your job is to find the word that has the **same relationship**.

### Example 1

**Hot : Cold :: Day : ?**

Relationship:

> Hot ↔ Cold = **opposites**

Therefore:

> Day ↔ **Night**

✅ **Answer: Night**

---

### Example 2

**Doctor : Hospital :: Teacher : ?**

Relationship:

> Doctor works in → Hospital

Therefore:

> Teacher works in → **School**

✅ **Answer: School**

---

### Example 3

**Bird : Nest :: Lion : ?**

Relationship:

> Bird lives in → Nest

Therefore:

> Lion lives in → **Den**

✅ **Answer: Den**

---

## 🔥 How this connects to One Word Substitution

In **One Word Substitution**, you learn:

> **Meaning/phrase → Word**

Example:

> One who loves mankind → **Philanthropist**

In **Verbal Analogy**, you use that vocabulary to identify a **relationship**.

For example:

> **Philanthropist : Humanity :: Misanthrope : ?**

You know:

* **Philanthropist** → loves humanity
* **Misanthrope** → hates humanity

So:

**Philanthropist : Humanity :: Misanthrope : Humanity**

The important thing is to identify **what connects the first pair** and apply the **same connection** to the second pair.

### 🧠 Your solving formula

**A : B :: C : ?**

1. Find the relationship between **A → B**
2. Apply the **same relationship** to **C**
3. Find the answer

Think:

> **A is to B as C is to ?**

That's the core of **Verbal Analogy**.
