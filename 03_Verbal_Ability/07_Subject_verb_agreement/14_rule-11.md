# ✅ Rule 11: Uncountable Nouns Take Singular Verbs

## What is an Uncountable Noun?

An **uncountable noun** is something you **cannot count individually**.

You cannot say:

❌ One information

❌ Two informations

❌ Three advices

These words don't have a plural form.

Therefore, they always take a **singular verb**.

---

# Rule

```text
Uncountable Noun
        ↓
Singular Verb
```

---

# Common Uncountable Nouns (Very Important)

### Information

✅ The information **is** correct.

❌ The information **are** correct.

---

### Advice

✅ His advice **was** helpful.

❌ His advice **were** helpful.

---

### News

This one confuses everyone because it ends in **-s**.

But:

**News is singular.**

✅ The news **is** shocking.

❌ The news **are** shocking.

---

### Furniture

✅ The furniture **is** expensive.

❌ The furniture **are** expensive.

---

### Equipment

✅ The equipment **has** arrived.

❌ The equipment **have** arrived.

---

### Luggage

✅ My luggage **is** heavy.

❌ My luggage **are** heavy.

# Why?

Take this sentence:

> **The furniture is beautiful.**

Ask:

Can you count furniture?

Can you say:

* One furniture ❌
* Two furnitures ❌

No.

You count the **items**, not the word **furniture**.

Example:

* Three **chairs** ✅
* Two **tables** ✅

But together:

* The **furniture is** beautiful.

---

# Another Example

### Information

Wrong:

❌ He gave me many informations.

Correct:

✅ He gave me a lot of information.

Or

✅ He gave me three pieces of information.

Notice:

We count **pieces**, not **information**.

# Placement Trap 1 🚨

### News

Many students think:

News ends with **s**.

So:

**are**

Wrong.

**News** is singular.

✅ The news **is** good.

# Placement Trap 2 🚨

### Mathematics

Ends with **s**.

Still singular.

✅ Mathematics **is** my favorite subject.

The same for:

* Physics **is**
* Economics **is**
* Statistics **is** *(when referring to the subject)*

---

# Placement Trap 3 🚨

### Money

You may have ₹1000.

Still:

✅ Money **is** important.

Not

❌ Money are important.

---

# Difference

### Countable

* Book → Books
* Student → Students
* Chair → Chairs

Plural verbs:

* Books **are**
* Students **have**

---

### Uncountable

* Information
* Furniture
* Advice
* Equipment

Always singular:

* Information **is**
* Furniture **is**
* Advice **was**
* Equipment **has**

---

# Memory Trick 🧠

Ask yourself:

> **Can I say "one ___, two ___, three ___"?**

If **YES** ✅

Countable.

Example:

One book

Two books

Three books

---

If **NO** ❌

Uncountable.

Example:

One information ❌

Two informations ❌

Therefore:

Singular verb.

# ⭐ One Extra Rule (Frequently Asked)

Sometimes you need to count an uncountable noun. In that case, use a **unit of measurement**.

Examples:

* **a piece of information**
* **two pieces of advice**
* **three items of furniture**
* **a glass of water**
* **two kilos of rice**

Notice what changes:

* **Pieces** are countable.
* **Information** remains uncountable.

Example:

* **One piece of information is** enough.
* **Two pieces of information are** enough.

The verb now agrees with **pieces**, not **information**.

---

This is the last major SVA rule that trips up placement candidates. After this, Rule 12 (amounts of time, money, distance, etc.) is much shorter and easier.
