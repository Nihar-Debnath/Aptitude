Excellent question. This is actually one of the **most important topics in English grammar**.

There are **not only "have" verbs**. English has **three Primary Auxiliary (Helping) Verbs**.

1. **Be**
2. **Have**
3. **Do**

Then there are **Modal Verbs** like can, could, will, would, shall, should, may, might, must, etc.

So the complete picture is:

```
English Verbs
│
├── Main Verbs (eat, play, study, write...)
│
├── Primary Auxiliary Verbs
│     ├── Be
│     ├── Have
│     └── Do
│
└── Modal Verbs
      ├── can
      ├── could
      ├── will
      ├── would
      ├── shall
      ├── should
      ├── may
      ├── might
      ├── must
      └── ought to
```

We'll learn them one by one.

# 1. BE Verb

The **be verb** has **8 forms**.

```
be
├── am
├── is
├── are
├── was
├── were
├── being
└── been
```

"Be" is the base form.

# BE vs HAVE

### BE

Shows

* state
* existence
* identity
* location
* continuous tense
* passive voice

Examples

I am happy.

She is a teacher.

They are in school.

He is running.

The work was completed.

---

### HAVE

Shows

* possession
* ownership
* perfect tenses

Examples

I have a car.

She has money.

They have finished.

# DO Verb (Coming Next)

The third primary auxiliary verb is

```
do

does

did

doing

done
```

It is used for

* Questions
* Negatives
* Emphasis
* Main verb ("do homework")

Examples

Do you play cricket?

He doesn't study.

I did my homework.

# Easy Memory Trick

Only the **Present Tense** changes according to singular and plural.

| Verb | Singular      | Plural |
| ---- | ------------- | ------ |
| BE   | is *(I → am)* | are    |
| HAVE | has           | have   |
| DO   | does          | do     |

---

In the **Past Tense**:

| Verb | Singular | Plural |
| ---- | -------- | ------ |
| BE   | was      | were   |
| HAVE | had      | had    |
| DO   | did      | did    |

---

In the **Future Tense**:

Everyone uses the same form.

| Verb | Singular  | Plural    |
| ---- | --------- | --------- |
| BE   | will be   | will be   |
| HAVE | will have | will have |
| DO   | will do   | will do   |

# ⭐ Golden Rule (Very Important)

Only **three verbs** change according to the subject in the **present tense**:

| Base Verb | Singular (He/She/It) | Plural (I/You/We/They) |
| --------- | -------------------- | ---------------------- |
| **be**    | is *(I → am)*        | are                    |
| **have**  | has                  | have                   |
| **do**    | does                 | do                     |

For **most other verbs**, the rule is simpler:

| Base Verb | Singular    | Plural    |
| --------- | ----------- | --------- |
| play      | **plays**   | **play**  |
| eat       | **eats**    | **eat**   |
| write     | **writes**  | **write** |
| study     | **studies** | **study** |
| go        | **goes**    | **go**    |

Examples:

* He **plays** cricket. ✅
* They **play** cricket. ✅
* She **writes** a letter. ✅
* We **write** letters. ✅

This is why **be**, **have**, and **do** are called **primary auxiliary verbs**—they have special forms that every English learner should memorize. Once you know these three and the general rule for other verbs, subject-verb agreement becomes much easier.
