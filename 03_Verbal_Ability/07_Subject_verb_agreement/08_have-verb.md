# Rule: **Have / Has Agreement**

The verb **"have"** changes according to the subject.

| Subject | Verb     |
| ------- | -------- |
| I       | **have** |
| You     | **have** |
| We      | **have** |
| They    | **have** |
| He      | **has**  |
| She     | **has**  |
| It      | **has**  |

---

## Examples ✅

* I **have** a laptop.

* You **have** a bike.

* We **have** a meeting.

* They **have** many friends.

* He **has** a car.

* She **has** long hair.

* It **has** four legs.

---

## Wrong ❌

* He **have** a car.
* She **have** a phone.
* They **has** homework.
* I **has** a book.

---

# Memory Trick

Notice something?

| Subject     | Be Verb | Have Verb |
| ----------- | ------- | --------- |
| I           | am      | have      |
| He/She/It   | is      | has       |
| You/We/They | are     | have      |

See the pattern?

Only **He / She / It** are different.

Everything else uses **have**.

# Present Perfect Tense

This is where you'll see **has/have** the most.

### Singular

* He **has finished** the work.
* She **has arrived**.
* It **has stopped** raining.

### Plural

* They **have finished** the work.
* We **have arrived**.
* You **have completed** the task.

---

# Simple Present

"Have" can also mean **possess**.

Examples:

* I **have** a laptop.
* She **has** a laptop.
* They **have** a house.
* He **has** a house.

---

# Easy Formula

```text
I / You / We / They  → have

He / She / It        → has
```

# Compare All Three Together

| Subject | Be  | Have | Action Verb |
| ------- | --- | ---- | ----------- |
| I       | am  | have | play        |
| You     | are | have | play        |
| He      | is  | has  | plays       |
| She     | is  | has  | plays       |
| It      | is  | has  | plays       |

| They    | are | have | play        |

This table is one of the most important things to memorize for Subject–Verb Agreement.

2. ✅ **Be** verbs (am/is/are)
3. ✅ **Have** verbs (have/has)
4. Then move to the special SVA rules (`and`, `either...or`, `everyone`, `one of`, etc.).

I think this order is much more logical and much less confusing.

# The Verb "Have"

The verb **have** has different forms depending on **tense** and **subject**.

| Tense   | Singular Subject | Plural Subject |
| ------- | ---------------- | -------------- |
| Present | **has**          | **have**       |
| Past    | **had**          | **had**        |
| Future  | **will have**    | **will have**  |

---

# 1. Present Tense

### Singular Subjects

* He **has** a car.
* She **has** a book.
* Ram **has** a pen.

Here the verb is **has**.

---

### Plural Subjects

* They **have** a car.
* We **have** books.
* The students **have** uniforms.

Here the verb is **have**.

---

# Special Case: I and You

Even though **I** and **you** are singular, they use **have**, not **has**.

| Subject | Verb |
| ------- | ---- |
| I       | have |
| You     | have |
| We      | have |
| They    | have |
| He      | has  |
| She     | has  |
| It      | has  |

Examples:

* I **have** a laptop.
* You **have** a bike.
* He **has** a bike.

---

# 2. Past Tense

In the past tense, everyone uses **had**.

* I **had** a car.
* You **had** a car.
* He **had** a car.
* They **had** a car.

No singular/plural difference.

---

# 3. Future Tense

Everyone uses **will have**.

* I **will have** a car.
* He **will have** a car.
* They **will have** a car.

Again, no singular/plural difference.

---

# Quick Summary

| Subject | Present | Past | Future    |
| ------- | ------- | ---- | --------- |
| I       | have    | had  | will have |
| You     | have    | had  | will have |
| He      | has     | had  | will have |
| She     | has     | had  | will have |
| It      | has     | had  | will have |
| We      | have    | had  | will have |
| They    | have    | had  | will have |

---

# Easy Trick

Think of it like this:

* **Present Tense**

  * Singular (He/She/It/Ram) → **has**
  * Plural (We/They/Students) → **have**
  * **I** and **You** → **have**

* **Past Tense**

  * Everyone → **had**

* **Future Tense**

  * Everyone → **will have**

---

## One More Thing (Very Important)

There are **two different uses** of **have/has/had**:

### 1. Main Verb (means "to possess")

* I **have** a phone.
* She **has** a dog.
* They **had** a house.

### 2. Helping (Auxiliary) Verb

Used to form perfect tenses:

* I **have** finished my work. *(Present Perfect)*
* He **has** eaten lunch. *(Present Perfect)*
* They **had** left before I arrived. *(Past Perfect)*
* She **will have** completed the project by tomorrow. *(Future Perfect)*

The forms (**have**, **has**, **had**, **will have**) remain the same whether they are used as a main verb or a helping verb; only their function changes.

> **Remember:** In the **present tense**, **has = singular (except I and You)** and **have = plural (plus I and You)**. In the **past**, everyone uses **had**. In the **future**, everyone uses **will have**.
