Absolutely bro 😄 — I'll keep each one **one line**, but give you the meaning + a quick example so it's easier to remember for the exam.

* **Dented** — Having a hollow/mark caused by being hit or pressed; *“The car door was dented after the accident.”*
* **Crusade** — A strong and determined campaign for a particular cause; *“She began a crusade against corruption.”*
* **Satirical** — Using humor or irony to criticize people, society, or politics; *“The movie is a satirical take on modern politics.”*
* **Lexicographically** — Arranged in dictionary/alphabetical order; *“The words are listed lexicographically: apple, ball, cat.”*
* **Commemorate** — To honor or remember an important person or event; *“The monument commemorates the soldiers who died in the war.”*
* **Liberate** — To free someone from control, imprisonment, or oppression; *“The army liberated the prisoners.”*
* **Expatiate** — To speak or write in great detail about a subject; *“The professor expatiated on the causes of climate change.”*
* **Unguarded** — Not protected or watched, or said/done without thinking; *“He made an unguarded comment during the interview.”*
* **Momentous** — Extremely important and having a major effect on the future; *“Getting his first job was a momentous occasion.”*
* **Jovial** — Cheerful, friendly, and happy; *“His jovial nature makes everyone comfortable around him.”*
* **Paltry** — Very small or insignificant, especially disappointingly small; *“He received a paltry salary for his hard work.”*
* **Gullible** — Too willing to believe things and therefore easily deceived; *“The gullible man believed the fake lottery message.”*
* **Depleted** — Greatly reduced or almost completely used up; *“The long journey depleted our water supply.”*
* **Prone** — Likely or naturally inclined to experience/do something; *“He is prone to making careless mistakes.”*
* **Urbane** — Sophisticated, polished, and confident in social situations; *“The urbane gentleman impressed everyone at the party.”*
* **Eminent** — Famous and highly respected, especially in a profession; *“He is an eminent scientist.”*
* **Immanent** — Existing or operating within something rather than coming from outside; *“The divine is immanent in nature.”*
* **Imminent** — About to happen very soon; *“The storm is imminent.”*
* **Emanant** — Coming or flowing out from a particular source; *“The light emanant from the lamp filled the room.”*
* **Erect** — Standing straight/upright; *“He stood erect during the national anthem.”*
* **Daunted** — Made to feel frightened or less confident by something difficult; *“She was not daunted by the difficult examination.”*
* **Blasphemy** — Words or actions showing disrespect toward God or something sacred; *“The statement was considered blasphemy by the religious community.”*
* **Nag** — To repeatedly annoy someone by complaining or reminding them; *“My mother kept nagging me to study.”*
