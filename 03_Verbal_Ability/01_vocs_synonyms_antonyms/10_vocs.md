Absolutely! These are **4 useful vocabulary words**. Here’s the meaning in simple English + Bengali-style understanding + an example so you can remember them for TCS/Cognizant.

| Word            | Meaning                                                                | Easy way to remember            | Example                                                   |
| --------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Dispersed**   | Scattered/spread over a wide area                                      | **Spread apart**                | The crowd **dispersed** after the event.                  |
| **Vanished**    | Disappeared completely; became impossible to see                       | **Gone suddenly/completely**    | The magician made the coin **vanish**.                    |
| **Disappeared** | Became impossible to see or find                                       | **No longer visible**           | The sun **disappeared** behind the clouds.                |
| **Weaned**      | Gradually stopped depending on something, especially milk or a habit   | **Gradually stopped**           | The baby was **weaned** from breast milk.                 |
| **Obscurement** | The act/state of making something unclear, hidden, or difficult to see | **Hidden / unclear**            | The **obscurement** of the facts made the case difficult. |
| **Heights**     | High places or the measurement of how high something is                | **High level/place**            | He is afraid of **heights**.                              |
| **Silhouette**  | A dark outline of a person/object seen against a lighter background    | **Dark outline**                | I could see her **silhouette** through the window.        |
| **Shades**      | Slight differences of color; also things that block light              | **Color variations / darkness** | The artist used different **shades** of blue.             |

### ⭐ Most important distinction for this question

**Dispersed ≠ disappeared**

* **Dispersed** → spread/scattered
  → *The students dispersed after the class.*

* **Disappeared** → completely went out of sight
  → *The sun disappeared behind the clouds.*

**Heights ≠ silhouette**

* **Heights** → how high something is / high places
* **Silhouette** → **dark outline** of something

So in your question:

> **disappeared, silhouette** ✅

means the sun **went out of sight**, leaving/appearing as a **dark outline**.

### 🧠 Quick VOC memory

**Disperse = Spread**
**Disappear = Go away**
**Vanish = Completely disappear**
**Silhouette = Dark outline**
**Obscure = Hide / make unclear**
**Shade = Darkness / color variation**
**Height = How high**
**Wean = Gradually stop dependence**
