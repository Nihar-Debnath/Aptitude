# **Garrulous** *(adjective)*

### Meaning

**Excessively talkative**, especially about unimportant or trivial things.

### Usage

* **My grandfather is quite garrulous** and loves telling old stories.
  → He talks a lot.

* **The garrulous passenger kept everyone awake.**
  → The passenger wouldn't stop talking.

* **She became garrulous after meeting her old friends.**
  → She started talking continuously.

### Common Collocations

* garrulous old man
* garrulous neighbor
* garrulous speaker
* become garrulous

### Synonyms

**Talkative** *(likes talking a lot)*, **chatty** *(friendly and enjoys casual conversation)*, **loquacious** *(very talkative; formal)*, **verbose** *(uses more words than necessary)*, **wordy** *(contains too many words)*.

### Antonyms

**Taciturn** *(naturally speaks very little)*, **reserved** *(keeps thoughts to oneself)*, **quiet** *(doesn't talk much)*, **reticent** *(reluctant to speak)*, **silent** *(not speaking at all)*.

### Memory Tip

**Garrulous = Talks too much.**

# **Act up** *(phrasal verb)*

### Meaning

**Act up** has several common meanings depending on the context:

1. **To behave badly or misbehave.** *(Most common for children or pets.)*
2. **To stop working properly or malfunction.** *(For machines, devices, or body parts.)*
3. **To become troublesome or cause problems.**

### Usage

* **The children started acting up** in class.
  → They began misbehaving.

* **My laptop is acting up** today.
  → It isn't working properly.

* **My knee acts up** whenever it gets cold.
  → It starts causing pain.

### Common Collocations

* act up in class
* act up again
* engine acting up
* computer acting up
* knee/back acting up

### Synonyms

**Misbehave** *(behave badly)*, **malfunction** *(stop working properly)*, **play up** *(British English; malfunction or misbehave)*, **cause trouble** *(create problems)*.

### Antonyms

**Behave** *(act properly)*, **function** *(work correctly)*, **operate normally** *(work as expected)*, **cooperate** *(behave helpfully)*.

### Memory Tip

**Act up = Behave badly or stop working properly.**

# **Fasten on** *(phrasal verb)*

### Meaning

**Fasten on** means **to fix your attention, interest, or thoughts firmly on something**, or **to attach yourself firmly to something**.

### Usage

* **The child fastened on the colorful balloon.**
  → The child's attention became fixed on it.

* **The media fastened on one small mistake.**
  → They focused heavily on that mistake.

* **She fastened on his words.**
  → She paid very close attention to what he said.

### Common Collocations

* fasten on an idea
* fasten on a mistake
* fasten on someone's words
* fasten on a detail
* fasten on a target

### Synonyms

**Focus on** *(concentrate on)*, **fixate on** *(become obsessively focused on)*, **concentrate on** *(give full attention to)*, **cling to** *(hold onto firmly, physically or mentally)*, **latch onto** *(grasp or become interested in quickly)*.

### Antonyms

**Ignore** *(pay no attention to)*, **overlook** *(fail to notice)*, **dismiss** *(treat as unimportant)*, **disregard** *(intentionally pay no attention to)*.

### Memory Tip

**Fasten on = Fix your attention firmly on something.**

Think:

> **Fasten a seatbelt → Attach firmly.**
> **Fasten on an idea → Attach your mind firmly to it.**

# **Bona fide** *(adjective)*

### Meaning

### Usage

* **She is a bona fide expert** in cybersecurity.
  → She is a genuine expert.

* **This is a bona fide offer.**
  → It is a real and legitimate offer.

* **Only bona fide students** can enter the library.

### Common Collocations

* bona fide expert
* bona fide customer
* bona fide student
* bona fide reason
* bona fide offer

### Synonyms

**Genuine** *(truly real)*, **authentic** *(not fake; original)*, **legitimate** *(lawful and valid)*, **real** *(not false or imaginary)*, **valid** *(officially acceptable)*.

### Antonyms

**Fake** *(not genuine)*, **counterfeit** *(made to deceive)*, **fraudulent** *(dishonest or illegal)*, **bogus** *(false or fake)*, **phony** *(pretending to be real)*.

### Memory Tip

**Bona fide = 100% genuine and authentic.**

# **Epiphany** *(noun)*

### Meaning

**A sudden moment of understanding, realization, or insight that changes the way you think.**

### Usage

* **I had an epiphany** while reading the book.
  → I suddenly understood something important.

* **His failure led to an epiphany** about what truly mattered.
  → He had a life-changing realization.

* **It was an epiphany that changed** her career.
  → A sudden insight changed her direction.

### Common Collocations

* have an epiphany
* sudden epiphany
* moment of epiphany
* life-changing epiphany
* personal epiphany

### Synonyms

**Realization** *(understanding something clearly)*, **insight** *(deep understanding)*, **revelation** *(surprising new understanding)*, **awakening** *(becoming aware)*, **breakthrough** *(major discovery or progress)*.

### Antonyms

**Confusion** *(lack of understanding)*, **misunderstanding** *(incorrect understanding)*, **ignorance** *(lack of knowledge)*, **uncertainty** *(not knowing)*.

### Memory Tip

**Epiphany = "Aha!" moment.** 💡

# **Quest** *(noun / verb)*

### Meaning

**(Noun):** A long, determined search for something important.
**(Verb):** To search for or seek something.

### Usage

* **His quest for knowledge** never ended.
  → His determined search for knowledge continued.

* **They began a quest** to find the lost treasure.
  → They started an important search.

* **She is on a quest** to become a better programmer.
  → She is pursuing a meaningful goal.

### Common Collocations

* quest for knowledge
* quest for success
* lifelong quest
* heroic quest
* personal quest
* embark on a quest

### Synonyms

**Search** *(look for something)*, **pursuit** *(continued effort to achieve something)*, **mission** *(important task with a purpose)*, **journey** *(trip toward a goal)*, **expedition** *(organized journey of exploration)*.

### Antonyms

**Abandonment** *(giving up the search)*, **surrender** *(stop trying)*, **withdrawal** *(retreat from an effort)*, **inaction** *(doing nothing)*.

### Memory Tip

**Quest = A determined search for an important goal.**

Think:

> **Quest = Search + Purpose + Determination.** 🎯

# **Vixen** *(noun)*

### Meaning

1. **A female fox.** *(Literal meaning.)*
2. **An ill-tempered, fierce, or spirited woman.** *(Figurative; can be negative or playful depending on context.)*

### Usage

* **The vixen protected her cubs.**
  → The female fox defended her babies.

* **She was called a vixen** because of her fiery temper.
  → She was considered fierce or sharp-tempered.

### Common Collocations

* female vixen
* red vixen
* cunning vixen
* fiery vixen

### Synonyms

**Female fox** *(literal meaning)*, **shrew** *(bad-tempered woman; old-fashioned)*, **termagant** *(harsh, quarrelsome woman; literary)*.

### Antonyms

**Fox** *(male fox)*, **gentle woman** *(kind and mild woman, figurative)*.

### Memory Tip

**Vixen = Female fox (or a fierce woman).**

# **Doe** *(noun)*

### Meaning

**The adult female of certain animals**, especially a **deer**, but also rabbits, hares, goats, and antelopes.

### Usage

* **The doe ran into the forest** with her fawn.
  → The female deer ran away.

* **The hunter spotted a doe** near the river.
  → He saw a female deer.

### Common Collocations

* doe and fawn
* white-tailed doe
* young doe
* graceful doe

### Synonyms

**Female deer** *(most common meaning)*, **hind** *(female deer, especially red deer; less common)*.

### Antonyms

**Buck** *(male deer)*, **stag** *(adult male deer)*, **hart** *(male red deer; literary)*.

### Memory Tip

**Doe = Female deer.** 🦌

# **Rooster** *(noun)*

### Meaning

**An adult male chicken.** *(Called a **cock** in traditional English.)*

### Usage

* **The rooster crowed** at sunrise.
  → The male chicken made its morning call.

* **The rooster protects the hens.**
  → The male chicken watches over the female chickens.

### Common Collocations

* crowing rooster
* rooster crow
* farm rooster
* rooster and hen

### Synonyms

**Cock** *(adult male chicken; traditional term)*, **cockerel** *(young male chicken)*.

### Antonyms

**Hen** *(adult female chicken)*.

### Memory Tip

**Rooster = Male chicken that crows.** 🐓
