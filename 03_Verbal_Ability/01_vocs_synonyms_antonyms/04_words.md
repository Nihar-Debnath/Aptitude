Great list! I'll explain each word in a way that's useful for **vocabulary building**, **interviews**, and **daily English**.

---

# 1. Credibility *(noun)*

**Meaning:** The quality of being trusted or believed.

**Simple:** If people believe you, you have credibility.

**Example:**

* The scientist has a lot of credibility.
* Good work builds credibility.

**Synonyms:** Trustworthiness, reliability

**Remember:** **Credibility = Can people trust you?**

---

# 2. Perceived *(verb/adjective)*

**Meaning:** Thought of or understood in a particular way.

**Simple:** What people think about something.

**Example:**

* He is perceived as a good leader.
* The task was perceived as difficult.

**Remember:**
Reality and perception are not always the same.

---

# 3. Punctuation *(noun)*

**Meaning:** Marks used in writing.

Examples:

* . (period/full stop)
* , (comma)
* ? (question mark)
* ! (exclamation mark)

**Example:**

* Good punctuation makes writing easier to understand.

---

# 4. Distant *(adjective)*

**Meaning 1:** Far away.

> The school is distant from my house.

**Meaning 2:** Emotionally cold.

> He became distant after the argument.

---

# 5. Taciturn *(adjective)*

**Meaning:** Someone who speaks very little.

**Simple:** Quiet by nature.

**Example:**

* My grandfather is taciturn but very kind.

**Synonyms:**

* Reserved
* Quiet
* Silent

---

# 6. August *(adjective)*

⚠️ Not the month.

**Meaning:** Respected, noble, or impressive.

**Example:**

* He belongs to an august family.
* The judge was an august figure.

**Synonyms:**

* Distinguished
* Honorable
* Noble

---

# 7. Dignified *(adjective)*

**Meaning:** Calm, respectful, and graceful.

**Example:**

* She remained dignified during the interview.
* He handled criticism in a dignified way.

**Synonyms:**

* Respectable
* Graceful
* Noble

---

# 8. Nurturing *(verb/adjective)*

**Meaning:** Helping someone or something grow.

**Example:**

* Parents should provide a nurturing environment.
* Teachers nurture young minds.

**Think of:**
A mother taking care of her child.

---

# 9. Collateral Damage *(phrase)*

**Meaning:** Unintended damage while trying to achieve another goal.

Originally used in war.

**Example:**

* The building was collateral damage during the attack.

Also used metaphorically:

* Stress can cause collateral damage to relationships.

---

# 10. Juvenile *(adjective/noun)*

**Meaning 1:** Young.

> Juvenile animals need care.

**Meaning 2:** Immature.

> Stop making juvenile jokes.

---

# 11. Metaphor *(noun)*

**Meaning:** Comparing two things without using "like" or "as."

Example:

> Time is money.

Not literally true, but it compares time with money.

More examples:

* Life is a journey.
* The world is a stage.

---

# 12. Rouge *(noun)*

Pronounced: **Roohzh**

**Meaning:** Red makeup used on the cheeks.

Example:

* She applied rouge before the event.

⚠️ Don't confuse:

**Rouge** → Makeup

**Rogue** → A dishonest or dangerous person

---

# 13. Sentry *(noun)*

**Meaning:** A guard who watches an area.

Example:

* A sentry stood at the gate all night.

Think:
Soldier guarding a castle.

---

# 14. Lure *(verb/noun)*

**Meaning:** To attract or tempt someone.

**Example:**

* The advertisement lured customers.
* Money lured him into the business.

**Synonyms:**

* Attract
* Tempt
* Entice

---

# 15. Unimpeachable *(adjective)*

Pronounced:
**un-im-PEE-chuh-bul**

**Meaning:** Cannot be doubted or questioned.

Example:

* She has an unimpeachable reputation.
* The evidence was unimpeachable.

**Simple:**
100% trustworthy.

---

# 16. Unleashed *(verb)*

**Meaning:** Released with great force or freedom.

Example:

* The company unleashed a new product.
* The storm unleashed heavy rain.
* He unleashed his full potential.

Think:
Something powerful being set free.

### 💡 Vocabulary Tip

Whenever you learn a new word, remember these four things:

1. **Meaning** – What does it mean?
2. **Pronunciation** – How do you say it?
3. **Example** – Use it in a sentence.
4. **Synonym** – Learn one similar word.

This method helps you remember words much longer than memorizing definitions alone.
