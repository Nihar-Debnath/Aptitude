## **Ponzi** *(noun/adjective, as in "Ponzi scheme")*

**Meaning:** **A type of financial fraud where money from new investors is used to pay returns to earlier investors instead of earning real profits.**

**Pronunciation:** *PON-zee* /ˈpɒn.zi/

### Simple Meaning

> **A fake investment scam that pays old investors with new investors' money.**

### How it's Used

**Common phrases:**

* **Ponzi scheme**
* **Ponzi scam**
* **Ponzi operator**

### Examples ✅

* **The company was exposed as a Ponzi scheme.**
* **Thousands of people lost their savings in the Ponzi scam.**
* **The fraudster ran a Ponzi operation for years.**

### How it's NOT Used ❌

❌ **He is ponzi.** *(Wrong: "Ponzi" usually describes the **scheme**, not a person.)*

❌ **This business is Ponzi** *(unless you mean it is actually a fraudulent Ponzi scheme).*

### Synonyms

* Investment fraud
* Pyramid scam *(similar, but not the same)*
* Financial scam

### Antonyms

* Legitimate investment
* Genuine business

### Memory Trick

Think:

> **Ponzi = Pay Old with New Investors.** 💸

No real profit is generated—the money just keeps circulating until the scheme collapses.

### Difference

* **Ponzi Scheme** → Investors are paid using **money from new investors**.
* **Pyramid Scheme** → Participants mainly earn by **recruiting new members**, who then recruit others.

> ⚠️ The term comes from **Charles Ponzi**, who became famous for running this type of fraud in the 1920s.

# **Mediation** *(noun)*

**Pronunciation:** *mee-dee-AY-shun* /ˌmiː.diˈeɪ.ʃən/

### Synonyms
