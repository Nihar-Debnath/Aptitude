### Sentences

**1.** His political career came to an abrupt end with China's military operation.
**2.** He attracted as he repelled.
**3.** He was responsible for the debacle.
**4.** A man of paradoxes, **Menon** remained an enigma.

Options:

* A. **4312**
* B. **1342**
* C. **4213**
* D. **4123**

## Step 1 — Check the starting sentence

Look only at the first number of each option:

**A → 4**
**B → 1**
**C → 4**
**D → 4**

Interesting! Three options start with **4**.

Now compare 1 and 4.

Sentence 1:

> **His** political career came to an abrupt end...

Whose political career?

We don't know yet. ❌

Sentence 4:

> A man of paradoxes, **Menon** remained an enigma.

This actually **introduces Menon**. ✅

So we get:

**Menon → His/He**

Therefore sentence **4 should come first**.

❌ Eliminate **B (1342)**.

Remaining:

* A. **4312**
* C. **4213**
* D. **4123**

---

# Step 2 — Look for the strongest connection

Sentence 4 says:

> A man of **paradoxes**, Menon remained an enigma.

What does **paradox** mean here?

Someone having apparently **contradictory qualities**.

Now look at Sentence 2:

> He **attracted as he repelled**.

That's exactly a paradox!

Some people were attracted to him while others were repelled by him.

So sentence 2 **explains the "man of paradoxes" statement in 4.**

Strong connection:

> **4 → 2**

Now check our remaining options:

A. **4 → 3** ❌
C. **4 → 2** ✅
D. **4 → 1** ❌

Only one survives.

# ✅ Answer: C — 4213

And we could stop here in the exam.

---

## But let's verify it

**4 →** A man of paradoxes, Menon remained an enigma.

**2 →** He attracted as he repelled.

That's an example of his contradictory/paradoxical personality.

**1 →** His political career came to an abrupt end with China's military operation.

Now we move to what happened to his career.

**3 →** He was responsible for **the debacle**.

"The debacle" refers back to the disastrous event/military situation mentioned in 1.

So:

> **4 → 2 → 1 → 3**

✅ **C. 4213**

---

### Notice how little work was actually necessary

We used only two clues:

**Clue 1: Name → Pronoun**

> 4: **Menon**
> ↓
> 1/2/3: **His/He/He**

Therefore **4 starts**.

**Clue 2: General idea → Explanation**

> 4: **man of paradoxes**
> ↓
> 2: **attracted as he repelled**

Therefore **4 → 2**.

Once you find **42**, only **Option C** contains it.

**That's where you stop.** No need to waste exam time proving every remaining connection unless you want a quick final verification.
