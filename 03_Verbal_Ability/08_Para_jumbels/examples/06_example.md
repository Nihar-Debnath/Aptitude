💡 One more pattern to add to your notes:

### **Contrast Pair**

Words like:

* But
* However
* Yet
* Nevertheless
* On the contrary
* Instead

usually **cannot begin a paragraph**. They almost always **contrast with the immediately preceding idea**.

Example:

```
People think X.

BUT

Actually Y.
```

This is one of the fastest elimination clues in TCS para jumbles.
