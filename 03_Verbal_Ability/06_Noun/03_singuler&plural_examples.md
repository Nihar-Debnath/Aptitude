# Question 1 (TCS Style)

Choose the correct sentence.

A. She gave me many advices.

B. She gave me much advices.

C. She gave me many advice.

D. She gave me much advice.

---

## Step 1

Find the noun.

```
advice
```

Ask yourself

> Is advice countable or uncountable?

It is **uncountable**.

So

❌ advices

is impossible.

---

Option A

many advices

Wrong.

---

Option B

much advices

Wrong.

---

Option C

many advice

Wrong.

"many" is used for countable nouns.

---

Option D

much advice

Correct.

✅ Answer = D

---
