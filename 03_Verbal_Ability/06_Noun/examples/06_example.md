# Sentence A

> **My friend's sister's son is a dancer.**

Let's break it down.

```text
My

```

Is it grammatically correct?

Does it sound natural?

✅ Yes.

Native speakers say things like:

* My uncle's daughter's wedding
* My mother's friend's house

The son

✅ Yes.

Is it natural?

✅ Yes.

# So which one is better?

Usually,

### Sentence A

> My friend's sister's son...

is **more natural**.

Why?

Because the owner is a **person**.

Remember the rule we learned.

### For people

Prefer

```text
's
```

Examples

Rahul's bike

Teacher's pen

Mother's love

Friend's sister

---

### For things

Prefer

```text
of
```

Examples

The roof of the house

The door of the car

The pages of the book

# Why not always use "of"?

Imagine this.

Sentence B

> The son of my friend's sister...

is correct.

But imagine a longer sentence.

> The son of the brother of the friend of my father...

😵‍💫

It becomes difficult to read.

Now compare

> My father's friend's brother's son...

Although it has many **'s**, it's actually easier to follow because English speakers are very familiar with family relationships expressed this way.

Suppose

```text
Rahul
│
Brother
│
Son
```

Natural English

> Rahul's brother's son

Instead of

> The son of Rahul's brother

Both are correct.

But English prefers the first one.

# Placement Exam Trick

For **family members**, always prefer **'s**.

Examples

✅ My mother's friend

❌ The friend of my mother (correct but less natural)

---

✅ Rahul's uncle

❌ The uncle of Rahul

---

✅ My brother's wife

❌ The wife of my brother

If the chain becomes very long.
