2. What technologies does the team use most often?

4. How does the company support learning and professional growth?
5. Are there opportunities to work on different projects or technologies?
6. What are the next steps in the interview process?

---

## Final interview tips

* **Keep answers to 1–2 minutes** unless the interviewer asks for more detail.
* **Be honest.** If you don't know something, say so and explain how you'd learn it.
* **Support your answers with examples** from your projects whenever possible.
* **Stay positive.** Avoid criticizing previous teachers, classmates, or companies.
* **Know your resume thoroughly.** Anything listed on it is fair game for questions.
