# If You Know Nothing About the Topic

Remember this **6-step formula**. It works for almost every topic.

1. **Introduction**

   > "Today, I would like to talk about..."

2. **Definition**

   > "This topic means..."

3. **Importance**

   > "It is important because..."

4. **Example**

   > "For example..."

5. **Personal Opinion**

   > "In my opinion..."

6. **Conclusion**

   > "Overall, I believe..."

This structure can easily help you speak for **1–2 minutes** without getting stuck.
