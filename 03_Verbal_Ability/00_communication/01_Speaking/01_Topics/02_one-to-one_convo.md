## One thing I want you to remember

You're not calling him because he "owes" you a job. You're calling because he has experience that you don't. If, by the end of the conversation, he thinks:

> *"This guy is respectful, has built real projects, listens carefully, and is genuinely trying to improve."*

then you've already succeeded. A referral often comes *after* someone forms that impression.

If you prepare well, this won't feel like asking for a favor—it will feel like the start of a professional relationship.
