# My Recommended Workflow

Suppose today you're studying **Present Simple**.

### Step 1

Read your notes (15–20 min)

⬇️

### Step 2

Watch one explanation video (10 min)

⬇️

### Step 3

Do **20–30 grammar questions** on a practice site.

⬇️

### Step 4

Write **10 sentences** using the grammar.

⬇️

### Step 5

Speak for **5 minutes**, making a conscious effort to use that grammar naturally.

# Since you're preparing for a communication assessment...

I wouldn't spend hours solving hundreds of multiple-choice questions.

Instead, I'd aim for a balance like this:

* **30% Grammar Rules** → Learn the concept.
* **30% Grammar Exercises** → Confirm you understand it.
* **40% Real Usage** → Use it in speaking and writing.

The last part is what helps you communicate confidently.
