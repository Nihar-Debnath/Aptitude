# 1. Read first 3 lines to get an idea of the passage.

### What it means

Don't jump to Blank (1).

First understand:

> **What is this paragraph about?**

Every cloze test has one central theme.

Example:

> Rahul wanted to become a doctor. He studied day and night...

Now you already know the passage is about **hard work and success**.

So if a blank comes like

> Rahul never ____ hope.

You can easily guess

✅ lost

because of the theme.

---

# 2. Link the sentences together.

This is one of the most important rules.

Many students solve like this:

Sentence 1 ✅

Sentence 2 ✅

Sentence 3 ✅

They treat every sentence separately.

Wrong.

Instead think like you're reading a story.

Example

> There was no competition.
>
> Companies sold anything.
>
> Then competition increased.
>
> Customers got choices.

Notice how every sentence supports the next one.

That's why the answer became

> pick and choose

instead of random guessing.

# 4. Tone

Tone means

> **What emotion or attitude does the passage have?**

Different tones use different words.

---

### Positive tone

> The new invention helped thousands of people.

Blank

It was a ____ invention.

Options

* wonderful ✅
* terrible
* horrible
* useless

Positive passage → positive word.

---

### Negative tone

> The company suffered huge losses.

Blank

Employees felt ____.

Options

* disappointed ✅
* excited
* cheerful
* joyful

Negative passage → negative word.

---

### Funny tone

The missing word should also sound humorous.

---

### Serious tone

The missing word should sound formal.

# 5. Negate options (Elimination)

Very important in TCS.

Sometimes you don't know the answer.

Instead remove wrong ones.

Example

Blank

> She was ____ after running 20 km.

Options

* tired
* sleeping
* elephant

You may not know.

But

elephant ❌

beautiful ❌

tired

sleeping

"Tired" fits better.

That's elimination.

Example

You don't know?

# The 6-step Cloze Test Formula (Memorize this)

Whenever you see a blank, ask yourself these questions in order:

1. **What is the overall theme of the passage?**
2. **How does this sentence connect with the previous and next sentences?**
3. **What type of word is missing?** (noun, verb, adjective, adverb, pronoun, preposition, etc.)
4. **Does the word match the tone?** (positive, negative, formal, humorous)
5. **Can I eliminate any impossible options?**
6. **Are there keywords or common word pairs (collocations) around the blank?**

If you follow these six steps consistently, you'll solve most TCS Cloze Test questions with much higher accuracy instead of relying on guesswork.
