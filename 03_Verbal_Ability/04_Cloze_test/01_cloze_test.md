A **Cloze Test** is a very common topic in **Verbal Ability**, especially in exams like **TCS Ignite, TCS NQT, Cognizant, Wipro, Infosys, Capgemini**, etc.

### What is a Cloze Test?

A cloze test is a **paragraph with multiple blanks**, and you have to choose the **most suitable word** for each blank from the given options.

It tests whether you understand:

* Vocabulary
* Grammar
* Sentence flow
* Context
* Logic of the paragraph

---

### Example

> Technology has become an important part of our ______ (1). It helps people ______ (2) faster and makes communication more ______ (3).

Options:

**(1)**

* A. lives ✅
* B. tables
* C. bottles
* D. pencils

**(2)**

* A. sleep
* B. work ✅
* C. cry
* D. dance

**(3)**

* A. difficult
* B. expensive
* C. efficient ✅
* D. angry

Completed paragraph:

> Technology has become an important part of our **lives**. It helps people **work** faster and makes communication more **efficient**.

## Skills needed to solve a Cloze Test

### 1. Vocabulary

Know the meanings of words.

Example:

> He was ______ after winning the competition.

* happy ✅
* broken
* empty
* sleeping

---

### 2. Grammar

The word should fit grammatically.

Example:

> She ______ to school every day.

* go
* goes ✅
* going
* gone

---

### 3. Context

Sometimes more than one word is grammatically correct, but only one fits the meaning.

Example:

> It was raining heavily, so we carried an ______.

* umbrella ✅
* television
* pillow
* bicycle

---

### 4. Collocations

Some words naturally go together.

Examples:

* make a decision ✅
* take a shower ✅
* strong coffee ✅
* heavy rain ✅

Not:

* do a decision ❌
* powerful rain ❌

---

## How to solve a Cloze Test (Exam Strategy)

1. **Read the entire paragraph first.**

   * Understand the overall idea before filling any blanks.

2. **Fill the easiest blanks first.**

   * These often help you understand the rest of the paragraph.

3. **Use grammar clues.**

   * Check the tense, articles (a, an, the), prepositions, and subject-verb agreement.

4. **Use context.**

   * Ask yourself: *What word makes the paragraph meaningful?*

5. **Read the completed paragraph again.**

   * Make sure it sounds natural and logical.

## Example (TCS-style)

> Reading books is a good habit. It helps us ______ (1) our knowledge and improves our ______ (2) skills. People who read regularly usually have a better ______ (3) of the world.

### Blank (1)

Options:

* increase ✅
* decreases
* broken
* forgot

### Blank (2)

Options:

* communication ✅
* sleeping
* eating
* dancing

### Blank (3)

Options:

* understanding ✅
* bottle
* window
* machine

Final paragraph:

> Reading books is a good habit. It helps us **increase** our knowledge and improves our **communication** skills. People who read regularly usually have a better **understanding** of the world.

## Common question patterns in TCS

* Vocabulary-based
* Grammar-based
* Prepositions
* Articles (a, an, the)
* Conjunctions (and, but, although, however)
* Tenses
* Pronouns
* Context-based meaning
* Collocations (word combinations)

---

## Quick tips for TCS Ignite

* Read the full paragraph before answering.
* Focus on the **overall meaning**, not just one sentence.
* Eliminate options that are grammatically incorrect.
* If two options seem correct, choose the one that best fits the context and natural word combination.
* Practice daily—after solving enough questions, you'll start recognizing common patterns.

Since you're preparing for **TCS Ignite**, make sure you practice **Cloze Tests** along with **Reading Comprehension**, **Error Spotting**, **Sentence Improvement**, **Para Jumbles**, and **Synonyms/Antonyms**, as these are among the most frequently tested verbal topics.
