Sentence Correction and Error Correction are two of the most common topics in **Verbal Ability** for companies like **TCS, Cognizant, Capgemini, Accenture, Infosys, and Wipro**.

Although they sound similar, they test different skills.

---

# 1. Sentence Correction

### Meaning

You are given a sentence with a grammatical mistake. Your job is to **choose the correct version** of the sentence.

### What it Tests

* Grammar
* Sentence structure
* Correct word usage
* Tenses
* Articles
* Prepositions
* Subject-Verb Agreement

### Example

❌ **She don't like coffee.**

Options:

* A. She don't like coffee.
* B. She doesn't likes coffee.
* C. **She doesn't like coffee.** ✅
* D. She not like coffee.

**Answer:** C

---

Another Example

❌ **Neither of the boys are present.**

✅ **Neither of the boys is present.**

**Why?**
"Neither" is singular, so the verb should be **is**, not **are**.

---

# 2. Error Correction (Error Spotting)

### Meaning

Instead of correcting the whole sentence, you have to **find where the mistake is**.

### What it Tests

Same grammar topics, but your job is to **identify the error**, not rewrite everything.

### Example

**She / don't / like coffee / No Error**

A. She

B. don't ✅

C. like coffee

D. No Error

Because **don't** should be **doesn't**.

---

Another Example

**Each of the students / have submitted / the assignment / No Error**

Error:
**have submitted** ❌

Correct:
**has submitted** ✅

---

# Topics You Need to Learn

Most questions come from these areas:

| Topic                  | Example                                                           |
| ---------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Subject-Verb Agreement | He **goes**, not He **go**                                        |
| Tenses                 | I **have eaten**, not I **has eaten**                             |
| Articles               | a, an, the                                                        |
| Prepositions           | interested **in**, good **at**                                    |
| Pronouns               | Everyone brought **his/her**, not **their** (traditional grammar) |
| Conjunctions           | Although, Because, Neither...nor                                  |
| Modals                 | can, could, should, must                                          |
| Active & Passive Voice | The work was completed.                                           |
| Parallelism            | She likes **reading, writing, and singing.**                      |
| Word Choice            | affect vs effect                                                  |

---

# Difference

| Sentence Correction                | Error Correction            |
| ---------------------------------- | --------------------------- |
| Correct the sentence               | Find the mistake            |
| Choose the best version            | Identify the incorrect part |
| Tests grammar + sentence structure | Tests grammar awareness     |

# Example Asked in TCS/Cognizant

### Sentence Correction

❌ He have completed the project.

✅ He has completed the project.

---

### Error Spotting

He / have completed / the project / yesterday.

Error:
**have completed** ❌

Correct:
**completed** or **has completed** (depending on context)

## Which is Easier?

* **Sentence Correction** → Easier if you know grammar rules because you can compare options.
* **Error Correction** → Slightly harder because you must detect the exact mistake without being guided.
