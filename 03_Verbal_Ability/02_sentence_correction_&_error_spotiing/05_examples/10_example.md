# Step 2: Understand the verb "voice"

The verb **voice** means:

> **to express**

Examples:

* She voiced her opinion.
* The public voiced concern.
* They voiced their objections.

Notice what comes after **voice**.

We say:

> **voice + concern**

NOT

> **voice + concerned**

Look at these words:

| Word      | Part of Speech | Meaning |
| --------- | -------------- | ------- |
| concern   | Noun / Verb    | worry   |
| concerned | Adjective      | worried |

Examples:

### Concern (Noun)

✅ They expressed **concern**.

✅ The report raised **concern**.

---

### Concerned (Adjective)

✅ I am **concerned** about pollution.

✅ She is **concerned** about her exams.

---

After **voiced**, we need a **noun**.

So:

> voiced **concern** ✅

Not:

> voiced **concerned** ❌

# Original Sentence

> **Census reports in India have voiced concerned over the declining trend in the sex ratio.**

Let's break it into pieces.

## Subject

> Census reports

Who is doing the action?

👉 Census reports

---

## Verb

> have voiced

What does **voice** mean?

It means:

> **to express**

Examples:

* He voiced his opinion.
* She voiced her anger.
* They voiced their concern.

So,

> **voice = express**

---

Now replace "voice" with "express".

The sentence becomes:

> Census reports have **expressed concerned**...

Does that sound right?

❌ No.

We say:

> expressed **concern**

not

> expressed **concerned**

---

# Why?

Because **express** and **voice** need a **thing** after them.

That "thing" is usually a **noun**.

Look:

### Example 1

> I expressed my happiness.

What is **happiness**?

It is a **thing (noun)**.

---

### Example 2

> She voiced her opinion.

Opinion = noun

---

### Example 3

> They voiced concern.

Concern = noun

---

# But what is "concerned"?

Look at these two sentences.

### Sentence 1

> I have a concern.

What is **concern**?

It is a **noun**.

It means:

> a worry

---

### Sentence 2

> I am concerned.

Now what is **concerned**?

It describes **me**.

So it is an **adjective**.

Meaning:

> worried

---

## Compare

### Noun

> concern

means

> worry

Example:

> His concern is money.

---

### Adjective

> concerned

means

> worried

Example:

> He is concerned about money.

---

# Here's the biggest trick

After **is/am/are/was/were**, we usually use an adjective.

Example:

> I am happy.

Happy = adjective

---

> She is concerned.

Concerned = adjective

---

But after **voice** or **express**, we usually use a noun.

Example:

> express happiness

> express concern

> voice concern

> voice anger

---

Never

> express happy ❌

> voice concerned ❌

---

# Think like this

Suppose I ask you:

> What did he express?

Possible answers:

* disappointment ✅

These are all **things (nouns)**.

I cannot answer:

* concerned ❌
* angry ❌
* happy ❌

Because these are **describing words (adjectives)**.
