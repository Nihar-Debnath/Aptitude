> Q. (a) belt is a vital (b) part of clothing (c) for every men


Let's solve it **the same way you should solve it in an exam.**

### Sentence

> (A) **belt is a vital**
> (B) **part of clothing**
> (C) **for every men**
> (D) No Error

---

# Step 1: Read the sentence

> Belt is a vital part of clothing for every men.

Does it sound natural?

Something feels wrong in **"every men."**

---

# Step 2: Find the grammar rule

The word **every** is always followed by a **singular countable noun**.

### Rule

✅ every + singular noun

Examples:

* Every **student** is present.
* Every **child** likes chocolate.
* Every **man** should exercise.
* Every **book** has a title.

❌ Every students
❌ Every children
❌ Every men

---

# Step 3: Apply the rule

The sentence has:

> every **men** ❌

But:

* **man** = singular
* **men** = plural

So it should be:

> every **man** ✅

---

# Correct Sentence

> **A belt is a vital part of clothing for every man.**

Notice another small improvement:

We usually say:

> **A belt is...**

instead of

> Belt is...

because **belt** is a singular countable noun. A singular countable noun normally needs an article (**a**, **an**, or **the**) when we're talking about one item in general.

So the most natural sentence is:

> **A belt is a vital part of clothing for every man.**

---

# Is there one error or two?

This depends on the exam.

### If the exam expects **only one error** (which most placement tests do):

✅ **Option C** is the intended answer because **every men** is clearly wrong.

### From a strict grammar point of view:

There is also an issue in **Part A** because **"belt"** should usually be **"A belt."**

---

# Exam Tip

In many error-spotting questions, **the exam setter intends only one error**. If you notice two possible issues:

1. Find the **obvious grammatical rule** being tested.
2. See whether the other issue is a matter of style or omitted article.
3. In placement tests, choose the option with the **clear grammatical error**.

Here, the clear rule is:

> **Every + Singular Noun**

So the expected answer is **Option C**.

---

## Grammar Rule to Remember

| Correct ✅     | Incorrect ❌    |
| ------------- | -------------- |
| Every man     | Every men      |
| Every student | Every students |
| Every child   | Every children |
| Every person  | Every people   |

This is one of the most frequently tested rules in sentence correction and error spotting.
