> Q. (a) Englishmen is (b) running hastily on (c) the platform of the railway station


Let's solve it step by step.

### Sentence

> (A) **Englishmen is**
> (B) **running hastily on**
> (C) **the platform of the railway station**

---

# Step 1: Find the Subject

Subject = **Englishmen**

Now ask yourself:

Is **Englishmen** singular or plural?

* **Englishman** = One person (Singular)
* **Englishmen** = More than one person (Plural)

Examples:

* An **Englishman** lives here. ✅
* Two **Englishmen** live here. ✅

---

# Step 2: Check the Verb

The sentence says:

> **Englishmen is**

Since **Englishmen** is plural, we need a plural verb.

| Subject    | Correct Verb |
| ---------- | ------------ |
| Englishman | is           |
| Englishmen | are          |

So,

❌ Englishmen **is**

✅ Englishmen **are**

Therefore, **Part A contains the error.**

---

# Step 3: Check the Remaining Parts

### Part B

> running hastily on

This is grammatically fine.

---

### Part C

> the platform of the railway station

This is also grammatically correct.

---

# Correct Sentence

> **Englishmen are running hastily on the platform of the railway station.**

---

# Grammar Rule

## Singular vs Plural Nouns

Some nouns change completely in their plural form.

| Singular   | Plural     |
| ---------- | ---------- |
| Man        | Men        |
| Woman      | Women      |
| Englishman | Englishmen |
| Frenchman  | Frenchmen  |
| Policeman  | Policemen  |
| Fireman    | Firemen    |

Remember:

* **Man** → **Men**
* **Englishman** → **Englishmen**

---

## Subject–Verb Agreement

| Subject    | Verb |
| ---------- | ---- |
| Englishman | is   |
| Englishmen | are  |
| Woman      | is   |
| Women      | are  |
| Child      | is   |
| Children   | are  |

---

## Exam Trick

Whenever you see words ending in **-men** or **-women**, don't be fooled. They are **plural**, so they take **plural verbs**.

Examples:

* The **policemen are** on duty. ✅
* The **firemen have** arrived. ✅
* The **Englishmen are** waiting. ✅

❌ The policemen is...
❌ The Englishmen is...

### **Answer: (A)**

**"Englishmen is"** should be **"Englishmen are."**
