# Step 1: Read the sentence

> **The man to who I sold my house was a cheat.**

The underlined part is:

> **to who I sold**

Ask yourself:

**Who sold the house?**

👉 **I** sold the house.

**To whom did I sell it?**

👉 To the man.

So "the man" is the **object** of the preposition **to**.

---

# Step 2: Apply the "who vs whom" rule

This is one of the most important rules.

## Rule

### Use **who** → Subject

Who performs the action.

Examples

* Who came here?
* Who is singing?

Here "who" is doing the action.

---

### Use **whom** → Object

Who receives the action.

Examples

I met **whom?**

I met **him.**

Since "him" is an object,

use **whom**.

---

# Step 3: Use the HIM Test

Replace the pronoun.

Sentence:

> I sold my house to ______.

Can we say

> I sold my house to **he**?

❌ No.

Can we say

> I sold my house to **him**?

✅ Yes.

Since the answer is **him**,

we use

> **whom**

---

# Step 4: Check the options

### A)

> to whom I sell

Grammar is okay regarding **whom**.

But tense changed.

Original:

> sold

Option:

> sell

❌ Wrong tense.

---

### B)

> to who I sell

Two mistakes.

* wrong pronoun
* wrong tense

❌ Wrong.

---

### C)

> who was sold to

This changes the meaning completely.

Original:

I sold **my house**.

Now it sounds like

**the man was sold**.

😂 That's obviously wrong.

❌ Reject.

---

### D)

> to whom I sold

Perfect.

* correct tense
* correct pronoun

✅ Correct Answer

---

### E)

No correction

Original has

> to who

which is incorrect.

❌ Wrong.

---

# Final Answer

✅ **D) to whom I sold**

The complete sentence becomes:

> **The man to whom I sold my house was a cheat.**

---

# Why "whom"?

Let's draw the sentence.

```
I  ----sold----> house

house ----to----> the man
```

The man is **not selling**.

The man is **receiving**.

Therefore

the man is the **object**.

Object ⇒ **whom**

---

# A shortcut for exams

Whenever you see **who/whom**, use this trick.

Replace the word with **he/him**.

If **he** fits → use **who**

If **him** fits → use **whom**

Examples

### Example 1

Who called you?

Replace:

He called you.

✅ "He" works.

So

**Who** called you?

---

### Example 2

To whom did you speak?

Replace:

I spoke to him.

"Him" works.

So

**Whom**

---

### Example 3

The girl who won the race.

Replace:

She won the race.

"She" = subject

Use **who**.

---

### Example 4

The girl whom I met yesterday.

Replace:

I met her.

"Her" = object

Use **whom**.

---

## One more thing (important for competitive exams)

In **formal English**, the correct sentence is:

> **The man to whom I sold my house...**

But in **spoken English**, people often say:

> **The man who I sold my house to...**

or

> **The man I sold my house to...**

These are common in conversation, but **sentence correction questions usually test formal grammar**, so **"to whom"** is the expected answer.
