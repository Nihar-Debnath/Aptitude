Let me teach you a repeatable method that works for almost every sentence improvement question.

### Step 1: Read the whole sentence

Didi was so stabilized by the accident that she lost the ability to speak.

Now ask:

What happened because of the accident?

* She lost the ability to speak.

* She was deeply shocked.

* She was mentally affected.

So the highlighted word should mean:

severely shocked / emotionally disturbed / psychologically affected

### Step 2: Check the highlighted word

### “stabilized”

What does stabilize mean?

to make steady, balanced, calm, or stable.

Examples:

* The doctor stabilized the patient.

* The government stabilized the economy.

* She became emotionally stable after therapy.

Now read the sentence:

“She was so stabilized by the accident that she lost the ability to speak.”

Does becoming stable make someone lose the ability to speak?

❌ No. The meaning is opposite.

So the word must be replaced.

### Step 3: Test each option by meaning

### A. Traumatized ✅

Meaning:

deeply shocked, emotionally or psychologically injured.

Sentence:

Didi was so traumatized by the accident that she lost the ability to speak.

This makes perfect sense.

Accident → trauma → inability to speak from shock.

✅ Correct.

### B. Ashamed ❌

Meaning:

feeling guilty or embarrassed.

Would an accident necessarily make her ashamed?

No.

And shame usually does not explain losing the ability to speak from shock.

Reject.

### C. Betrayed ❌

Meaning:

deceived by someone trusted.

An accident does not betray a person.

Reject.

### D. Corrupted ❌

Meaning:

morally spoiled or dishonest.

Completely unrelated.

Reject.

### Answer

### Correct answer

A

### Traumatized

Didi was so traumatized by the accident that she lost the ability to speak.

“Traumatized” matches the context of severe emotional shock caused by an accident.

### But how do you solve such questions in the exam?

Here is the 4-step TCS method.

### TCS Sentence Improvement Method

### STEP 1: Find the cause and effect

Look for words like:

* so...that

* because

* therefore

* as a result

* due to

* after

* before

Here:

```
Accident  →  lost ability to speak
```

So the missing word must connect the cause to that effect.

### STEP 2: Ask “What meaning is required?”

Required meaning:

shocked / mentally affected

Not:

* stable

* ashamed

* betrayed

* corrupted

### STEP 3: Eliminate unrelated options

| Option      | Related to accident shock? |
| ----------- | -------------------------- |
| Traumatized | ✅ Yes                      |
| Ashamed     | ❌ No                       |
| Betrayed    | ❌ No                       |
| Corrupted   | ❌ No                       |

Only one fits the context.

### STEP 4: Substitute and read aloud

Always replace the word and read the full sentence.

If it sounds natural and logical, it is usually correct.

### Vocabulary Pattern TCS Loves

These questions are often context-based.

Example:

He was elated after winning the award.

Needed meaning: very happy.

She was devastated by the loss.

Needed meaning: extremely sad.

The child was terrified by the noise.

Needed meaning: very frightened.

The victim was traumatized by the accident.

Needed meaning: psychologically shocked.

### Memory Trick

Think of the accident timeline:

Accident

Trauma / shock

Could not speak

Only “traumatized” naturally connects the accident to the psychological shock that caused the speech loss.

So:

Accident → Trauma → Speech loss

Only traumatized fits that chain.

### One More Exam Tip

In sentence improvement, first decide:

### Is this a grammar question?

Example:

He can sings.

Then apply grammar rules.

### Or is this a meaning/context question?

Example:

stabilized by the accident

Then ignore grammar and ask:

“What word best fits the situation?”

This question is 90% vocabulary/context, 10% grammar.
