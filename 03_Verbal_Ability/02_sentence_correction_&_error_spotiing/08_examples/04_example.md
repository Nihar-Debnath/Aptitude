Excellent! This is **not a vocabulary question**. This is a **grammar question**, specifically on **Conditionals**.

You'll notice that sentence improvement questions are often just **grammar topics in disguise**.

Let's solve it step by step.

---

# Step 1: Read the sentence

> **If the team were knowing about the alligators, they would not have decided to go on a picnic there.**

Don't look at the options yet.

Ask yourself:

**Is this talking about the present or the past?**

The clue is:

> **would not have decided**

This means:

They **already decided** in the past.

So we're talking about a **past unreal situation**.

---

# Step 2: Identify the Conditional Type

Remember the conditionals we studied.

### Type 3 Conditional

Used for:

> Imagining a different past.

Structure:

```text
If + Past Perfect
      ↓
would have + V3
```

Example:

> If I **had studied**, I **would have passed**.

Reality:

I didn't study.

I didn't pass.

---

Now compare with our sentence.

Sentence:

> If the team ______ about the alligators,
> they **would not have decided**...

The second part already has:

> would have + V3

So the first part **must** be:

> had known

---

# Step 3: Check the options

---

## A. knew

Sentence:

> If the team knew...

This is **Type 2**.

Type 2 goes with:

> would + V1

Example:

> If I knew him, I would help him.

But our sentence has:

> would have decided

Mismatch.

❌ Wrong.

---

## B. did know

Sentence:

> If the team did know...

"Did know" is used for **emphasis**, not for this conditional.

Example:

> I **did** call you!

It doesn't fit here.

❌ Wrong.

---

## C. had known ✅

Sentence:

> If the team **had known** about the alligators,
> they **would not have decided**...

Perfect.

This follows:

```text
If + had + V3
        ↓
would have + V3
```

✅ Correct.

---

## D. No substitution required

Original:

> were knowing

Two problems:

### Problem 1

**Know** is a **stative verb**.

Stative verbs usually do **not** take continuous forms.

We say:

✅ I know him.

❌ I am knowing him.

Similarly:

❌ were knowing

---

### Problem 2

Wrong conditional tense.

So the original sentence is incorrect.

❌ Reject.

---

# Final Answer

✅ **C. had known**

---

# Why "were knowing" is wrong?

This is another grammar rule.

Some verbs describe a **state**, not an action.

These are called **Stative Verbs**.

Examples:

* know
* believe
* understand
* love
* hate
* own
* belong
* remember

These usually **don't use -ing**.

Wrong:

❌ I am knowing him.

Correct:

✅ I know him.

Wrong:

❌ They were knowing.

Correct:

✅ They knew.

or

✅ They had known.

---

# The Hidden Pattern

Whenever you see:

> **would have + V3**

Immediately think:

> **Type 3 Conditional**

Then look back.

The "if" clause should almost always be:

> **had + V3**

---

## Quick Memory Table

| If Clause        | Main Clause         | Type       |
| ---------------- | ------------------- | ---------- |
| If I study       | I will pass         | Type 1     |
| If I studied     | I would pass        | Type 2     |
| If I had studied | I would have passed | **Type 3** |

## Exam Shortcut ⚡

When solving sentence improvement:

### Step 1

Look for clues like:

* would
* would have
* will
* had
* if

### Step 2

Match the conditional pattern.

Here:

> **would have decided**

⬇

Your brain should immediately say:

> **Type 3 → had known**

That's how you solve it in under 10 seconds.
