# ✅ Rule 3: **I and You**

Even though **I** and **You** can refer to one person, they **do not** follow the normal singular rule.

Normally:

* He → **plays** ✅
* She → **writes** ✅

But:

* I → **play** ❌ ~~plays~~
* You → **play** ❌ ~~plays~~

### Examples ✅

* I **play** football.

* I **work** at TCS.

* I **have** a laptop.

* You **play** football.

* You **work** hard.

* You **have** talent.

### Wrong ❌

* I **plays** football.
* You **works** here.
* I **has** a car.

---

## Why?

Because **I** and **You** always take the **base verb** (like plural subjects), even when referring to one person.

Think of them as **special subjects**.

---

## Memory Trick

Only remember:

| Subject  | Verb     |
| -------- | -------- |
| He       | plays    |
| She      | writes   |
| It       | runs     |
| **I**    | **play** |
| **You**  | **play** |
| **We**   | **play** |
| **They** | **play** |

Notice that **I** and **You** behave like **We** and **They**, not like **He/She/It**.

# ✅ Rule 4: **Be Verb Agreement**

The verb **"be"** is irregular, so you must memorize it.

| Subject | Be Verb |
| ------- | ------- |
| I       | **am**  |
| He      | **is**  |
| She     | **is**  |
| It      | **is**  |
| You     | **are** |
| We      | **are** |
| They    | **are** |

---

### Examples ✅

* I **am** tired.
* He **is** a doctor.
* She **is** my friend.
* It **is** raining.
* You **are** late.
* We **are** students.
* They **are** engineers.

### Wrong ❌

* I **is** happy.
* He **are** a doctor.
* They **is** playing.
* You **am** my friend.

---

## Memory Trick

Think of it like this:

```
I        → am
He/She/It → is
You/We/They → are
```

Just memorize this pattern—it appears in almost every English exam.
