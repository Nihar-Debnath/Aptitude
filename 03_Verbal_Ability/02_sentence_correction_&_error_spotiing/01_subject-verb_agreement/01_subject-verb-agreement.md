Excellent choice. **Subject-Verb Agreement (SVA)** is the **#1 grammar topic** for placement exams (TCS, Cognizant, Capgemini, Accenture, Infosys, Wipro).

The golden rule is simple:

# Subject-Verb Agreement

## Rule 1: Singular Subject → Singular Verb

> **One person/thing = Verb ends with -s/-es (Present Tense)**

✅ Examples

* **He plays** football.
* **She writes** well.
* **The dog barks** loudly.
* **Nihar studies** every day.

❌ Wrong

* He play football.
* She write well.

---

## Rule 2: Plural Subject → Plural Verb

> **More than one = Base form of the verb (no -s)**

✅ Examples

* **They play** football.
* **We study** English.
* **The dogs bark** loudly.
* **Students work** hard.

❌ Wrong

* They plays football.
* Students works hard.

---

## Rule 3: I and You

Even though **I** and **You** refer to one person, they take a **plural verb** in the present tense.

✅ Examples

* I **am** happy.
* I **play** cricket.
* You **are** my friend.
* You **work** here.

❌ Wrong

* I plays.
* You works.

---

# Rule 4: "Be" Verb Agreement

| Subject     | Verb |
| ----------- | ---- |
| I           | am   |
| He/She/It   | is   |
| You/We/They | are  |

Examples

* I **am** tired.
* He **is** a doctor.
* They **are** students.

---

# Rule 5: Two Subjects Joined by **and**

Usually → **Plural Verb**

✅ Examples

* Tom and Jerry **are** friends.
* Reading and writing **are** important.
* My father and mother **live** in Kolkata.

❌ Wrong

* Tom and Jerry is friends.

---

# Rule 6: "Each", "Every", "Everyone", "Everybody", "Someone", "Anyone", "Nobody"

These are **always singular**.

✅ Examples

* Everyone **is** ready.
* Each student **has** a book.
* Nobody **knows** the answer.
* Someone **has** called you.

❌ Wrong

* Everyone are ready.
* Each students have books.

---

# Rule 7: Neither...Nor / Either...Or

The verb agrees with the **subject closest to it**.

✅ Examples

* Either the teacher or the students **are** coming.
* Either the students or the teacher **is** coming.
* Neither Rahul nor his friends **have** arrived.
* Neither the boys nor Rohan **has** finished.

---

# Rule 8: Collective Nouns

Words like:

* Team
* Family
* Committee
* Jury
* Class

Usually take a **singular verb** when acting as one unit.

✅ Examples

* The team **is** winning.
* The family **has** arrived.
* The committee **has** made a decision.

---

# Rule 9: "One of"

**One of + plural noun → singular verb**

✅ Examples

* One of the students **is** absent.
* One of my friends **works** at TCS.

❌ Wrong

* One of the students are absent.

---

# Rule 10: "The number of" vs "A number of"

### The number of → Singular

✅ The number of students **is** increasing.

### A number of → Plural

✅ A number of students **are** absent.

---

# Rule 11: Uncountable Nouns

Words like:

* Information
* Advice
* Furniture
* News
* Equipment
* Luggage

These take **singular verbs**.

✅ Examples

* The news **is** shocking.
* The information **is** correct.
* The furniture **looks** beautiful.

---

# Rule 12: Amounts of Time, Money, Distance

When considered as **one unit**, use a singular verb.

✅ Examples

* Five kilometers **is** a long walk.
* Ten thousand rupees **is** enough.
* Two hours **is** sufficient.

---

# Common Placement Mistakes

| ❌ Wrong                                        | ✅ Correct                                       |
| ---------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- |
| He go to school.                               | He goes to school.                              |
| They goes home.                                | They go home.                                   |
| Everyone are happy.                            | Everyone is happy.                              |
| Each students have books.                      | Each student has a book.                        |
| One of the boys are absent.                    | One of the boys is absent.                      |
| The news are good.                             | The news is good.                               |
| Neither the teacher nor the students is ready. | Neither the teacher nor the students are ready. |

---

# Quick Trick 🧠

Ask yourself:

> **Who is doing the action?**

That is the **subject**. Then decide:

* **One person/thing → Singular verb**
* **More than one → Plural verb**
