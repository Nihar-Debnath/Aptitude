Excellent! This is a **preposition error**—another favorite in Error Spotting.

Let's solve it step by step.

---

# Question

> **The father brought the fruits (A) / and distributed them (B) / between his five children. (C) / No error (D)**

---

# Step 1: Read the sentence

Ignore the divisions first.

> The father brought the fruits and distributed them between his five children.

Now ask:

**How many children?**

👉 **Five**

---

# Step 2: Focus on the preposition

The important word is:

> **between**

Many students memorize:

> Between = 2

> Among = More than 2

This works for most competitive exams.

Since there are **five children**, we should use:

> **among**

---

# Correct sentence

> **The father brought the fruits and distributed them among his five children.**

---

# Answer

The error is in **Part (C)**.

✅ **Option C**

---

# The Rule

## Between

Used for **two** people or things.

Examples

✅ Between Ram and Shyam

✅ Between India and Nepal

✅ The ball is between the two chairs.

---

## Among

Used for **more than two** people or things.

Examples

✅ Among five students

✅ Among the players

✅ Among the villagers

---

# Memory Trick

```text
2 → Between

3 or more → Among
```

---

# But Wait! (Advanced English)

Modern English has a more advanced rule.

Many grammar books say:

> **Between** is used when talking about **individual, separate relationships**, even if there are more than two.

Example:

> The treaty was signed **between India, Japan, Australia, and the USA.**

This is correct because the treaty is between specific countries.

Similarly,

> Divide the money **between** the five brothers.

This is accepted in modern English because the money is divided into individual shares.

---

# So why do exams say "among"?

Competitive exams (SSC, Banking, CDS, State PSC, etc.) usually follow the **traditional rule** because it's easier to test:

| Number of People | Expected Exam Answer |
| ---------------- | -------------------- |
| 2                | Between              |
| 3 or more        | Among                |

So for exam purposes:

> **between his five children** ❌

> **among his five children** ✅

---

# Exam Cheat Sheet

| Use     | Example               |
| ------- | --------------------- |
| Between | between Ram and Shyam |
| Between | between two houses    |
| Among   | among the students    |
| Among   | among five friends    |
| Among   | among the crowd       |

## A Quick Quiz

Tell me which preposition is correct.

1. She divided the chocolates **_____** her two sisters.
2. He was sitting **_____** the crowd.
3. The agreement was signed **_____** India and China.
4. The prize was distributed **_____** ten winners.

Try answering with just:

> **1. Between / Among**
> **2. Between / Among**
> **3. Between / Among**
> **4. Between / Among**

This will help make the rule stick.
