### Error: **"discovery bone ornaments"**

We need **of** after "discovery":

> **with the discovery of bone ornaments** ✅

Because the correct structure is:

> **discovery of + thing**

Examples:

* discovery **of** gold
* discovery **of** a new planet
* discovery **of** ancient bones
* discovery **of** bone ornaments

### Correct sentence:

> **Ancient jewellery or decoration has a new meaning with the discovery of bone ornaments.**

Therefore:

**Answer: C. (R)** ✅

And notice: **"the discovery" is actually correct here.**
The error is **not "the"**; it's the missing **"of"**.
