# ✅ Rule 5: Two Subjects Joined by **"and"**

### Rule

When **two different subjects** are joined by **and**, use a **plural verb**.

### Formula

```text
Subject + and + Subject → Plural Verb
```

---

## Examples ✅

* **Rahul and Mohan are** friends.
* **My father and mother live** in Kolkata.
* **Tea and coffee are** available.
* **Reading and writing are** important skills.

---

## Wrong ❌

* Rahul and Mohan **is** friends.
* My father and mother **lives** in Kolkata.

---

# Exception 1: One Person with Two Titles

If two nouns refer to **the same person**, use a **singular verb**.

### Examples ✅

* **The CEO and founder is** giving a speech.
  *(One person = CEO + Founder)*

* **My friend and mentor has** helped me a lot.
  *(One person = Friend + Mentor)*

### But...

* **The CEO and the founder are** giving speeches.
  *(Two different people)*

---

# Exception 2: One Thing / One Idea

If **and** joins two words that together mean **one thing**, use a **singular verb**.

### Examples ✅

* **Bread and butter is** my favorite breakfast.
* **Fish and chips is** a popular dish.
* **Rice and curry is** today's lunch.

These are treated as **one meal**, not two separate items.

# Memory Trick 🧠

### Ask yourself:

**Are there really two different people/things?**

✔️ Yes → **Plural verb**

❌ No (one person, one dish, one idea) → **Singular verb**
