# ✅ Rule 6: **Each, Every, Everyone, Everybody, Someone, Somebody, Anyone, Anybody, No one, Nobody, Everything, Nothing**

### Rule

These words are called **Indefinite Pronouns**.

Although they may refer to **many people**, they are **grammatically singular**.

👉 Therefore, they always take a **singular verb**.

---

## Words You Must Memorize

```text
Each
Every
Everyone
Everybody
Someone
Somebody
Anyone
Anybody
No one
Nobody
Everything
Nothing
Something
Anything
```

All of these are **singular**.

# Why Are They Singular?

Take this sentence:

> **Everyone is happy.**

You might think:

> "Everyone" means many people.

Yes, **in meaning** it refers to many people.

But **in grammar**, **everyone** is treated as **one group**.

That's why we say:

✅ Everyone **is**

❌ Everyone **are**

---

# Common Placement Mistakes 🚨

❌ Everyone **are** ready.

✅ Everyone **is** ready.

---

❌ Each students **have** books.

✅ Each **student has** a book.

Notice two corrections:

* **students → student**
* **have → has**

---

❌ Nobody **know** the answer.

✅ Nobody **knows** the answer.

---

# Memory Trick 🧠

Remember this phrase:

> **"Every ONE is ONE."**

Notice the word:

**EveryONE**

It ends with **ONE**.

So think:

```text
ONE
↓

Singular
↓

is / has / plays
```

The same idea works for:

* Someone
* Anyone
* No one

They all end in **one**.

# Difference

### Every Student

Means:

> Each student individually.

Example:

* **Every student has** an ID card.

---

### All Students

Means:

> The whole group.

Example:

* **All students have** ID cards.

Notice:

* **Every → has**
* **All → have**

## ⭐ Placement Tip

Whenever you see **everyone, somebody, anyone, nobody, each, every**, **stop thinking about the meaning**.

Think only about the grammar:

> **These words are always singular.**

Then automatically choose:

* **is** ✔️
* **has** ✔️
* **plays** ✔️
* **works** ✔️

This single rule appears very frequently in aptitude and communication assessments.
