This is one of the **most frequently tested grammar structures** in Error Spotting and Sentence Correction.

Let's understand it from the **concept**, not by memorizing.

---

# What does "No sooner...than" mean?

It means:

> **As soon as**

or

> **Immediately after**

The second event happened **immediately** after the first.

Timeline:

```text
Event 1          Event 2
|----------------|
Step out     →   It starts raining
```

There is almost **no gap** between the two events.

Hence,

> **No sooner...than**

---

## Normal Sentence

> I stepped out.
> Immediately it started raining.

Combine them:

> **No sooner had I stepped out than it started raining.**

Meaning:

> The moment I stepped outside, it started raining.

---

# Structure

The grammar pattern is:

```text
No sooner + had + Subject + V3 + than + Subject + V2
```

Example

> No sooner **had** I **finished** my homework **than** my friend arrived.

Break it down:

* had → Helping verb
* finished → Past participle (V3)
* than → Connector
* arrived → Simple Past (V2)

---

# Why "had"?

Because the first action happened **before** the second.

Past Perfect shows

> This happened first.

Simple Past shows

> This happened next.

Timeline

```text
First action              Second action

Had finished    →      arrived
```

---

# Another Example

Normal

> The train left.
> Immediately I reached the station.

Using the rule

> No sooner **had the train left than** I reached the station.

Meaning

> I reached almost immediately after the train left.

---

# Why is there inversion?

Look carefully.

Normal sentence

```text
I had stepped out.
```

After "No sooner"

```text
Had I stepped out...
```

The helping verb comes **before** the subject.

This is called **Inversion**.

It is similar to questions.

Compare

Question

> Had you eaten?

Grammar pattern

> Had + Subject

Exactly the same.

---

# Rule

Whenever a sentence starts with

* No sooner
* Never
* Rarely
* Hardly
* Barely
* Scarcely
* Seldom

English often uses **inversion**.

---

# Examples

### Normal

I had reached home.

After "No sooner"

> No sooner **had I reached** home...

---

### Normal

She had entered the room.

After "No sooner"

> No sooner **had she entered** the room...

# Common Exam Mistakes

## Mistake 1

❌ No sooner did I arrive than...

Wrong

Use

✅ had

---

## Mistake 2

❌ No sooner had I arrived when...

Wrong connector

Correct

✅ than

Remember

```text
No sooner → than
```

Never

```text
No sooner → when
```

---

## Mistake 3

❌ No sooner had I arrive

Wrong

After "had"

Use V3

```text
Had + arrived
Had + eaten
Had + gone
```

---

# Related Structures

These are asked together in exams.

## Hardly...when

Structure

```text
Hardly + had + Subject + V3 + when + Subject + V2
```

Example

> Hardly had I entered the room **when** the lights went off.

---

## Scarcely...when

Example

> Scarcely had she sat down **when** the phone rang.

---

Notice

```text
No sooner → than
Hardly → when
Scarcely → when
Barely → when
```

This is a favorite exam trick.

---

# Comparison

### As soon as

> As soon as I reached home, it started raining.

Normal English.

---

### No sooner...than

> No sooner had I reached home than it started raining.

More formal.

Same meaning.

# Error Spotting Tricks

Examiners intentionally make these mistakes.

### Wrong

> No sooner had I reached home **when** it started raining.

Error

**when**

Correct

**than**

---

### Wrong

> No sooner did I reach home than...

Error

**did**

Correct

**had**

---

### Wrong

> No sooner had I reach home than...

Error

**reach**

Correct

**reached**

(V3 after "had")

---

### Wrong

> No sooner I had reached home than...

Error

No inversion.

Correct

> No sooner **had I reached** home than...
