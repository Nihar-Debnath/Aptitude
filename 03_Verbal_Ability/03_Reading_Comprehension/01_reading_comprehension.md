**Reading comprehension** is the ability to **read a text, understand its meaning, and explain or answer questions about it**.

It involves several skills, such as:

* Understanding the main idea of the passage.
* Finding important details.
* Understanding the meaning of new words from the context.
* Making inferences (figuring out information that is not directly stated).
* Summarizing the text.

**Example:**

**Passage:**
*"Ali went to the market because he needed to buy some fruits. He bought apples and oranges."*

**Question:** Why did Ali go to the market?

**Answer:** He went to the market to buy some fruits.

Reading comprehension helps students improve their reading, thinking, and learning skills.
