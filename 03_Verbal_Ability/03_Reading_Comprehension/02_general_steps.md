These are a common strategy for solving **Reading Comprehension (RC)** questions quickly, especially in aptitude tests and placement exams like **TCS, Cognizant, Capgemini, Accenture**, etc.

Let's break them down.

---

# Step 1: Skim the Passage

**Skimming** means **reading quickly to understand the overall idea**, not every single word.

You're trying to answer questions like:

* What is this passage about?
* What is the author's main point?
* Is it talking about science, history, business, or technology?

### Example

**Passage**

> Artificial Intelligence is transforming healthcare. Doctors now use AI to detect diseases earlier and improve treatment plans. However, experts believe AI should assist doctors rather than replace them.

When skimming, you don't stop at every sentence.

You simply think:

> "Okay, this passage is about AI in healthcare and how it helps doctors."

That's enough.

⏱ Time: around **20–40 seconds**.

---

# Step 2: Read the Questions

Now, **don't read the passage again yet**.

Read all the questions first.

Example:

1. What is the main idea?
2. Why do experts support AI?
3. What does "assist" mean?

Now your brain knows **what information to look for**.

Instead of reading everything carefully, you'll search for the answers.

---

# Step 3: Go to the Detailed Reading on the Critical Areas

Now return to the passage.

This time, read **only the parts related to the questions**.

These important sections are called **critical areas**.

### Example

Question:

> Why do experts believe AI should not replace doctors?

Go back to the sentence:

> "Experts believe AI should assist doctors rather than replace them."

Read that sentence carefully.

You don't need to reread the entire passage.

---

## Another Example

### Passage

> The Amazon rainforest produces a significant amount of oxygen. It is home to millions of plant and animal species. Deforestation has increased over the past decade, threatening biodiversity.

### Questions

1. Why is deforestation a concern?
2. What is the Amazon rainforest known for?

Now you know the critical areas.

For **Question 1**, focus on:

> "Deforestation has increased... threatening biodiversity."

For **Question 2**, focus on:

> "Produces a significant amount of oxygen."

No need to reread every line.

---

# Simple Memory Trick

| Step                    | What You Do                         | Goal                      |
| ----------------------- | ----------------------------------- | ------------------------- |
| **1. Skim**             | Read quickly                        | Understand the main topic |
| **2. Questions**        | Read all questions                  | Know what to search for   |
| **3. Detailed Reading** | Read only important parts carefully | Find accurate answers     |

## For TCS, Cognizant, and other placement tests

This strategy is useful because:

* It saves time.
* You avoid reading the whole passage multiple times.
* You focus only on information needed to answer the questions.

As you're preparing for communication and aptitude assessments, practicing this method daily with 2–3 passages will help improve both your speed and accuracy.
