This slide is giving you a **framework** for solving Sentence Completion questions. Instead of guessing the answer, you follow these clues one by one.

Let's go through every point in detail.

---

# 1. Logic is Important ⭐ (Most Important Rule)

Every sentence expresses **one logical idea**.

Your job is to understand:

* What is happening?
* What should happen next?
* What word makes the sentence meaningful?

### Example

> The road was flooded, so the buses were ______.

Options:

* cancelled ✅
* dancing
* flying
* beautiful

The logic is:

Flooded road → buses cannot run.

So **cancelled** is the only logical answer.

---

# 2. Predict the Answer First

Before looking at the options,

**Hide the options mentally.**

Read the sentence and think:

> "If I had to write this myself, what word would I use?"

### Example

> He worked very hard and finally became ______.

Without seeing options you might think:

* successful
* rich
* famous

Now check the options.

If one matches your prediction, it is usually correct.

This prevents confusing options from misleading you.

---

# 3. Support Signals (Ideas Move in the Same Direction)

These words tell you that the second idea **supports or adds to** the first idea.

Common support signals:

* and
* also
* similarly
* moreover
* furthermore
* in addition
* besides
* as well as
* not only...but also

### Example

> Rahul is intelligent and ______.

Options:

* hardworking ✅
* lazy
* careless
* dishonest

Since **and** joins similar ideas,

Positive → Positive

So **hardworking** fits.

---

### Another Example

> She is kind; moreover, she is very ______.

Again,

"Moreover" means "also."

So the blank should contain another positive quality.

---

# 4. Opposite Signals (Ideas Change Direction)

These words tell you that the next part will **contrast** with the first.

Common opposite signals:

* but
* however
* although
* though
* even though
* despite
* in spite of
* yet
* whereas
* rather
* unless

### Example

> Although he was tired, he continued ______.

Options:

* sleeping
* working ✅
* resting
* relaxing

Although = contrast

Expected:

Tired → Normally stop

But...

He continued → working

---

### Example

> She is poor, but she is very ______.

Options:

* generous ✅
* greedy
* lazy
* weak

Poor usually means less money.

"But" tells us something surprising.

So generous fits.

---

# 5. Root Words

Sometimes you don't know the exact meaning of a word.

But you know its **root**.

That helps.

Example roots:

| Root   | Meaning  | Example       |
| ------ | -------- | ------------- |
| bio    | life     | biology       |
| geo    | earth    | geography     |
| aqua   | water    | aquarium      |
| chrono | time     | chronology    |
| tele   | distance | telephone     |
| auto   | self     | autobiography |

---

### Example

Suppose the option is

**Aquatic**

You don't know the word.

But

"Aqua" = water

Aquatic = related to water.

You can often eliminate wrong options using roots.

---

# 6. Sentence Structure (Cause & Effect)

Some words show

Cause → Effect

Look for these words:

Cause:

* because
* since
* for
* as

Effect:

* therefore
* thus
* hence
* consequently
* so
* subsequently

---

### Example

> It rained heavily; therefore, the match was ______.

Rain

↓

Match

↓

Cancelled

Easy.

---

### Example

> Since he practiced daily, he became a ______ player.

Practice

↓

Improvement

↓

Skilled player

---

Notice how the relationship is

Cause → Result

---

# 7. Sentence Structure (Parts of Speech)

Sometimes grammar alone tells you the answer.

Identify what type of word is missing.

Possible parts of speech:

| Part of Speech | Example             |
| -------------- | ------------------- |
| Noun           | boy, car, happiness |
| Pronoun        | he, she, they       |
| Verb           | run, eat, write     |
| Adjective      | beautiful, honest   |
| Adverb         | quickly, slowly     |
| Preposition    | in, on, at          |
| Conjunction    | and, but, because   |

---

### Example

> She is a very ______ girl.

After

"a very"

comes

Adjective

Possible answer:

* honest ✅

Not

* honesty ❌ (noun)

---

### Example

> He runs very ______.

After a verb,

you need an adverb.

Correct:

quickly ✅

Not

quick ❌

# The Complete Solving Strategy

Whenever you solve a Sentence Completion question, follow this sequence:

1. **Read the entire sentence** without looking at the options.
2. **Understand the logic** of what the sentence is trying to say.
3. **Predict the missing word** yourself.
4. **Identify signal words**:

   * **Support signals** (and, also, moreover) → same direction.
   * **Opposite signals** (but, however, although) → opposite direction.
   * **Cause & effect signals** (because, therefore, since, so) → reason or result.
5. **Check the grammar** by identifying the required part of speech (noun, verb, adjective, adverb, etc.).
6. **Use vocabulary or root words** if you don't know an option completely.
7. **Eliminate incorrect options** and choose the one that is **logically, grammatically, and contextually correct**.

## Shortcut for TCS Ignite

For most TCS Ignite sentence completion questions, these four checks solve the majority of problems:

**Logic → Signal Words → Parts of Speech → Elimination**

If you master these, you'll be able to solve most questions quickly without relying on advanced vocabulary.
