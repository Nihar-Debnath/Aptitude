### **Answer: ✅ B. Ivory towers**

### Meaning of the sentence

> **It is a common criticism that most of the academicians live in their ivory towers, so they are not aware of the real-life challenges.**

**Simple meaning:**

Many people criticize that **professors, researchers, or scholars stay only in the world of books and theories**, so they **don't understand the practical problems that ordinary people face**.

### Meaning of the options

* **A. Homes** ❌ Just a place to live. Doesn't explain being disconnected.
* **B. Ivory towers** ✅ **Idiom:** A place or state where people are **isolated from the real world** and focus only on theories or academic work.
* **C. Glass palaces** ❌ Not a standard English idiom.
* **D. Big flats** ❌ Just large apartments; doesn't fit the meaning.

### Trick to solve

Look at the clue:

> **"...so they are not aware of the real-life challenges."**

Ask yourself:

**Who is not aware of real-life problems?**

➡️ Someone who is **isolated from reality**.

That is exactly what **"ivory tower"** means.

**Answer: ✅ B. Ivory towers**
