The correct answer is **C. Commensurate** ✅

> **They should face legal sanctions commensurate with their actions.**

### What does "commensurate with" mean?

**Commensurate with = proportional to / appropriate to / corresponding to**

So the sentence means:

> They should face legal punishment **proportionate to what they did**.

For example:

* A minor offense → minor punishment
* A serious offense → serious punishment

So the punishment should be **commensurate with the action**.

### ⭐ Important vocabulary pattern

This is also testing a **collocation / fixed word combination**:

> **commensurate with + noun**

Examples:

* Salary should be **commensurate with experience**.
* Punishment should be **commensurate with the crime**.
* Responsibility should be **commensurate with authority**.

So whenever you see **"commensurate with"**, remember:

> **COMMENSURATE WITH = PROPORTIONAL TO**

This type of question is best solved by **knowing word meanings + common word combinations (collocations)**, not just grammar.
